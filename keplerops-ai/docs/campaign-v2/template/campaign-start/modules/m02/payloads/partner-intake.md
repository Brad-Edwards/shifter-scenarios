# Orion Partner Intake

KeplerOps accepts release questions, compatibility reports, model candidates,
and evaluation helpers from registered partners. Open a support request at the
Partner Intake desk and attach the exact document or artifact under review.

Documents are extracted, indexed, cited by Orion, and routed according to their
declared release question. Accepted requests receive a scoped Orion Review Room
invitation by email. Package and model submissions proceed through separate
inspection and disposable-import stages; an allowed scan is not an adoption
decision.

Reviewers publish stage status for extraction, indexing, citation, routing,
download, inspection, import, and compatibility testing. Rejected submissions
retain a specific reason so a partner can correct them.
