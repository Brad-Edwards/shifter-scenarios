# Event Critical Route

## Purpose

All 134 operations remain scored, playable, and required for full catalog
coverage. They are not one serial queue. This document identifies the bounded
strategic route a team can complete during a two-day event while specialist
lanes proceed in parallel.

The route is a pacing contract, not a reduced scenario profile. Every OSS
service, enterprise dependency, optional operation, and ATLAS behavior remains
materialized in the same range.

## Route Budget

- Target: 35-40 scored operations.
- Current design: 39 operations.
- Team shape: two to four participants may split parallel lanes.
- Difficulty mix: 8 Accessible, 17 Intermediate, 10 Advanced and 4 Expert,
  worth 9,700 points.
- Calibration envelope: 13.92-28.5 participant-hours before team parallelism;
  infrastructure queue time is a defect/operations measure, not difficulty.
- Primary outcome: initial foothold, internal AI discovery, agent compromise,
  participant-created model manipulation, real distillation, original model
  acquisition, compromised release, production effect, exact exfiltration, and
  offline proof of both stolen and reproduced capability.
- No operation may be placed on this route solely to satisfy ATLAS coverage.

## Route Acceptance Gates

- Every prerequisite of the 39 operations is another route operation or an
  explicit start-state capability.
- A parallel operation may improve or shorten a route step but cannot be its
  hidden prerequisite.
- Each act exposes an Accessible or Intermediate continuation while Advanced
  and Expert work runs in parallel.
- The exact bytes and identities created on the route remain unchanged through
  the final causal join.
- A four-person fresh team can complete the route during the event without
  management-plane intervention or a full reset.
- Full-catalog playtest independently proves every operation outside the route.
