# KeplerOps ATLAS Coverage Plan

Operator/validator planning document. Do not distribute this technique mapping,
hidden evidence ids, or planned flag outcomes to participants.

## Decision Summary

KeplerOps opens as a selectable AI attack board. A participant arrives at the
AI console with 25 independent roots across the starting modules; no account
discovery, credential hunt, enterprise reconnaissance, or lateral movement must
be completed first. The board has several genuine AI-security receipts curated
to finish inside a 15-minute window. There is no enforcement cap or pack
runtime timer. The authoritative inventory is in the modular SDL; the
board-depth rationale and route hypotheses are rendered in
[`challenge-portfolio.md`](challenge-portfolio.md).

This document is a coverage **plan** and status ledger, not a golden claim. The
pack is `draft`, but the participant proof campaigns have now recorded
participant-equivalent proof for all 134 playable challenge contracts through
the retained GCP range and its Kasm participant surface. The accepted proof set
covers the original kernels, the Module 01 expansion and Module 02 expansion supply-chain additions, and the
Module 03 expansion through Module 10 expansion full-ATLAS expansion rows that are present in the runtime
board. ATLAS expansion design assigned the 139 rows originally missing from the proven core
to 75 participant-sized challenge contracts or nine justified existing-receipt
extensions. Seventy-four of those 75 challenge-design rows are now
participant-proven; `kep-m02-g` is the one remaining planned contract and still
depends on the physical accelerator attestation decision.

The final participant-equivalent walkthrough composed real vector retrieval,
model and classifier attacks, durable memory and restart, bounded adversarial
search, Airflow training, MLflow/MinIO lineage, live-teacher proxy extraction,
signed approval confusion, production alias reload, brokered impact, and the
complete 3,422,777,952-byte contained artifact transfer. The retained range was
reset afterward and reported ready, isolated health. The remaining work before
golden is playtest calibration, release hardening, evidence/documentation
reconciliation, and the separate hardware-attestation decision.
The reviewed distribution and complete inventory are in
[`atlas-challenge-architecture.md`](atlas-challenge-architecture.md).
Post-manual static checks, reset proof, evidence reconciliation, and teardown
now pass but remain lifecycle evidence outside this coverage-plan result. The
generation-17 integrated functional rehearsal passes after Module 01
qualification, and its canonical reset into generation 18 passes. The current
range is intentionally retained. The remaining reliability work is likewise
outside an ATLAS coverage count.

## ACES Semantic Boundary

The canonical ACES document is
[`../sdl/keplerops-ai.sdl.yaml`](../sdl/keplerops-ai.sdl.yaml), parsed and
semantically validated with the exact PyPI pin in
[`../validation/requirements.txt`](../validation/requirements.txt): `raes==2.0.0`.
Each module is an ACES `behavior_specification` and uses the governed
`ai_offensive_behavior_refs` surface.

That RAES 2.0.0 surface governs **ATLAS tactics**, not techniques. Its source
model is `ParticipantBehaviorSpecification.ai_offensive_behavior_refs`; its
controlled vocabulary is `participant-ai-offensive-behavior-activities`, which
adopts the 16 ATLAS 2026.06 tactics. RAES 2.0.0 does not define a governed
ATLAS technique-level field.

In the RAES repository, the defining sources are:

- `implementations/python/packages/raes/participant_behavior_specification.py`
  defines the `ai_offensive_behavior_refs` field.
- `contracts/concept-authority/controlled-vocabularies-v1.json` binds that
  field's governed scope to the 16 ATLAS tactic terms.
- `contracts/concept-authority/atlas-tactics-source-v1.json` pins the adopted
  MITRE ATLAS 2026.06 tactic source and digest.

Therefore:

1. ACES is authoritative for each module's ATLAS tactic semantics.
2. `content.core.atlas-technique-catalog` in the environment SDL is the
   explicit KeplerOps catalog for 173 technique-level challenge variants.
3. [`../validation/validate_aces_sdl.py`](../validation/validate_aces_sdl.py) verifies the
   installed ACES version, ACES parsing and semantics, and the tactic joins
   between the native behavior fields, governed extensions, and embedded
   catalog.
4. [`../validation/validate_oracle.py`](../validation/validate_oracle.py) verifies the exact
   ATLAS catalog, source relationships, module assignments, outcomes, evidence,
   and projection integrity digests.

This distinction prevents the pack from presenting a bespoke pack-local ledger
as though ACES itself defined that semantic surface.

## How To Review The Technique Plan

For each row, review five things together:

1. **ATLAS ID and name** identify the exact pinned 2026.06 technique.
2. **Tactic(s)** show its source ATLAS relationships, not a locally inferred
   bucket.
3. **Evidence** names the oracle evidence boundary the implementation must
   eventually satisfy.
4. **Planned in-world variant** states what the participant would actually do;
   it must not collapse to merely reading a fact or triggering a pre-seeded
   verdict.
5. **Module** determines when and on which AI surface the variant is offered.
