proof-store, model-host, or oracle-file access to satisfy a challenge. The
proof established that:

- the live range-local vLLM model disclosed the exact synthetic canary and

  participant request;

  independently issued and verified afterward.

The one-pass proof also rejected the retired generic inference shortcut,
participant-supplied canary and instruction values, a sample from the wrong

dataset-store and inference-gateway dependency closure that owns the query,
attempt, baseline, and runtime state. The model host and unrelated services
were not restarted between samples. One final scoped cleanup and a full
15-service health check returned the retained range to clean, ready generation
21 after the campaign.

canonical reset generation 49. Only the dataset, proof, policy, and gateway VMs
were recycled. Their exact SDL-rendered startup scripts and immutable image
digests matched the reviewed source. Each service was reset at the existing
generation; the dataset verifier passed after the gateway rebuilt the trusted
embedding baseline. The range health report then recorded 15 of 15 services
ready, 15 assets, nine subnets, and isolation ready.

No Terraform plan was applied. The retained reliability range uses the
existing project and tenant; no project, account, billing, or IAM operation was
performed. The earlier Phase-E teardown verified the prior range absent while
retaining the existing project. The current range remains intentionally
retained for later module qualification.
