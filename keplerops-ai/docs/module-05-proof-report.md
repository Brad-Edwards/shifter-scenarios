# Module 05 Participant-Proof Report

## Result

All five module-05 agent-persistence challenges are `participant-proven` on
the `gcp_full` profile. On 2026-07-18 a generation-21 reliability campaign
entered through the external Kasm browser terminal and passed every challenge
at 30/30 participant-path trials, above the required 27/30 gate. Every result
has a 0.886483 lower bound on its 95% Wilson interval.

For A through C, each trial began with a fresh live-model-authored
`remember_preference` call and then exercised the item-specific later session
and thread boundary. After the final plant, the participant requested one real
supervised worker restart; D and E then passed 30 post-restart live-model
activations each. Every E trial crossed real OPA policy and recorded the
contained `publish_notice` broker effect.

The reliability campaign did not request flags or receipts. Its owner-only
report retains only aggregate counts and intervals, with no prompts,
completions, durable memory, marker values, tool payloads, proof bodies, or
model data. Item-specific receipts had already passed both the automated
rehearsal and the final manual walkthrough.

The final scenario-wide walkthrough revalidated all five participant paths in
reset generation 55 on 2026-07-16. One model-authored memory crossed session
and thread boundaries, survived a participant-requested worker process restart,
and drove a real post-restart brokered effect. All five current-generation
receipts issued and independently verified through the participant proof
route.

This is durable evidence for Module 05 implementation. It is not a scenario-wide reliability
result or a golden claim. The pack remains `draft` while Modules 08 through 10
retain open reliability work.

## Participant-Surface Path

The successful runs used the synthetic in-world participant identity and the
same Kasm desktop, inference gateway, range-local SmolLM2/vLLM model, policy,
tool broker, and receipt routes available to a participant. They did not use
SSH, Terraform, cloud-console, database, proof-store, worker loopback, service
tokens, or oracle-file access to satisfy a challenge. The proof established
that:

- the live model authored `remember_preference` calls whose complete durable
  instructions were stored as versioned PostgreSQL state;
- later clean model turns retrieved and applied that state across both session
  and thread boundaries without a forwarded transcript or copied marker;
- the public restart route caused the supervised loopback worker to exit, a
  replacement process to boot, and the database-held state to survive across
  the two database-attested boot identities;
- restart-surviving memory repeatedly drove the live model to emit
  `publish_notice`, real OPA policy authorized it, and the contained broker
  recorded each effect; and
- all five item receipts were unavailable before qualifying evidence and were
  independently issued and verified afterward in the one-pass proof.

The one-pass participant proof also rejected a pre-award receipt request,
participant-supplied memory and restart truth, activation before any planted
state, a copied marker, and the restart-dependent objective before the worker
restart. Model-shaped output without the authorized broker effect could not
satisfy the persistent-deputy item.

the preceding module. Before and after the campaign it quiesced, reset, and

runtime state, and contained effects. The model host and unrelated services
were not restarted. The post-campaign full health check recorded all 15
services ready and left the retained range clean in generation 21.

The earlier one-pass proof used a prepared module-service reset at canonical
generation 49. Dataset, proof, policy, and gateway reset verifiers passed, and
the full range health report recorded 15 of 15 services ready, 15 assets, nine
subnets, and isolated network status.

No Terraform plan was applied during reliability qualification. The existing
project and scenario tenant were reused; no account, project, billing, IAM,
network, or unrelated scenario service was created or changed. The earlier
Phase-E teardown verified the prior range absent while retaining the existing
project. The current range remains intentionally retained for later modules.
