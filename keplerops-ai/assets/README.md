# `assets/` - KeplerOps AI Systems bespoke content

The ACES environment module at
[`../sdl/modules/environment.sdl.yaml`](../sdl/modules/environment.sdl.yaml)
declares every planted file, dataset, generated artifact, target, and source
binding. The referenced participant briefing, enterprise material, synthetic
credentials and datasets, service source, policy, workflow, model supply
contract, and reset/oracle fixtures live here and are consumed by the
`gcp_full` realization.

Committed source is not a live-build or golden claim. Assets remain pack-local,
synthetic, and joined to their SDL declarations. Real provider
keys, customer data, private model weights, live tenant data, repo-root `.env`,
and mutable or unverified external inputs do not belong here.
