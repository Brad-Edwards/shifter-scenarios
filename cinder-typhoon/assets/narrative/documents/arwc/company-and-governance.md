# Your regional water company

Staff and public reference • September 2026

Alterra Regional Water Company is jointly owned by Pine and River municipal
districts and serves approximately 160,000 residents and local businesses.
The company formed in 2009 by bringing the districts' water-service functions
together. It supplies drinking water from source through treatment and
distribution, including household billing and meter services. Wastewater remains
with separate municipal services.

The owners appoint a joint board with district representatives and independent
members. The board considers the service plan, budget, and rate proposals through
the public approval process. A general manager leads the company. The director
of regional operations, Luc Venn-Morel, reports to the general manager and
presents the service implications of major operational decisions.

Pine and River remain service and cost centres within one company. Shared costs
follow the agreed allocation method. District-specific commitments are accounted
for against the district receiving the specified service. Joint ownership does
not mean every charge is automatically split equally.

Water charges support ordinary operations and planned renewal. Longer-lived
projects have separately approved capital funding. Annual service results,
board reporting, explanations to customers, and consultation over planned work
are part of the company's normal public responsibilities.

ARWC has about 230 staff. Our next five-year programme combines steady renewal,
workforce development, and service for modest regional growth. The aim is a
service the next generation can maintain and customers can continue to rely on.
