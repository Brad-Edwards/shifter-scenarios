# Which period does my water bill cover?

Customer-service explanation • Accepted 15 September 2026

The billing period is the interval identified on your bill. It tells you which
part of your service history the bill covers. The date a bill is issued is a
separate date and can be later than the end of that interval.

Your bill identifies the readings used to calculate metered consumption and
whether a reading is actual or estimated. If a date or reading is unclear,
customer service can explain the entries on your individual account.

When contacting us, tell us which bill and which part you would like explained.
There is no need to reconstruct our internal process before asking the question.
We can explain the billing period first, then discuss how the relevant reading
was obtained or applied if that would help.

For an appointment concerning your meter, customer service will work with the
appointments team to agree the practical arrangements. A question about your
bill does not itself book a meter visit.
