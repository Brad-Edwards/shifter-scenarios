# The treatment refurbishment, 2021–2023

Staff induction background • September 2026

The refurbishment was completed in planned stages while the service continued.
It is an achievement involving operations, maintenance, engineering, planning,
and the colleagues who kept other routine work moving at the same time.

Public reporting recorded progress and the completed result. Staff who worked
through the stages remember the practical coordination: preparing work, fitting
it around existing responsibilities, asking questions early, and leaving a
useful account for the people taking over.

The induction programme should give newer colleagues an opportunity to meet
some of the people behind the work and ask what the experience was like.
The practical arrangements still need to be discussed with the staff involved.

A colleague who joined after 2023 may know the completed facilities without
knowing their history. Both experiences belong in the company. Nobody needs to
have been here for a particular project to take pride in the service now.
