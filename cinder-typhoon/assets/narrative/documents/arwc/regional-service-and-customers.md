# Regional service and customer relationships

Customer-service reference • September 2026

Most customers encounter ARWC through their ordinary water service, bills, meter
appointments, planned work, and information about seasonal use. Municipal
facilities are customers alongside households and businesses.

Cairn Reach Reservoir is one important reserve within a wider source portfolio.
ARWC also has an established supplementary bulk-supply agreement with Merewick
Bulk Water Cooperative. Merewick serves neighbouring systems from a larger,
different source and storage portfolio. ARWC is a contract customer, rather
than a member owner. Available deliveries depend on Merewick's commitments and
conditions, and are limited by the agreement.

Belvarn Packaging is a River district manufacturer whose shorter production
runs make clear scheduling useful. Its staff are considering an efficiency
project and ask practical questions about metering and ordinary bills. Belvarn
also works with the local training college on apprenticeships.

Talvern Growers Cooperative represents Pine district growers, including nurseries
and producers of high-value horticultural crops. ARWC provides a limited,
supplementary seasonal irrigation allowance through the treated distribution
service. Members' other water arrangements are outside that contract. Talvern
helps members understand the allowance and arrange common information sessions.

Different customers need different explanations. A useful response starts with
the question they asked and the service involved. Customer-service staff can
bring in planning or other colleagues when a question needs their expertise.
