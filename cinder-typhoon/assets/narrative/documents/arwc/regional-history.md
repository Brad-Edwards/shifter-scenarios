# Becoming a regional service

Records and staff induction note • September 2026

Before 2009, Pine and River operated separate water services. Bringing them
together made it possible to share planning, purchasing, and management while
local depots continued their practical work. It also allowed the company to
develop specialist expertise that the districts had often bought separately.

The change took time. Staff learned colleagues' routines, and records retained
names and descriptions from the services that produced them. Some of those
names still occur in conversation. Many employees joined after the merger and
identify more strongly with their team or profession than a predecessor service.

Between 2014 and 2017, staged distribution renewal completed several neighbourhood
jobs that staff still remember with pride. Customer service adopted common
account procedures during 2018–2019. FieldKest was introduced during the same
period for selected external handovers, reducing repeated collection of records.

The treatment refurbishment completed between 2021 and 2023 is another shared
achievement. More recently, training and succession have received attention as
experienced employees consider their next career stages. The 2025 apprentice
intake is part of that continuing development.

The staff-history scanning project has preserved photographs and other material
from earlier offices. Descriptions identify where and when an item belongs so
someone can understand it without finding a colleague who remembers it personally.
