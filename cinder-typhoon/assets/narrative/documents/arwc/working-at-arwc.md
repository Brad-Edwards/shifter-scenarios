Our service has overlapping working days. Some colleagues work plant shifts,
some travel between field assignments, and others provide customer-service or
office coverage. Check those arrangements when organizing a meeting or staff
event. Main-office hours do not describe everyone's day.

Most operational and customer-service positions are covered by established
collective agreements. Management and some professional roles have separate
employment arrangements. Your manager and the appropriate staff representative
can explain the arrangements for your position.

Development can mean deeper technical expertise, training work, a different
specialism, or a supervisory role. Apprentices work through planned assignments
under supervision. They arrive with different prior experience and should feel
able to ask questions, including asking for an explanation a second time.

Experienced colleagues also have development goals. Planning for succession
includes time to prepare and teach, as well as the teaching session itself.
Long service is not a substitute for asking somebody about their plans.

Voluntary staff activities include the autumn photography display and staff
walk. Some colleagues participate; others have different interests or commitments.
There are many ways to belong to the company.
