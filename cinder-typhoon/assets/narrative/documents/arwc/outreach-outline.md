# Before water reaches the tap

Community session outline • Agreed at rehearsal, 10 September 2026

**Opening question:** What happens before water reaches our tap?

Explain that ARWC looks after a connected service: obtaining source water,
treating it, keeping the distribution system working, and helping customers
understand the service they receive. People with different skills do those jobs
alongside one another.

Owen's relay comparison is useful for explaining that different responsibilities
contribute to the same result. Make clear that the work continues throughout
the day; one department does not finish permanently when another takes over.

Use an everyday example such as arranging a meter visit. A customer-service
colleague helps agree the appointment, field staff do the practical work, and
the account records the result. Leave time for the audience's questions.

The separate Pine and River services joined in 2009. Mention that history if
it helps answer a question, rather than making it the introduction everyone
must sit through.
