# Rillhaven maintenance workshop — draft for rehearsal

Talia Mornac • 8 September 2026

The workshop is planned for Thursday, 24 September. Rillhaven's seasonal handover
is complete, and its staff have asked to work through an example and discuss
their experience.

I propose opening with one ordinary submission and explaining where it belongs
in a maintenance package. The customer should then have time to describe how
that fits their working day and what still takes unnecessary effort.

Maya can help with the questions about ongoing support. I would like to lead
the presentation myself. We should record product suggestions separately from
work we agree to deliver.

For tomorrow's rehearsal, I particularly want to check whether the example is
clear and whether enough time remains for questions. The introduction has already
been shortened; I do not think a longer product tour will help this audience.
