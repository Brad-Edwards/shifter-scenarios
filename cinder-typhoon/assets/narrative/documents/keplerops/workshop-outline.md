# Rillhaven maintenance workshop

Preparation outline • Talia Mornac • Revised 9 September 2026

**Planned date:** Thursday, 24 September 2026

**Lead:** Talia Mornac. Maya Ranscombe will take questions about ongoing support.

Rillhaven's seasonal handover is complete. The workshop is an opportunity for
its staff to work through an example, discuss their experience, and offer product
feedback before the busiest part of the heating season.

## Proposed shape

1. Ask what each group wants to get from the session.
2. Work through one ordinary submission and its place in a maintenance package.
3. Let the customer explain which parts fit their own working day and which
   parts still take more effort than they should.
4. Discuss support questions with Maya.
5. Record product suggestions separately from commitments to deliver work.

The example should make clear who needs the result and what they do with it.
The introduction can be short. We should preserve time for questions rather
than trying to demonstrate every part of the product.

## After the rehearsal

The opening remains organized around the example. Maya's suggestion to explain
its intended audience earlier was useful and is included. Talia will lead the
presentation; Maya will support the discussion in the agreed part.

The workshop has not yet taken place. The follow-up will record questions,
agreed work, and suggestions once the customer has had the session.
