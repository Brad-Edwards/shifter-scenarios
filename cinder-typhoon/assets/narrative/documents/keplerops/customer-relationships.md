# Working with established utility customers

Support and implementation reference • September 2026

ARWC adopted FieldKest during 2018–2019 to improve the receipt of external field
and maintenance records. The early work reduced repeated collection of material
and helped us understand how a supported integration fits an established utility.

The relationship has matured. ARWC expects continuity when an individual colleague
is away. We support a product and agreed implementation work, rather than assuming
every request is a continuation of the original consultancy. Maya and Theo keep
much of the daily relationship moving, with Rowan and Clara contributing technical
expertise and commercial and procurement colleagues managing the contract.

ARWC is an important reference and renewal customer. A reference conversation
requires an agreed agenda and staff time. The customer should describe its own
experience in its own words.

Rillhaven District Heating uses a different supported interface and prepares
seasonal maintenance records ahead of increased heating demand. Its staff offer
constructive product feedback and appreciate practical workshops. A good idea
raised at a workshop is recorded as a suggestion until the appropriate work has
been agreed.

Our other utility customers have their own calendars, supported interfaces,
and habits. Keeping a useful account of those differences is part of the work.
