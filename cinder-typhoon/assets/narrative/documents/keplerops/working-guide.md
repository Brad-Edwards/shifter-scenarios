# Working at KeplerOps

New-starter guide • Revised 7 September 2026

Welcome. Your buddy can help you find the people behind the names you encounter
in your first weeks. Ask questions as they arise; nobody expects the company
to become familiar all at once.

## What we make

FieldKest connects field submissions, maintenance packages, asset records, and
operational reports with the processes customers already use. We began as an
integration consultancy in 2016. The product grew out of repeatedly collecting
the same pieces of information so a customer could accept completed work.

We still earn our reputation through useful software and people who understand
its use. Our customers operate utilities. Their working day, procurement process,
and definition of a completed job may differ from ours.

## Working together

Most locally based colleagues use Wednesday and Thursday as common office days.
Agree your own arrangements with your manager. Remote colleagues participate in
decisions and demonstrations; leave a useful written account when a conversation
produces a decision others need to know about.

Bring an example when it helps explain a question. You do not need a presentation
for every discussion. Release meetings normally have an agenda and an ending;
product discussions sometimes need a follow-up when an example raises another
question.

Your manager will discuss development with you. Specialist progression and
management are both available paths. Asking about a future role does not commit
you to applying for the next vacancy.

Social arrangements are optional. Some colleagues lunch together; others use
the break for errands or quiet time. Introductions across functions are part
of getting to know the work, and your buddy can arrange them.
