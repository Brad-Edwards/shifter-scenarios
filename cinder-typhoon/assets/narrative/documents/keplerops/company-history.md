# Ten years of KeplerOps

Background for anniversary contributions • August 2026

Leah founded KeplerOps in 2016 to solve a repeated customer problem: field work
was complete, but the information needed to accept it was scattered among people
and systems. The first team worked from rented rooms and spent a good deal of
time visiting customers.

During 2018 and 2019, that work became FieldKest, a product we could support and
improve across customers. ARWC was among the utilities that helped us learn
what a substantial, repeated integration needed to do. Rowan joined in 2018;
Maya joined in 2019.

Remote working became established during 2020 and 2021. We also tried a simplified
self-service edition. Demand was limited: customers generally needed help with
their actual integrations. We wound down the edition while supporting existing
customers through the transition. Some of its design ideas remain fondly remembered.

In 2022 we moved to the current office and kept the hybrid model. The new space
made smaller conversations easier, although opinions about the old office remain
divided.

A substantial multi-site implementation finished in 2024, during a period when
we deliberately kept growth modest. The project photograph captures a result
worth being proud of. Other colleagues remember the routine work they kept
moving while that project received attention.

Hiring resumed selectively in 2025 and 2026. We have 64 colleagues and a clearer
framework for development. Newer employees now own work that used to depend on
someone knowing its whole history.

For the anniversary gathering, contributions from different years will sit
alongside this chronology. Please obtain the contributor's agreement before
sharing a personal account. The gathering is being arranged for November.
