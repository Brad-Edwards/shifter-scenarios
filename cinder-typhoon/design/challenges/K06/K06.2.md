## Challenge description — player-facing

Your publication identity still cannot read the private support package. Cross the package-view boundary and recover its protected contents.

## Story purpose

Adds a separate registry data compromise after publication access.

## Prerequisites and starting position

**Required access or evidence:** [K06.1](../K06/K06.1.md).

**Starting material:** The package import workflow, its documented input form, a rehearsal consumer, and the registry interfaces exposed by the workflow.

**Campaign references:** No separately named capability output.

## Completion and downstream use

Recover the protected package through the distinct package-view authorization weakness. Merely reusing A01 for an already permitted read does not earn this score.

## Difficulty and inspiration

Requires distinguishing publication rights from the package view's separate access rules.

**Inspiration:** [Prison Pipeline (HTB Business CTF 2024; organizer Medium / writeup Easy)](../../reference-catalog.md#c01). Adapts the importer-to-registry portion of Prison Pipeline; production ARWC delivery remains K26.
