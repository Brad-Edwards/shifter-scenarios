## Challenge description — player-facing

The importer can retrieve material the developer cannot read directly. Recover usable authority over the release channel consumed by FieldLink customers.

## Story purpose

Creates the ordinary package-delivery route's publication capability.

## Prerequisites and starting position

**Required access or evidence:** [G03 — K05.1](../K05/K05.1.md).

**Starting material:** The package import workflow, its documented input form, a rehearsal consumer, and the registry interfaces exposed by the workflow.

**Campaign references:** `A01`.

## Completion and downstream use

Recover and demonstrate the limited registry publication authority. This supplies A01; recovering its configuration and using the resulting credential are one achievement.

## Difficulty and inspiration

A familiar application boundary with a clear registry target and bounded scope.

**Inspiration:** [Prison Pipeline (HTB Business CTF 2024; organizer Medium / writeup Easy)](../../reference-catalog.md#c01). Adapts the importer-to-registry portion of Prison Pipeline; production ARWC delivery remains K26.

## Technical design

### Surface and normal behavior
The FieldLink importer uses a registry configuration object that the developer cannot directly read, and a scoped Verdaccio publication identity for the customer connector channel.

### Vulnerability and intended solution
The importer exposes its resolved configuration in an error path after validating a normal package request. The intended solution is to recover and use the scoped credential through the registry's ordinary authenticated flow.

### Evidence and completion
Registry audit data must show the scoped identity performing its permitted release-channel action; this awards `A01`.

### Boundaries and reset
The identity is limited to the designated package scope and cannot read private support packages, administer registry policy, or act as the customer; reset revokes it.

### Author checks
Test direct developer denial, intended error evidence, scoped registry use, other-scope denial, no score for copied configuration, and revocation.
