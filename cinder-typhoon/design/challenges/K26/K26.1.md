## Challenge description — player-facing

Build an altered FieldLink package that preserves the customer's expected interface and publish it to the release channel the active ARWC integration consumes.

## Story purpose

Turns supplier authority into a customer-compatible delivery artifact.

## Prerequisites and starting position

**Required access or evidence:** ([A01 — K06.1](../K06/K06.1.md) AND [G02 — K25.2](../K25/K25.2.md)) OR ([K20.3](../K20/K20.3.md) AND [G02 — K25.2](../K25/K25.2.md) AND [G03 — K05.1](../K05/K05.1.md)).

**Starting material:** Earned publication authority, G02's customer binding, the consumed-package contract, and the customer's ordinary activation workflow.

**Campaign references:** `A03`.

## Completion and downstream use

Publish the compatible changed package with the correct tenant/channel binding. This supplies A03. Customer-side activation and execution remain K26.2.

## Difficulty and inspiration

A bounded package adaptation and explicit customer join; no advanced signing research is required.

**Inspiration:** [Prison Pipeline (HTB Business CTF 2024; organizer Medium / writeup Easy)](../../reference-catalog.md#c01). Adapts Prison Pipeline's package-consumption mechanism. The K20 authority alternative is optional; the A01 route is the ordinary medium route.
