## Challenge description — player-facing

Make the database service execute a job that produces a copy of its protected reconciliation log under the service's own execution identity.

## Story purpose

Completes the application-to-backend chain with a concrete effect.

## Prerequisites and starting position

**Required access or evidence:** [W07.2](../W07/W07.2.md).

**Starting material:** The earned planner session, its restricted query interface, and normal report/query examples.

**Campaign references:** No separately named capability output.

## Completion and downstream use

Demonstrate backend-context execution and recover the resulting reconciliation-log copy. The ledger read in W07.2 does not confer this score.

## Difficulty and inspiration

Requires a new execution transition beyond the restricted-query data capability.

**Inspiration:** [Aurors Archive (HTB Cyber Apocalypse 2025; Hard)](../../reference-catalog.md#c19). Retains Aurors Archive's restricted-database boundary after the browser chain.
