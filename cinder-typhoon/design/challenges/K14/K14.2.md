## Challenge description — player-facing

Use the workload trust relationship to establish the scoped role intended for another audience and retrieve its protected role record.

## Story purpose

Expands identity scope through a meaningful trust mistake.

## Prerequisites and starting position

**Required access or evidence:** [K14.1](../K14/K14.1.md).

**Starting material:** The earned workload context, its trust documentation, and the support-export service's ordinary policy view.

**Campaign references:** No separately named capability output.

## Completion and downstream use

Demonstrate the resulting role against its protected record. Obtaining and using that role earn one score; this is the K14 scoped-role output.

## Difficulty and inspiration

A bounded identity-trust join with clear role and audience evidence.

**Inspiration:** Original challenge for this campaign.
