## Challenge description — player-facing

Run a feedback policy through your earned scheduler capability so the bounded objective is met across the disclosed demand and calibration conditions.

## Story purpose

Extends a one-case result into a robust process-control achievement.

## Prerequisites and starting position

**Required access or evidence:** [W33.3](../W33/W33.3.md).

**Starting material:** A supplied forecast, ordinary commissioning records, a practice model, and the earned process/control context for live stages. W16's calibration record is an optional alternative input.

**Campaign references:** No separately named capability output.

## Completion and downstream use

Demonstrate the policy through the scheduler in independent private rehearsal cases within the published bounds, using available observations. A standalone offline policy or one replayed winning schedule is insufficient; scenario time advances on demand.

## Difficulty and inspiration

An authored feedback-policy problem beyond the source-inspired fixed trajectory.

**Inspiration:** [Terraforming Mars (Hack-a-Sat 2023; relative 4/5)](../../reference-catalog.md#c30). Terraforming Mars supplies constrained trajectory planning. The water setting, scheduler boundary, and feedback-policy extension are authored adaptations.
