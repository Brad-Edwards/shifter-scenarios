## Challenge description — player-facing

Carry the planned release across the scheduler's authorization boundary and demonstrate its bounded economic effect in the live rehearsal.

## Story purpose

Adds a real application/control problem after offline planning.

## Prerequisites and starting position

**Required access or evidence:** ([W33.2](../W33/W33.2.md) AND [PROCESS_INTERPRETATION](../../capability-routes.json) AND [CONTROL](../../capability-routes.json) AND [MODE](../../capability-routes.json) AND [CONSEQUENCE_PLAN](../../capability-routes.json)).

**Starting material:** A supplied forecast, ordinary commissioning records, a practice model, and the earned process/control context for live stages. W16's calibration record is an optional alternative input.

**Campaign references:** No separately named capability output.

## Completion and downstream use

Demonstrate accepted scoped actions, actual bounded reserve loss, and the resulting economic effect. Bind the advanced schedule to the current evidence and recompute its consequence using the earned model; a valid plan for another schedule or checkpoint is insufficient.

## Difficulty and inspiration

Composes the constrained plan with a separate scheduler authorization problem.

**Inspiration:** [Terraforming Mars (Hack-a-Sat 2023; relative 4/5)](../../reference-catalog.md#c30). Terraforming Mars supplies constrained trajectory planning. The water setting, scheduler boundary, and feedback-policy extension are authored adaptations.
