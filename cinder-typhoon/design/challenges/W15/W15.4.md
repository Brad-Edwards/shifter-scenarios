## Challenge description — player-facing

Recover the protected maintenance approval through the downstream authority check that still restricts the review capability.

## Story purpose

Completes an advanced path to a useful approval artifact.

## Prerequisites and starting position

**Required access or evidence:** [W15.3](../W15/W15.3.md).

**Starting material:** The maintenance drawing viewer, cache observations, ordinary review material, and a protected approval service.

**Campaign references:** `W15.APPROVAL`.

## Completion and downstream use

Recover the current approval with its maintenance and revision binding. This supplies W15.APPROVAL for W26; it grants no live control on its own.

## Difficulty and inspiration

Requires composing cache, session, and the approval service's separate token constraints.

**Inspiration:** [OmniWatch (HTB Business CTF 2024; directory Medium / writeup Hard)](../../reference-catalog.md#c24). Adapts OmniWatch's cache, browser, and token-boundary composition; its original tier labels differ.
