## Challenge description — player-facing

Recover the restricted cache association for the maintenance drawing and identify the version the review workflow will serve.

## Story purpose

Grounds the cache investigation in an actual maintenance record.

## Prerequisites and starting position

**Required access or evidence:** [W13.SESSION](../../capability-routes.json) OR [W03.READ](../../capability-routes.json).

**Starting material:** The maintenance drawing viewer, cache observations, ordinary review material, and a protected approval service.

**Campaign references:** No separately named capability output.

## Completion and downstream use

Recover the real cache/drawing association. Public viewer configuration or an unrelated cached object is insufficient.

## Difficulty and inspiration

A bounded cache and document-binding investigation.

**Inspiration:** [OmniWatch (HTB Business CTF 2024; directory Medium / writeup Hard)](../../reference-catalog.md#c24). Adapts OmniWatch's cache, browser, and token-boundary composition; its original tier labels differ.
