## Challenge description — player-facing

Recover the limited release principal behind the protected build dossier and establish what it can actually access.

## Story purpose

Starts the durable-delivery branch with a scoped cloud capability.

## Prerequisites and starting position

**Required access or evidence:** ([G01 — K01.1](../K01/K01.1.md) AND [G02 — K25.2](../K25/K25.2.md) AND [K09.1](../K09/K09.1.md)) OR ([G01 — K01.1](../K01/K01.1.md) AND [G02 — K25.2](../K25/K25.2.md) AND [K13.1](../K13/K13.1.md)) OR ([G01 — K01.1](../K01/K01.1.md) AND [G02 — K25.2](../K25/K25.2.md) AND [K04.4](../K04/K04.4.md)).

**Starting material:** G02's current customer binding, a developer-facing release-dossier interface identified by the earned lead, build records, and the dossier's package-interface examples. A dossier lead supplies a location, not a cloud principal.

**Campaign references:** No separately named capability output.

## Completion and downstream use

Cross the build-record boundary and demonstrate the recovered principal against its protected release dossier. The dossier also supplies the package-interface examples used by K29.3. A credential string alone is insufficient.

## Difficulty and inspiration

A bounded authorization problem tied to the discovered build dossier.

**Inspiration:** [PipeDream (HTB Business CTF 2025; Hard)](../../reference-catalog.md#c27). PipeDream supplies the cloud/source/signing composition. The final rollover objective is an authored extension.
