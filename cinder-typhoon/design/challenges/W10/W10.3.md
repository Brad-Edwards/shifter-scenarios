## Challenge description — player-facing

Cross the linked-document service's separate access boundary and recover the full restricted planning record behind that excerpt.

## Story purpose

Turns partial disclosure into a complete protected planning artifact.

## Prerequisites and starting position

**Required access or evidence:** [W10.2](../W10/W10.2.md).

**Starting material:** The district-report assistant's source records, ordinary search interface, and linked planning documents.

**Campaign references:** No separately named capability output.

## Completion and downstream use

Recover the correct district's full record and revision. Repeating the excerpt or generating a summary earns no new credit. No plant authority is granted.

## Difficulty and inspiration

Requires a distinct document-ownership decision after the retrieval disclosure.

**Inspiration:** [ATLAS application-trust inspiration](../../threat-inspiration.md#atlas). ATLAS informs the data-isolation theme; these objectives use deterministic retrieval, not costly model attacks.
