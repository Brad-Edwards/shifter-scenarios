## Challenge description — player-facing

Construct a measurement interaction that causes a controlled change outside its intended estimator state.

## Story purpose

Begins a deep attack on a real planning component.

## Prerequisites and starting position

**Required access or evidence:** [OT_READ](../../capability-routes.json).

**Starting material:** The isolated planning estimator, normal measurement inputs, its analysis material, and a local reproducer.

**Campaign references:** No separately named capability output.

## Completion and downstream use

Demonstrate a reproducible controlled state violation. A crash or an ordinary change caused by a valid measurement does not count.

## Difficulty and inspiration

Native lifecycle/state analysis inspired by Kalman At Me Bro.

**Inspiration:** [Kalman At Me Bro (Hack-a-Sat 2023; relative 5/5)](../../reference-catalog.md#c13). Retains Kalman At Me Bro's native measurement-state exploitation; difficult mathematics is not the challenge premise.
