## Challenge description — player-facing

Develop the useful memory capability needed to influence the estimator's protected planning state under its operating constraints.

## Story purpose

Establishes a new exploitation capability beyond the original fault.

## Prerequisites and starting position

**Required access or evidence:** [W23.1](../W23/W23.1.md).

**Starting material:** The isolated planning estimator, normal measurement inputs, its analysis material, and a local reproducer.

**Campaign references:** No separately named capability output.

## Completion and downstream use

Demonstrate controlled access to the designated protected state while the estimator remains usable. Repeating the first state violation is insufficient.

## Difficulty and inspiration

A demanding native exploitation transition with retained estimator constraints.

**Inspiration:** [Kalman At Me Bro (Hack-a-Sat 2023; relative 5/5)](../../reference-catalog.md#c13). Retains Kalman At Me Bro's native measurement-state exploitation; difficult mathematics is not the challenge premise.
