## Challenge description — player-facing

Recover the release access exposed by the failed training build and retrieve the protected rehearsal-channel manifest.

## Story purpose

Establishes a limited release capability with a concrete result.

## Prerequisites and starting position

**Required access or evidence:** the supplied training workspace.

**Starting material:** The training repository, retained build diagnostics, a sample package, and the rehearsal publication/consumer interfaces.

**Campaign references:** No separately named capability output.

## Completion and downstream use

Recover and demonstrate the scoped publication access through the protected manifest. This supplies T03.4; copying a credential alone is insufficient.

## Difficulty and inspiration

A short build-artifact and scoped-access task.

**Inspiration:** Original challenge for this campaign.

## Technical design

### Surface and normal behavior

The training build service publishes ordinary failed-job logs and stores its
rehearsal-channel manifest in the package registry. A failed release step logs
a registry configuration reference for the job's scoped publisher identity.

### Vulnerability and intended solution

The job output over-discloses a valid but narrowly scoped registry credential.
The intended solution is to recover it from the genuine build evidence and use
the registry's normal protected-manifest read, rather than treat log text as
completion.

### Evidence and completion

Registry audit data must show the scoped identity reading the rehearsal-channel
manifest. That event awards the publication capability consumed by T03.4 and
records the manifest revision.

### Boundaries and reset

The credential can read its one manifest and publish only the training formatter
scope; it cannot alter build configuration or other packages. Reset revokes and
regenerates the player-specific credential.

### Author checks

Test readable failed logs, correct credential scope, protected manifest read,
denial for another package scope, absence of score for copied secret text, and
credential revocation on reset.
