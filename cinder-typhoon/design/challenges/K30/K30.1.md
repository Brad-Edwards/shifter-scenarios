## Challenge description — player-facing

Recover the scoped backup principal from the build records and establish its allowed operation while direct access to the source database remains denied.

## Story purpose

Introduces a separate control-plane capability over protected history.

## Prerequisites and starting position

**Required access or evidence:** [K13.4](../K13/K13.4.md) OR [K09.4](../K09/K09.4.md).

**Starting material:** The earned workload identity's authenticated build-record view, a backup-catalog lead, and records describing the protected history. K09.1 and K13.1 can reveal the lead earlier but supply no authenticated workload context.

**Campaign references:** No separately named capability output.

## Completion and downstream use

Demonstrate the backup principal's permitted scope and the continuing source-data restriction. Finding the catalog alone does not count.

## Difficulty and inspiration

A bounded metadata and identity-scope investigation.

**Inspiration:** [codebuild_secrets (CloudGoat; Hard challenge lab)](../../reference-catalog.md#c28). Adapts CloudGoat codebuild_secrets' protected-data recovery problem; the source is a Hard lab, not a timed CTF.
