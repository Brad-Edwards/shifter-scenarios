## Challenge description — player-facing

Recover the protected archive and the maintenance history it contains.

## Story purpose

Gives the reconstruction an actual data-recovery payoff.

## Prerequisites and starting position

**Required access or evidence:** [W12.2](../W12/W12.2.md).

**Starting material:** A quarantined historical collector, its retained collection records, an encrypted archive, and a referenced current data service.

**Campaign references:** No separately named capability output.

## Completion and downstream use

Recover the complete, correctly bound historical collection. A sample transformation or an unrelated decrypted record does not count.

## Difficulty and inspiration

Requires applying the recovered behavior to the archive's actual protection and context.

**Inspiration:** [Confinement (HTB Cyber Apocalypse 2024; Hard)](../../reference-catalog.md#c17). Confinement supplies collector and archive reconstruction. The historical-to-current collection continuation is an authored extension.
