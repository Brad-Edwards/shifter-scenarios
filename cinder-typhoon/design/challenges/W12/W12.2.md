## Challenge description — player-facing

Reconstruct the collector's cryptographic behavior well enough to reproduce its handling of the supplied archive samples.

## Story purpose

Establishes understanding before recovering the protected collection.

## Prerequisites and starting position

**Required access or evidence:** [W12.1](../W12/W12.1.md).

**Starting material:** A quarantined historical collector, its retained collection records, an encrypted archive, and a referenced current data service.

**Campaign references:** No separately named capability output.

## Completion and downstream use

Produce the correct sample transformations and collection context. Naming a cryptographic algorithm is insufficient.

## Difficulty and inspiration

Program and cryptographic reconstruction directly inspired by Confinement.

**Inspiration:** [Confinement (HTB Cyber Apocalypse 2024; Hard)](../../reference-catalog.md#c17). Confinement supplies collector and archive reconstruction. The historical-to-current collection continuation is an authored extension.
