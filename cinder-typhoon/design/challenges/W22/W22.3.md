## Challenge description — player-facing

Open the sealed engineering project and recover the commissioning decision omitted from the business report. Establish which project revision the decision concerns.

## Story purpose

Completes the protected-project investigation with the engineering finding that explains the missing warning in the business report.

## Prerequisites and starting position

**Required access or evidence:** [W22.2](../W22/W22.2.md).

**Starting material:** The protected project-viewer delivery, ordinary viewing examples, and the engineering package associated with the current project evidence.

**Campaign references:** No separately named capability output.

## Completion and downstream use

Recover the project-bound key, the readable engineering material, and its commissioning decision as one achievement. The opened package also supplies the retained review helper, captured commissioning session, and matching archive context for W22.4. The decision provides optional context for W34; it supplies neither live revision evidence nor control authority.

## Difficulty and inspiration

Whole-program reconstruction beyond the instruction fragments in W22.2. Key recovery, decryption, and interpretation are the completion of that work; no additional score is reserved for using the recovered key.

**Inspiration:** [Heart Protector (HTB Cyber Apocalypse 2025; Hard)](../../reference-catalog.md#c25). Adapts Heart Protector's runtime virtual-machine and protected-artifact problem.
