## Challenge description — player-facing

Reconstruct the viewer's embedded instruction behavior and reproduce the designated program fragments.

## Story purpose

Builds a substantive model of the viewer's internal machine.

## Prerequisites and starting position

**Required access or evidence:** [W22.1](../W22/W22.1.md).

**Campaign references:** No separately named capability output.
