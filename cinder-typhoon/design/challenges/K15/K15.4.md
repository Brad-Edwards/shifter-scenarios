## Challenge description — player-facing

Recover the ARWC collection from the handed-over export material. Resolve its partition and revision records so the result belongs to the current customer binding.

## Story purpose

Produces useful customer intelligence, not merely a downloaded archive.

## Prerequisites and starting position

**Required access or evidence:** ([K15.3](../K15/K15.3.md) AND [G02 — K25.2](../K25/K25.2.md)).

**Starting material:** Support job references, an ordinary customer-export interface, and the earned cloud or support identity.

**Campaign references:** No separately named capability output.

## Completion and downstream use

Recover a coherent collection for G02's customer and revision. Mixed tenants, stale partitions, and the job's empty receipt do not satisfy the objective.

## Difficulty and inspiration

A bounded data-reconstruction join after the export change.

**Inspiration:** Original challenge for this campaign.
