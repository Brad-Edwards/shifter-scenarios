## Challenge description — player-facing

Someone from Cinder worked this lead before you. Recover the staging artifact buried in their retained document and identify the collection it belongs to.

## Story purpose

Connects the current operation to an earlier unfinished attempt.

## Prerequisites and starting position

**Required access or evidence:** the supplied KeplerOps developer foothold.

**Starting material:** A retained staging bundle from an earlier Cinder attempt, its carrier document, and a short collection note.

**Campaign references:** No separately named capability output.

## Completion and downstream use

Recover the actual staging artifact and its collection binding. It supplies the material for K03.2, not the final archive contents.

## Difficulty and inspiration

A bounded carrier/artifact investigation rather than a large forensic image.

**Inspiration:** [Game Invitation (HTB Cyber Apocalypse 2024; Hard)](../../reference-catalog.md#c04). The inspiration is document and script reconstruction; the objective remains collection for Cinder.
