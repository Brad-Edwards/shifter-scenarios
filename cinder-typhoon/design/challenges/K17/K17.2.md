## Challenge description — player-facing

The support account's recovery process exposes a separate opportunity. Establish the alternate support identity and recover its private recovery note.

## Story purpose

Adds a distinct identity compromise beyond the retained session.

## Prerequisites and starting position

**Required access or evidence:** [B01 — K17.1](../K17/K17.1.md).

**Starting material:** The repository's support-client material, retained session artifacts, and ordinary support-account workflows.

**Campaign references:** No separately named capability output.

## Completion and downstream use

Demonstrate the alternate recovered identity and its private note. Reusing B01 or changing its local session label does not count.

## Difficulty and inspiration

A bounded recovery-workflow flaw with clear account evidence.

**Inspiration:** Original challenge for this campaign.
