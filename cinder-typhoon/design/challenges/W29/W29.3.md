## Challenge description — player-facing

Construct a release plan that reduces usable reserve within the operating envelope and explains the resulting replacement cost and restrictions.

## Story purpose

Defines the intended non-catastrophic consequence before live action.

## Prerequisites and starting position

**Required access or evidence:** ([W29.1](../W29/W29.1.md) AND [W29.2](../W29/W29.2.md) AND [MODE](../../capability-routes.json)).

**Starting material:** The current settlement tariff, allocation evidence, fresh process observations, and the supplied reserve/consequence model.

**Campaign references:** `W29.PLAN`.

## Completion and downstream use

Produce the bounded plan and its predicted reserve and economic effects. This supplies W29.PLAN and CONSEQUENCE_PLAN; the tariff, current evidence, and mode must agree.

## Difficulty and inspiration

A documented consequence calculation and evidence join, with no hidden physics puzzle.

**Inspiration:** Original challenge for this campaign.
