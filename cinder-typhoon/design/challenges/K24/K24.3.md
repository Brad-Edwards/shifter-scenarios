## Challenge description — player-facing

Make the completion application retrieve the internal support-job handover under the job's execution identity, although the ordinary completion response cannot disclose that document.

## Story purpose

Completes an insecure-AI episode with actual application execution.

## Prerequisites and starting position

**Required access or evidence:** [K24.2](../K24/K24.2.md).

**Starting material:** The exposed assistant configuration, the completion feature's ordinary examples, and its limited job interface.

**Campaign references:** No separately named capability output.

## Completion and downstream use

Recover the internal handover through an action executed in the completion job's context. Generated code or a fabricated copy of the document does not count.

## Difficulty and inspiration

A clear application trust boundary around completed code, without a costly model attack.

**Inspiration:** [Chrono Mind (HTB Business CTF 2024; Easy)](../../reference-catalog.md#c02). Adapts Chrono Mind's application context and completed-code trust boundaries, with small bounded inference.
