## Challenge description — player-facing

Obtain the scoped completion credential from context outside the feature's intended selection.

## Story purpose

Establishes a limited application capability through context access.

## Prerequisites and starting position

**Required access or evidence:** [K24.1](../K24/K24.1.md).

**Starting material:** The exposed assistant configuration, the completion feature's ordinary examples, and its limited job interface.

The completion feature's retained input records and ordinary validation messages provide evidence about accepted context references. The player can compare them with the returned completion and test a recovered credential against the limited job interface, which responds under its normal access rules. These materials remain within the starting access and do not expose protected contents or the expected secret. The application does not tell the player that a context-selection exploit succeeded or which attack step to try next.

**Campaign references:** No separately named capability output.

## Completion and downstream use

Recover and demonstrate the completion credential's actual scope. Public sample keys or generated guesses do not count.

## Difficulty and inspiration

A bounded application-context selection problem.

**Inspiration:** [Chrono Mind (HTB Business CTF 2024; Easy)](../../reference-catalog.md#c02). Adapts Chrono Mind's application context and completed-code trust boundaries, with small bounded inference.
