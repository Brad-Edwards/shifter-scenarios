## Challenge description — player-facing

Reconcile the allocation ledger and meter export. Establish the actual reserve discrepancy hidden by the reassuring business report.

## Story purpose

Reveals why Cinder's target is financially vulnerable: the company has committed water that its reassuring report misrepresents. The investigation changes the player's understanding of the target before it changes the plant.

## Prerequisites and starting position

**Required access or evidence:** ([W09.1](../W09/W09.1.md) AND [W09.2](../W09/W09.2.md)).

**Starting material:** Corporate allocation records, a separately published meter export, readable units/period definitions, and integration discovery material.

**Campaign references:** `W09.DATA`.

## Completion and downstream use

Produce the correctly bound unit, period, and identifier reconciliation with the actual reserve discrepancy and its supporting records. This supplies W09.DATA. The explanation must account for the physical assets represented by the records.

The completed intelligence view puts the company's reported position beside the corrected reserve and allocation commitments, identifying the records responsible for the difference. The player sees what the missing water means to ARWC, not just a corrected total. This is an evidence-based reveal in Cinder's briefing; it neither edits the corporate ledger nor moves water, and it supplies no later tariff or control capability.

## Difficulty and inspiration

A bounded cross-record interpretation problem with supplied definitions.

**Inspiration:** Original challenge for this campaign.
