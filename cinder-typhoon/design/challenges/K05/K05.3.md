## Challenge description — player-facing

The release manifest and the customer's recorded selection do not describe the same dependency. Reconcile the two and recover the exact discrepancy.

## Story purpose

Turns release metadata into an operationally useful finding.

## Prerequisites and starting position

**Required access or evidence:** [G03 — K05.1](../K05/K05.1.md).

**Starting material:** The readable FieldLink package workspace, interface examples, release records, and an ordinary consumer-check facility.

**Campaign references:** No separately named capability output.

## Completion and downstream use

Produce a correctly bound comparison of package, version, and consumer revision. It must identify the actual disagreement, not just differing text.

## Difficulty and inspiration

A small evidence join involving version and consumer identity.

**Inspiration:** Original challenge for this campaign.

## Technical design

### Surface and normal behavior
The release manifest and active tenant record each declare package, version, consumer revision, timestamp, and provenance for `TEN-ARWC-047`.

### Vulnerability and intended solution
This is a structured reconciliation task. The intended solution joins the two authoritative records and identifies the exact dependency discrepancy, excluding stale tenant and package values.

### Evidence and completion
The scorer verifies a four-field comparison with both record IDs and makes the discrepancy available to K05.4.

### Boundaries and reset
Records cannot be edited and the comparison runs against a fixed synthetic dataset; reset removes only player evidence.

### Author checks
Test clear active/obsolete labeling, a unique discrepancy, rejection of text-only comparison, correct provenance references, and reset.
