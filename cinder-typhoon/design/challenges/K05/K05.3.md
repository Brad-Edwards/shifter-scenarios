## Challenge description — player-facing

The release manifest and the customer's recorded selection do not describe the same dependency. Reconcile the two and recover the exact discrepancy.

## Story purpose

Turns release metadata into an operationally useful finding.

## Prerequisites and starting position

**Required access or evidence:** [G03 — K05.1](../K05/K05.1.md).

**Starting material:** The readable FieldLink package workspace, interface examples, release records, and an ordinary consumer-check facility.

**Campaign references:** No separately named capability output.

## Completion and downstream use

Produce a correctly bound comparison of package, version, and consumer revision. It must identify the actual disagreement, not just differing text.

## Difficulty and inspiration

A small evidence join involving version and consumer identity.

**Inspiration:** Original challenge for this campaign.
