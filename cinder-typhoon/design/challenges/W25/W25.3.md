## Challenge description — player-facing

Resolve why the supplied practice requests receive acknowledgements without producing the expected outlet movement.

## Story purpose

Makes command feedback accountable to process observations.

## Prerequisites and starting position

**Required access or evidence:** ([W25.1](../W25/W25.1.md) AND [W25.2](../W25/W25.2.md)).

**Starting material:** The documented outlet operating envelope, separate permissive records, and a private practice model with visible observations.

**Campaign references:** No separately named capability output.

## Completion and downstream use

Correctly relate the practice requests, permissives, and observed outcomes. An acknowledgement alone does not establish an actuation.

## Difficulty and inspiration

A bounded state and observation join in the practice model.

**Inspiration:** Original challenge for this campaign.
