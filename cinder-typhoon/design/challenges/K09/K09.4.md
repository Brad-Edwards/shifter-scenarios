## Challenge description — player-facing

Use the compromised job workflow to execute a limited action in its runner context and recover the protected job note.

## Story purpose

Turns build access into a useful scoped execution foothold.

## Prerequisites and starting position

**Required access or evidence:** [K09.3](../K09/K09.3.md).

**Starting material:** The developer's build history, ordinary job interface, artifact references, and a sample FieldLink build.

**Campaign references:** No separately named capability output.

## Completion and downstream use

Demonstrate actual execution and recover the job note. This context enables workload and maintenance branches; it grants no customer execution.

## Difficulty and inspiration

Requires adapting the earned job capability to a separate execution objective.

**Inspiration:** Original challenge for this campaign.
