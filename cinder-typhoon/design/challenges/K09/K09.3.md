## Challenge description — player-facing

Make the test job consume a support input outside the collection its ordinary form allows. Recover the restricted review output it produces.

## Story purpose

Crosses a build-input boundary before attempting execution.

## Prerequisites and starting position

**Required access or evidence:** [K09.2](../K09/K09.2.md).

**Starting material:** The developer's build history, ordinary job interface, artifact references, and a sample FieldLink build.

**Campaign references:** No separately named capability output.

## Completion and downstream use

Produce the protected review output under the intended job identity. This establishes a distinct input capability, not general runner execution.

## Difficulty and inspiration

One application input boundary with a small job/artifact join.

**Inspiration:** Original challenge for this campaign.
