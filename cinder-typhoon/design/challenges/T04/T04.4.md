## Challenge description — player-facing

The training report says the latest outlet test succeeded, but the observations disagree. Determine what actually happened during this attempt and explain the discrepancy through the supplied evidence.

## Story purpose

Ends training with a compact investigation into conflicting process claims.

## Prerequisites and starting position

**Required access or evidence:** the supplied training workspace.

**Starting material:** Small training snapshots, readable field and status legends, a private practice tank, and a separate reporting replay case.

**Campaign references:** No separately named capability output.

## Completion and downstream use

Recover the current attempt's result and the evidence establishing the report's error. This separate replay does not reuse T04.3's result for another score.

## Difficulty and inspiration

A bounded provenance and observation join across a short evidence set.

**Inspiration:** Original challenge for this campaign.

## Technical design

### Surface and normal behavior

A training report records a green test outcome while the same attempt has an
immutable controller request, HMI status capture, and independent observation
trace. The report's author and generation timestamp are ordinary seeded data.

### Vulnerability and intended solution

The reporting job correlates a stale accepted request with the latest test
record, ignoring the later blocked transition. The intended solution is a
timeline reconstruction from the supplied records; T04.3 is neither required
nor sufficient because this card uses a separate attempt.

### Evidence and completion

The completion record names the attempt, actual result, stale source record,
and contradictory observation. The scorer validates their correlation IDs and
timestamps, not a generic claim that the report is wrong.

### Boundaries and reset

All records are read-only training evidence and the report cannot be edited.
Reset removes player conclusions only; it does not rewrite the discrepancy.

### Author checks

Verify report and trace initially disagree, identifiers make the stale join
discoverable, the current attempt has a unique result, an unrelated trace fails,
T04.3's event cannot score this card, and reset is deterministic.
