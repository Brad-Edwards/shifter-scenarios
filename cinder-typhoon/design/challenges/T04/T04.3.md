## Challenge description — player-facing

Move the specified bounded amount of water through the training outlet and verify the transfer from the tank's observations.

## Story purpose

Connects commands and operating conditions to actual effect.

## Prerequisites and starting position

**Required access or evidence:** the supplied training workspace.

**Starting material:** Small training snapshots, readable field and status legends, a private practice tank, and a separate reporting replay case.

**Campaign references:** No separately named capability output.

## Completion and downstream use

Demonstrate the requested transfer and final outlet state within the published bounds. An accepted request alone is insufficient; no campaign authority is granted.

## Difficulty and inspiration

A small documented state-sequence task with visible effects.

**Inspiration:** Original challenge for this campaign.

## Technical design

### Surface and normal behavior

The training HMI drives a deterministic isolated tank-and-outlet practice model.
It exposes a documented request interface, operating bounds, accepted/rejected
command record, and independently published tank observations.

### Vulnerability and intended solution

This is a safe control-verification task, not an authorization bypass. The
intended solution is to clear the condition learned in T04.2 through the normal
practice workflow, request the specified bounded transfer, and validate the
result against observations.

### Evidence and completion

Completion requires an accepted request, final outlet state, and matching
before/after measurements showing the requested transfer within tolerance.
Scoring consumes the independent practice transition rather than the request
receipt alone.

### Boundaries and reset

The practice model is per-player, has fixed water and movement bounds, and
cannot reach any campaign control state. Reset restores the initial tank volume,
mode, and trace history.

### Author checks

Test the block prevents movement, an in-range normal request succeeds, an
out-of-range request is rejected, request-only evidence fails, the observation
delta is correct, and reset returns the initial state.
