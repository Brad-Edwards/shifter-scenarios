## Challenge description — player-facing

Move the specified bounded amount of water through the training outlet and verify the transfer from the tank's observations.

## Story purpose

## Prerequisites and starting position

**Required access or evidence:** the supplied training workspace.

**Starting material:** Small training snapshots, readable field and status legends, a private practice tank, and a separate reporting replay case.

**Campaign references:** No separately named capability output.

## Completion and downstream use

Demonstrate the requested transfer and final outlet state within the published bounds. An accepted request alone is insufficient; no campaign authority is granted.

## Difficulty and inspiration

A small documented state-sequence task with visible effects.
