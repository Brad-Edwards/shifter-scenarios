## Challenge description — player-facing

Make the maintenance renderer accept a contractor-submitted work package for the privileged inspection preview it normally reserves for the maintenance reviewer.

## Story purpose

Crosses an application privilege boundary before execution.

## Prerequisites and starting position

**Required access or evidence:** [W26.1](../W26/W26.1.md).

**Starting material:** The contractor session, earned maintenance approval, the maintenance rendering workflow, and its scoped action interface.

**Campaign references:** No separately named capability output.

## Completion and downstream use

Demonstrate the restricted preview action with the correct inspection and approval binding. Reading the approval or displaying an ordinary contractor preview does not count.

## Difficulty and inspiration

Application-authority composition inspired by apexsurvive's later stages.

**Inspiration:** [apexsurvive (HTB Cyber Apocalypse 2024; Insane)](../../reference-catalog.md#c08). Adapts the later renderer/action portion of apexsurvive; this is separate from K12's complete supplier browser chain.
