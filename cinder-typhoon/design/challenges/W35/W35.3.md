## Challenge description — player-facing

Make the replacement-supply workflow issue the recovered quote's order against the wrong district budget while retaining the quoted supplier, amount, and delivery period.

## Story purpose

Provides a complete local financial ending for a player who stays in corporate ARWC.

## Prerequisites and starting position

**Required access or evidence:** [W35.2](../W35/W35.2.md).

**Starting material:** The corporate procurement catalog, district quote references, and the replacement-supply ordering workflow.

**Campaign references:** No separately named capability output.

## Completion and downstream use

Demonstrate the actual simulated order, the wrong purchasing district, and liability matching the recovered quote. A changed report total or an order for a different quantity does not count; no plant movement is required.

The conclusion shows the issued purchase order and the liability against the wrong district together. Procurement's routine acknowledgement thanks that district for supporting regional resilience, giving the unauthorized charge a dry bureaucratic punchline. The acknowledgement is authored copy after the proven order, not another action or an acceptance phrase. This complete corporate ending introduces replacement-water costs before the reservoir finale, without claiming that a player who stops here has opened the gates.

## Difficulty and inspiration

A separate report-to-purchasing authorization and identity-binding problem.

**Inspiration:** Original challenge for this campaign.
