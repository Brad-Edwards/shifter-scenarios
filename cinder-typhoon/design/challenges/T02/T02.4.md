## Challenge description — player-facing

Your recovered account can list its own assignments. Obtain the other operator's sealed rehearsal brief through the assignment service.

## Story purpose

Introduces authorization beyond successful authentication.

## Prerequisites and starting position

**Required access or evidence:** [T02.3](../T02/T02.3.md).

**Starting material:** The rehearsal identity desk, candidate account notes, a public directory, recovery workflow, and assignment service.

**Campaign references:** No separately named capability output.

## Completion and downstream use

Recover the specified other operator's private brief while retaining the recovered account's identity. Reading its own handover is not new credit.

## Difficulty and inspiration

A bounded ownership and assignment-binding problem.

**Inspiration:** Original challenge for this campaign.

## Technical design

### Surface and normal behavior

The assignment service lists assignments for the recovered training subject and
serves sealed briefs by assignment ID. Each brief has a distinct owner,
rehearsal scope, and immutable document revision.

### Vulnerability and intended solution

The service checks that a caller has any training assignment but does not bind
the requested brief to that caller's assignment. The intended solution is a
record-level authorization test using the session earned in T02.3.

### Evidence and completion

Completion is a read of the specified other operator's brief, recorded with the
recovered subject, requested assignment, and document revision. Reading the
caller's own brief is ordinary behavior and earns no new event.

### Boundaries and reset

Only two synthetic training assignments exist and document access is read-only.
The flaw cannot enumerate arbitrary accounts or cross into KeplerOps. Reset
clears player events and restores the session fixture.

### Author checks

Verify own-assignment access, denial without a recovered session, intended
cross-assignment read, no access to an unknown assignment, exact audit fields,
and repeatable reset.
