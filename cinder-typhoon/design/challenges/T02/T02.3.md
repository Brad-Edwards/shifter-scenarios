## Challenge description — player-facing

Use the identity evidence you recovered to take control of the rehearsal account through its recovery workflow and retrieve its private handover.

## Story purpose

Makes the identity trail produce actual account access.

## Prerequisites and starting position

**Required access or evidence:** ([T02.1](../T02/T02.1.md) AND [T02.2](../T02/T02.2.md)).

**Starting material:** The rehearsal identity desk, candidate account notes, a public directory, recovery workflow, and assignment service.

**Campaign references:** No separately named capability output.

## Completion and downstream use

Demonstrate the recovered identity and obtain its private handover. A recovery acknowledgement without usable access is insufficient. The session enables T02.4.

## Difficulty and inspiration

One explicit recovery workflow after two short evidence tasks.

**Inspiration:** Original challenge for this campaign.

## Technical design

### Surface and normal behavior

The training recovery workflow verifies an account, case reference, and a
staff-detail value before issuing a one-use recovery session. The recovered
account owns a private handover document in the identity desk.

### Vulnerability and intended solution

The workflow accepts independently observable identity attributes as if they
were proof of account ownership. The intended solution combines the exact
outputs of T02.1 and T02.2 in the normal recovery flow; neither alone is
sufficient.

### Evidence and completion

A successful recovery creates a player-scoped session tied to the rehearsal
subject and a handover-read event. Scoring requires both session issuance and
the private handover retrieval, then exposes the session for T02.4.

### Boundaries and reset

The session can view only this training account and expires rapidly. It cannot
change recovery factors, password, directory records, or any non-training
application. Reset revokes the session and restores case state.

### Author checks

Test each factor separately fails, the intended three-record join succeeds,
the resulting session reads only its own handover, T02.4 receives the expected
identity, and reset invalidates the session.
