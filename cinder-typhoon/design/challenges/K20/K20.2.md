## Challenge description — player-facing

Obtain the managed service capability needed by release administration despite the service's separate access boundary.

## Story purpose

Creates a substantive new identity capability before final impersonation.

## Prerequisites and starting position

**Required access or evidence:** [K20.1](../K20/K20.1.md).

**Starting material:** The delegated staff context, service relationship records, and the release-administration workflow.

**Campaign references:** No separately named capability output.

## Completion and downstream use

Demonstrate the managed identity against its protected administrative resource. Possessing unrelated staff credentials is insufficient.

## Difficulty and inspiration

Requires resolving managed-service authority within the inherited identity constraints.

**Inspiration:** [University (HTB retired machine; Insane; official synopsis)](../../reference-catalog.md#c10). Adapts University's later delegation, managed-service, and impersonation progression; source coverage is the cataloged official synopsis.
