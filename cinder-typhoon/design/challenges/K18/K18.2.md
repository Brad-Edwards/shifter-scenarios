## Challenge description — player-facing

Make the privileged note reviewer open the identity-provider administration view and recover its restricted support-application registration record.

## Story purpose

Turns a support-content foothold into a different application's authority.

## Prerequisites and starting position

**Required access or evidence:** [K18.1](../K18/K18.1.md).

**Starting material:** The support-note feature, ordinary identity-provider material, and a controlled note-review workflow.

**Campaign references:** No separately named capability output.

## Completion and downstream use

Recover the current support-application registration through the privileged review context. This establishes access to the registration, not permission to change its federation policy; that is K18.3.

## Difficulty and inspiration

Cross-application content and identity binding inspired by SOS or SSO?.

**Inspiration:** [SOS or SSO? (HTB Business CTF 2024; Hard)](../../reference-catalog.md#c18). Adapts SOS or SSO?'s support-content, identity, and backend-data composition.
