## Challenge description — player-facing

Reconstruct enough of the compiler's internal instruction behavior to reproduce the supplied policy fragments.

## Story purpose

Establishes a reusable understanding of the policy language.

## Prerequisites and starting position

**Required access or evidence:** [K11.1](../K11/K11.1.md).

**Starting material:** The retained FieldLink rules-compiler artifact, example policy inputs, and its acceptance interface.

**Campaign references:** No separately named capability output.

## Completion and downstream use

Produce matching results for the designated fragments, including their state effects. Naming instruction families alone is insufficient.

## Difficulty and inspiration

Compile-time virtual-machine interpretation inspired by Metagaming.

**Inspiration:** [Metagaming (HTB Cyber Apocalypse 2024; Hard)](../../reference-catalog.md#c07). Adapts Metagaming's compile-time program-analysis challenge to package policy.
