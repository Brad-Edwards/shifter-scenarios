## Challenge description — player-facing

The indexer still restricts what your corrupted object can influence. Establish a controlled write capability under those restrictions and demonstrate it against the designated protected state.

## Story purpose

Provides a substantive elite milestone before final execution.

## Prerequisites and starting position

**Required access or evidence:** [K04.2](../K04/K04.2.md).

**Starting material:** The native support-bundle indexer, source or analysis material, a local reproducer, and normal bundle examples.

**Campaign references:** No separately named capability output.

## Completion and downstream use

Demonstrate the scoped write capability while the indexer remains usable. The prior disclosure or another crash does not satisfy this achievement.

## Difficulty and inspiration

Retains the demanding allocator-control transition from Gloater; public solutions still require adaptation to this instance.

**Inspiration:** [Gloater (HTB Cyber Apocalypse 2024; Insane)](../../reference-catalog.md#c03). Retains Gloater's constrained native-exploitation progression in a support-bundle service.
