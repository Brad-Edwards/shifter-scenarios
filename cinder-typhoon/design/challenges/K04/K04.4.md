## Challenge description — player-facing

Turn the indexer compromise into execution under its service identity. Recover the release-exception queue the support engineer has been trying to clear.

## Completion and downstream use

## Difficulty and inspiration

A constrained final execution transition with convenient targets unavailable, directly inspired by Gloater.

**Inspiration:** [Gloater (HTB Cyber Apocalypse 2024; Insane)](../../reference-catalog.md#c03). Retains Gloater's constrained native-exploitation progression in a support-bundle service.

## Technical design

### Surface and normal behavior
The support indexer processes release-exception entries under a restricted service identity and holds an encrypted queue document available only through an internal completion routine.

### Vulnerability and intended solution
The intended solution turns K04.3's constrained control into invocation of the card's designated internal routine with valid queue arguments. It must demonstrate execution in the indexer context, not merely change a completion flag.

### Evidence and completion
The daemon logs the internal routine, service identity, queue record revision, and recovered exception dossier; that dossier is an alternative K29 lead.

### Boundaries and reset
The invoked routine can read only the one queue document and cannot start commands, persist code, or reach CI, cloud, or ARWC. Reset recreates its per-player daemon and queue state.

### Author checks
Verify queue access is denied normally, invalid routine arguments fail, intended invocation produces one audited dossier, no process escape exists, downstream dossier format is valid, and reset is clean.
