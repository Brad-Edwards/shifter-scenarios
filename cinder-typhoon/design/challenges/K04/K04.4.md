## Challenge description — player-facing

Turn the indexer compromise into execution under its service identity. Recover the release-exception queue the support engineer has been trying to clear.

## Story purpose

Completes the native branch with useful release intelligence.

## Prerequisites and starting position

**Required access or evidence:** [K04.3](../K04/K04.3.md).

**Starting material:** The native support-bundle indexer, source or analysis material, a local reproducer, and normal bundle examples.

**Campaign references:** No separately named capability output.

## Completion and downstream use

Demonstrate execution in the indexer's actual context and recover the protected exception queue. It is an alternative K29 dossier lead, not cloud authority or ARWC access.

## Difficulty and inspiration
