## Challenge description — player-facing

Construct a sequencer experiment that distinguishes the specified protected conditions through its measurements. Establish an interpretation that holds across the supplied reference cases.

## Story purpose

Turns a narrow observation flaw into a usable experimental capability.

## Prerequisites and starting position

**Required access or evidence:** [W16.2](../W16/W16.2.md).

**Starting material:** A retained diagnostic instrument artifact, its sequencer interface, ordinary observation examples, and protected calibration references.

**Campaign references:** No separately named capability output.

## Completion and downstream use

Demonstrate correct discrimination of the designated independent conditions. Raw timing differences or repeated ordinary sequences are insufficient.

## Difficulty and inspiration

Experimental construction and signal interpretation under the instrument's sequencing constraints.

**Inspiration:** [Spectrel Imaging (Hack-a-Sat 2023; relative 5/5)](../../reference-catalog.md#c14). Retains Spectrel Imaging's constrained experimental observation and protected-data recovery challenge.
