## Challenge description — player-facing

The training dispatch site removed its rehearsal page from the menu. Find the page and recover the delivery amendment the public notice omits.

## Story purpose

Introduces a separate web discovery with useful information.

## Prerequisites and starting position

**Required access or evidence:** the supplied training workspace.

**Starting material:** The supplied training workspace and dispatch site. The site's ordinary, accessible page material retains a reference to the withdrawn rehearsal material; following that evidence leads to the amendment. Inspecting the reference requires no other challenge completion. The retired courier-client material and diagnostic helper are also available for the other training objectives.

**Campaign references:** No separately named capability output.

## Completion and downstream use

Recover the actual unpublished amendment and its delivery binding. Finding an address alone does not count.

## Difficulty and inspiration

A short web investigation following an inspectable reference. The page's location is recoverable from the supplied site; inventing a path or getting lucky with a wordlist is not required. The reference is ordinary starting evidence, not a hint to unlock later.

**Inspiration:** Original challenge for this campaign.

## Technical design

### Surface and normal behavior

The training dispatch site is a small authored read-only web application. Its
public delivery notice names a rehearsal shipment; its static site manifest,
retired-content index, and ordinary linked assets retain a specific amendment
that the navigation no longer presents.

### Vulnerability and intended solution

Removal from navigation incorrectly substitutes for removal from the site's
published content inventory. The intended solution is evidence-led discovery
from the notice and published site metadata, followed by retrieving and
validating the actual amendment rather than enumerating arbitrary paths.

### Evidence and completion

The amendment carries a delivery binding that matches the public notice. A
server-side retrieval event and the amendment's immutable record ID establish
completion; an address or a guessed identifier alone does not.

### Boundaries and reset

Only the fixed rehearsal content namespace is exposed. The site has no write
route, and reset clears the player event while leaving the published inventory
unchanged.

### Author checks

Check that the notice supplies a fair content lead, metadata names one relevant
retired item, the amendment matches the delivery record, random path scans do
not create completion, and the expected retrieval is auditable.
