# People and relationships

Fifteen recurring people make up this draft: six KeplerOps employees, eight
ARWC employees, and one Veybridge contractor. They vary in how much of
themselves they bring to work. This is a connected slice of the organizations,
not their entire population.

The ten inherited actor IDs identify established characters. The five people
without those IDs are narrative additions. Organizational reporting is described
by function; their names and relationships do not assign technical permissions.

## KeplerOps

### Rowan Ito — FieldKest developer (`K-DEV-01`)

Rowan joined in 2018, when a durable product was beginning to emerge from
consulting work. They enjoy the moment a messy problem becomes understandable
and are particularly pleased when another engineer adopts their explanation.
They want a long technical career, with more influence over the product and
without becoming a people manager.

Their generosity comes with enthusiasm that can outrun the request. Maya asks
for an afternoon's help and receives three possible improvements; Rowan is
surprised when choosing among them creates more work. They can also become
attached to an elegant idea after the practical case for it has weakened.
Being told plainly what would actually help usually brings them back.

Rowan and Maya are friends as well as colleagues. Rowan recently repaired Maya's
bicycle, after being reminded that she wanted it back for Monday. Their proposed
improvements can wait. Rowan likes making
things with their hands partly because the results leave the screen.

**Voice and manner:** brief at first, then expansive once interested. Rowan
adds examples and sometimes a second message revising the first. With friends,
their self-mockery usually arrives after they recognize they have overdone it.

### Maya Ranscombe — customer support engineer (`K-SUP-01`)

Maya joined in 2019 and owns customer relationships as well as individual
cases, including ARWC. She remembers the person behind an old conversation and
can make a worried customer feel that somebody has properly heard them. She
likes this skill being recognized as expertise.

She is considering a first team-lead role. Earlier this year she chose not to
apply for an internal opening and was surprised by her disappointment when it
was filled. She congratulated the successful colleague sincerely. Since then,
she has begun discussing her next step with her manager instead of waiting to
feel completely ready.

Maya helped Talia settle into KeplerOps. She is proud of Talia's confidence and
occasionally offers advice Talia no longer needs. With Rowan she is warmer and
more direct than in customer correspondence. She protects her time for family
visits; a recent agreed on-call swap let her attend her sister's birthday dinner.

**Voice and manner:** remembers an earlier detail, asks a direct question, and
can tell a short story well. Her public patience has limits: in a familiar
thread she admits irritation, then decides what to do about it. She does not
turn every conversation into emotional support for somebody else.

### Evan Calderoux — release engineer (`K-REL-01`)

Evan joined in 2019. They enjoy being the person who can say something is
ready, and like ceremonies that mark work as complete: a short release meeting,
a shared meal, a photograph when a long project finishes. Their attention to
versions and acceptance coexists with a sentimental attachment to occasions.

They want to develop the release function without losing the hands-on work.
They are less comfortable delegating a familiar task than they admit. Rowan
sometimes finds their meeting preparation elaborate; Evan finds Rowan's
continuing discussion after a decision exhausting. They can admire each other's
work without becoming close friends.

Evan and Noor shared a house from 2019 to 2021 and have remained friends.
Their regular lunch matters to Evan as a continuation of that friendship. They
initially took Noor's recent cancellations more personally than Noor understood.
Outside work, Evan helps organize a small amateur film discussion group and is
happiest when someone recommends something unexpected.

**Voice and manner:** complete sentences, deliberate openings and endings,
quietly theatrical enthusiasm about a successful result. Messages to Noor can
be leisurely and full of tangents; release notes remain concise.

### Noor Aveling — platform and workload operator (`K-SRE-01`)

Noor joined in 2020 after working for another software employer. They like
KeplerOps' manageable scale and the fact that a sensible maintenance proposal
can reach someone empowered to fund it. They want better recognition for work
whose success is that other people never had to think about it.

Noor dislikes unnecessary meetings and is quick to offer a practical answer.
Their impatience sometimes makes them answer the easiest version of a colleague's
question instead of asking why it matters. They are much better at noticing
this when the colleague says so directly.

They kept Evan's lunch invitation in the category of a convenient routine long
after Evan had put it in the category of an important friendship. Noor cares
about Evan and was embarrassed to discover the difference. They also enjoy
unplanned afternoons and have recently taken up the clarinet. They like playing
with other beginners and are openly pleased by small improvements; at home,
being good at something immediately matters less to them than colleagues might
expect.

**Voice and manner:** short questions, blunt first drafts, understated warmth.
Noor often shows affection by making something easier for a friend, but can
learn that the friend wanted company rather than a solution. They can be quiet
without being unhappy or secretly disapproving.

### Talia Mornac — support quality reviewer (`K-REV-01`)

Talia joined support in 2023 with experience from another workplace. Her job
now centres on the quality of support reviews and on helping colleagues learn
from completed cases. She helped introduce the support assistant and enjoys
finding an improvement that makes a repetitive task less tedious.

She likes being prepared and can rehearse a short presentation far beyond what
it needs. Her next ambition is to lead a customer workshop herself. She wants
Maya's confidence in her, but would prefer Maya to stop supplying a helpful
alternative every time she explains her plan.

Talia is sociable in small groups, more reserved in a large meeting, and quicker
to make a dry observation once she knows who is listening. She updates the
new-starter guide because she remembers needing it. Her favourite correction
removed directions to a meeting room that had become storage; the useful outcome
was newcomers finding the right place, and the joke has mostly run its course.

**Voice and manner:** carefully organized professional writing; longer, more
playful exchanges with friends. She sometimes qualifies a perfectly good idea
before anyone has objected. When she finally asks Maya to let her try something
her own way, the request is polite but unambiguous.

### Leah Calder-Voss — founder and managing director

Leah founded KeplerOps in 2016 and remains its controlling owner. She wants a
profitable company that can still be a worthwhile employer ten years from now.
She likes customers recognizing individual staff, and knows enough about the
work to ask useful questions without doing everybody's job.

She also enjoys being needed. When someone makes a decision she has delegated,
she can suggest improvements before remembering that the decision was theirs.
She is working on that habit and occasionally makes the joke about herself
before anyone else does. Her ambition for independence inside the company is
sincere even when practising it feels uncomfortable.

Leah remembers early customer journeys more fondly than everyone who accompanied
her. For the tenth-anniversary gathering she initially imagined telling the
company's story herself. A colleague's suggestion to collect memories from
different hiring years pleased her, then made her briefly nervous about what
she would hear. She has a busy life outside KeplerOps and wants to take a proper
holiday without checking in to prove she remains useful.

**Voice and manner:** energetic, conversational, prone to explaining a decision
through something she remembers. She asks about a colleague's plans and usually
remembers the answer. Formal commercial writing is more restrained.

## ARWC

### Theo Marrivel — maintenance planner (`A-MAINT-01`)

Theo coordinates people, visits, materials, and work packages across a crowded
maintenance calendar. He enjoys getting an awkward week to fit together and
being able to tell a colleague that a practical nuisance has been sorted out.
He would like more say in planning improvements while keeping regular contact
with crews and contractors.

He can be impatient with an answer that lists difficulties without proposing
what to do next. He also volunteers to help before checking his own diary,
then grumbles about how much he has agreed to do. Clara can tell him he is
rushing; he is much more likely to accept it from her than from a vague group
reminder.

Theo and Mina have known each other for years and enjoy an intermittent rivalry
in the voluntary staff photography competition. Theo claims participation is
what matters and then asks remarkably specific questions about the judging.
He helps organize the staff walk, but some friends know him mainly as someone
who takes rather good photographs and is always suggesting another short stop.

**Voice and manner:** brisk with practical requests, anecdotal in familiar
conversation. His humour includes his own habits. He is capable of a simple
thank-you without attaching another request.

### Clara Morivelle — controls engineer (`A-CTRL-01`)

Clara joined ARWC in 2014. She likes the physical continuity of the work: an
improvement she helped make can still be useful years later. She wants to
develop as a senior specialist and teach some of what she knows, while keeping
time for engineering rather than filling her calendar with coordination.

She is precise about conclusions at work and much less economical when a friend
asks for travel suggestions. A request for one pleasant walk can produce an
enthusiastic account of several routes, lunch possibilities, and what she
particularly liked about each. Nadia appreciates the enthusiasm but sometimes
wishes Clara would let a recommendation remain a recommendation.

Mina helped Clara understand the practical routines around the plant when she
was new. They now consult each other as experienced colleagues; Clara still
remembers that initial kindness. She and Theo respect each other without
sharing all the same social circles.

**Voice and manner:** direct technical prose, patient explanation when invited,
visible delight about personal interests. When rushed, Clara can sound as
though the limits of her answer should have been obvious. She can apologize
for the tone without retracting a necessary qualification.

### Nadia Corvane — regional water planner (`A-PLAN-01`)

Nadia enjoys making a complicated regional choice understandable. She works
with engineering, operations, and business services, and wants to become better
at public explanation without losing the substance behind it. She is pleased
when someone outside planning can explain her recommendation back to her.

Her useful instinct to organize a problem can be tiring in a friendship.
Priya occasionally describes an annoying day and receives a thoughtful plan
when she wanted sympathetic company. Nadia is learning to ask first. She also
tends to overprepare for small social arrangements because she wants everyone
to enjoy themselves.

Nadia and Priya became friends through a community choir before they began
working closely at ARWC. They have shared meals and ordinary weekends that
have nothing to do with their departments. Clara is another friend, though
Nadia would not choose Clara to plan every day of a holiday. Nadia is content
to have different kinds of friendship with different people.

**Voice and manner:** uses comparisons and examples, readily admits when
someone's explanation is better than hers. In personal conversation she is
warmer and more speculative. Rosa's ability to make one point land has become
something she wants to learn from rather than simply supply facts to.

### Priya Naravel — district procurement officer (`A-PROC-01`)

Priya works in business services and looks after purchasing matters across the
two district budgets. She likes bringing a negotiation to a clear agreement,
and appreciates suppliers who remember what was actually agreed. She wants to
take on more substantial contract work without becoming the default owner of
every administrative question that lacks an obvious home.

She is proud of being organized but has a drawer of personal correspondence
she keeps intending to sort. At work she can become stubborn about a small
wording improvement after its practical value has stopped increasing. Rosa is
someone she trusts to say that a revised explanation is now good enough.

Priya enjoys Nadia's company and would like to be allowed an occasional complaint
that does not become a project. Their friendship is sufficiently established
for her to say this plainly. She sings with the community choir for pleasure
and rarely volunteers for its committee; she already administers enough things.

**Voice and manner:** clear requests, economical dry humour, more expansive
when recounting a small absurdity to a friend. She can enjoy a beautifully
worded formal letter and still prefer the person who answers a question simply.

### Luc Venn-Morel — director of regional operations

Luc reports to ARWC's general manager. He takes pride in representing work he
understands and wants the forthcoming renewal programme to leave the next
generation a service it can maintain. He is willing to carry an uncomfortable
recommendation into a public discussion when staff have explained its basis.

He likes marking achievements formally. Some staff appreciate this; others
would prefer a quiet thanks. He sometimes mistakes reluctance to give a speech
for lack of confidence, rather than a straightforward preference. Mina has
helped him see that distinction without making it a confrontation.

Luc is approaching a stage of his career where succession has become a personal
question, although he has set no retirement date. He enjoys being useful and
is curious about what a less scheduled life might contain. He has recently
started taking longer walks without tracking distance and is mildly surprised
by how often he still checks the time.

**Voice and manner:** measured in formal writing, more reflective one to one.
He can tell a long story when relaxed. His colleagues are permitted to respect
him without finding every story equally interesting.

### Mina Selverin — senior water treatment operator

Mina joined Pine's water service in 2007 and now works within ARWC's operations
function, reporting to a shift supervisor. She enjoys the practical competence
of a good crew and is proud of her part in the 2021–2023 refurbishment. She
wants time allocated properly for the teaching she is increasingly asked to do.

She is a patient instructor when someone is learning, less patient when a
meeting repeats something settled last week. Long familiarity can make her
forget which local references a newcomer will not understand. Owen's questions
have helped her notice that habit. She gives credit readily and prefers it to
be equally specific when directed at her.

Mina knows Clara from Clara's early years at ARWC and competes amiably with
Theo in the staff photography event. She enjoys gardening at home without
wanting every colleague's advice about it. She likes her job and has plans for
life after it, but is tired of people assuming that a long-serving employee
must already be counting down to retirement.

**Voice and manner:** concrete observations, understated jokes, comfortable
pauses. Her relaxed break-room conversation differs from her formal shift
records. She does not need to explain the whole utility whenever she appears.

### Owen Vardell — water distribution apprentice

Owen joined the 2025 intake after working in another practical trade. They are
an adult changing direction, with useful skills and substantial new material
to learn. Their supervisor arranges rotations; Mina teaches part of their plant
orientation, while distribution instructors oversee their ordinary field learning.

Owen wants to become dependable enough to be given a task without a long preamble.
They sometimes prepare an elaborate answer to avoid appearing inexperienced.
They still ask when uncertain; the personal difficulty is accepting that a
short, honest question can make a better impression than displaying everything
learned so far.

They are good at explaining an unfamiliar thing to someone seeing it for the
first time. Rosa noticed this during preparation for an outreach session.
Owen enjoys taking photographs with an ordinary phone and entered the staff
competition after Theo encouraged them. They have friends outside the company
and do not expect work to supply their entire social life.

**Voice and manner:** explanatory when nervous, more concise as they settle
in. Owen enjoys a joke but does not serve as the permanent innocent newcomer
for other characters to instruct. They also notice things others have missed.

### Rosa Nerivet — customer-service adviser

Rosa joined in 2018 and reports to the customer-service team supervisor. She
handles account and meter enquiries and helps maintain the team's explanations
of common questions. She likes finding the sentence that lets somebody
understand a bill without needing a tour of ARWC's internal terminology.

She wants more responsibility for customer communications while continuing to
speak to customers. She can be impatient with a technically comprehensive
explanation that never answers the opening question. Her own blind spot is
assuming an explanation that worked beautifully once will suit the next person.
She listens well enough to adjust when it does not.

Rosa grew up in River, spent several years elsewhere, and returned partly to
live nearer family. She knows Priya through work and has begun to enjoy Nadia's
company during their writing sessions. Neither relationship is yet a lifelong
friendship. She is enthusiastic about community events but occasionally decides
she would rather spend a free evening at home.

**Voice and manner:** conversational, lightly humorous, good at a concrete
example. She can finish a discussion decisively. A customer's gratitude means
something to her, though she does not expect every caller to provide it.

## Veybridge

### Jules Orswick — field specialist (`A-FIELD-01`)

Jules works for Veybridge Controls and Inspection, reporting through its field
service manager. They have visited ARWC sites for years and like a customer
whose crews take an interest in the practical work. They are also glad to get
home after a week of travel and to see people who do not want to discuss equipment.

They want to spend part of their time training newer Veybridge staff. They are
less keen on the permanent office role that colleagues sometimes assume is the
next step. Jules can be impatient with a late scheduling change, especially
when they have already adjusted personal plans, and have learned to state that
cost before agreeing to another favour.

Theo is a familiar professional contact; their easy conversation includes
photographs and local walks. Jules and Clara value each other's expertise.
These relationships have warmth without making Jules an honorary ARWC employee
or turning a purchasing decision into a personal favour.

**Voice and manner:** short practical messages while travelling; more talkative
when time permits. They tell a good account of a small inconvenience and know
when an old work story has already been heard.

| Rosa and Nadia | Rosa brings practical understanding of customers' questions. | Nadia brings the reasoning behind a decision. | A newer collegial relationship beginning to extend beyond its original purpose. |
| Mina and Owen | Mina brings long experience and local shorthand. | Owen brings prior trade experience and a newcomer's useful questions. | A teaching relationship with room for mutual learning, within their ordinary roles. |

Rowan and Evan are respected colleagues rather than close friends. Luc's place
in the organization affects how staff speak to him; he is not automatically
part of their private conversations. Jules has a separate employer and social
life. Other employees can be friendly without joining this particular set of
relationships. The background workforce includes laboratory staff, ordinary
IT, finance, commercial colleagues, and crews with their own friendships and
interests; later incidental appearances need only the detail appropriate to them.
