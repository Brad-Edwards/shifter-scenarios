# Maintenance and engineering voices

Author-only guidance for people with eight or more messages in issue 124. Sample
IDs refer to `assets/narrative/authoring/mail-maintenance-engineering.yaml`.
These notes and excerpts are not deployed to the workplace.

| Person | Working concern and writing habit | Finished sample |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Clara Morivelle, controls engineer | Names the record she checked and refuses to let a useful working revision become authority by implication. Precise, calm, comfortable with “not yet.” | `me-drawing-thread-01-p1-2`: “Not approved for work yet. Keep this beside the comments, not in the effective drawing set.” |

Other maintenance and engineering colleagues send one to four finished work
updates apiece. Their work-order language follows the roster's context and does
not promote them into recurring narrators merely to increase message volume.
