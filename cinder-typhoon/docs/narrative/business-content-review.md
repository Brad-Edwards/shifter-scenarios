# Customer and supplier reference review: issue 113

Author-only record. Opening state: 08:30 on 16 September 2026, UTC−04:00.

## Before authoring

The accepted workforce slice supplies 64 KeplerOps and 230 ARWC employees, with
commercial, procurement, support, and operations owners already present. It also
supplies 1,475 logical messages and 155 documents. External correspondents are
not employees and must not receive company directory rows or private workplace
collections. The existing mail package retains only copies held by KeplerOps or
ARWC; a cross-company message has one logical identity.

The company history says FieldKest became a supported product in 2018–2019 and
KeplerOps has roughly a dozen substantial utility customers. ARWC is a long-term
product customer, while Rillhaven is a separate heating utility customer whose
seasonal handover is complete by the snapshot. The ten additional accounts need
their own work and contract terms. They cannot turn KeplerOps into a subscription
business or imply that FieldKest controls plant equipment. ARWC remains a
contract customer of Merewick's finite bulk supply, and Talvern receives only a
supplementary seasonal allowance. Belvarn is a business service account, not a
residential customer population.

The adversarial pass found five specific risks in the proposed slice:

1. A generic `supplier` template would make Merewick capacity, Ardenvale
   calibration, and Ternwick records work sound interchangeable. Each therefore
   needs its own scope, document form, and correspondence vocabulary.
2. A shared account register could leak private negotiation notes to an ordinary
   document reader. Customer-facing and signed terms contain only agreed scope,
   dates, rates, and signatories; internal contact reviews have exact readers.
3. A new contact entered in the staff roster would imply employment or access.
   External people stay in correspondent identities and in reference records.
4. Newly invented contract dates could predate KeplerOps' product or contradict
   the completed Rillhaven handover. Every effective date and delivered event is
   checked against the snapshot and the existing history.
5. Large-scale mail authoring can produce repeated boilerplate or count copied
   mail as separate messages. The review will sample across organizations and
   media, measure repeated bodies, and count logical messages separately from
   retained copies.

The ten proposed additional utility organization names were searched as exact
phrases on 20 September 2026, with no exact company result returned. All new
recurring contact names were searched as quoted full names. Exact matches for
Ruth Danver, Sylvie Corven, Reece Fenner, and Mabel Dornin were replaced with
Ruth Varendon, Sylvie Dorswick, Reece Fendell, and Mabel Dornell, then checked
again. This is a collision screen, not an assertion that a name is globally
unique. No real biography or company data is used.
