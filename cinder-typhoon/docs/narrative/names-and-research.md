# Names, collision screening, and research

Checked on **19 September 2026**. KeplerOps Software and Alterra Regional Water
Company are retained by explicit author instruction. The author confirmed
ownership of **keplerops.com** and **alterrawaterco.com**, and explicitly
authorized both for scenario use. They are the canonical company domains for
future content. The fictional KeplerOps history and employees in this directory
do not describe its real business.

## What the screening establishes

Each proposed supporting organization's full name, the new product and site
names, and each proposed character's full name were searched as quoted phrases.
Returned results were inspected for an exact entity match. Some queries returned
no results; others returned unrelated words, surnames, places, or partial names.
Those are distinguished below.

The accurate claim is **no exact matching entity was identified in the returned
results**, not proof that nobody anywhere has ever used a name. This is an
editorial collision screen, not a worldwide registry or trademark clearance.
Characters are invented; no real person's biography, portrait, employer,
contact information, or correspondence is used to build them. Recheck names
before public assets are finalized, particularly if a proposed name changes.

## Naming alignment during asset authoring

The worldbuilding-only draft left technical material intact. The subsequent
asset pass aligned display names and personal login spellings in active SDL,
challenge cards, and the technical data contract using the table above. Stable
actor and business identifiers remain unchanged. Lowercase product package,
service, and internal dataset identifiers are retained technical identifiers.
Historical design documents may still contain earlier authoring labels.

For subsequent additions and changes:

- Update human-facing product, site, and character names across their owning
  documents and future assets.
- Retain stable business and actor identifiers unless a separate technical
  requirement justifies a change. `FLK` can remain the product's internal record
  prefix; `CRR` still describes Cairn Reach Reservoir.
- Review any old product name embedded in a future package or path explicitly,
  rather than assuming that changing prose has renamed a technical object.
- Update usernames and identity references together if they are changed. A
  display-name change must not split one person into two accounts accidentally.

The replacement names are not a fictional rebranding event. Do not fill inboxes
with explanations for why every employee changed their surname, or make the
old labels clues in the story. They are simply revisions to the authoring canon.

## Research used for plausibility

The business and personal stories are original. A small set of primary sources
informed the kinds of ordinary work the environment should contain:

| Source | What informed this draft | What was not imported |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Harvey Smith and Matthias Worch, *What Happened Here? Environmental Storytelling*, GDC 2010](https://www.gdcvault.com/play/1012696/What-Happened-Here-Environmental) | The environment can carry narrative through what the player finds and through reactions to their actions. Applying that principle to retained enterprise work is this draft's design choice. | A copied game plot, character, asset, or claim that this source prescribes mailbox counts. |
| [Halifax Water, publications and reports](https://www.halifaxwater.ca/publications-reports) | A utility's documentary life includes business plans, budgets, annual reporting, governance, and customer-facing material. | Its people, finances, incidents, geography, documents, or operational design as fictional victim data. |

adversarial review; the second draft makes its own worldbuilding decisions.
