Accounts notices use regular structures because they record payments, balances,
arrangements, appointments, adjustments, copy dispatch, or receipt of a case.
That consistency belongs to the automated medium. Customer messages range from
one practical question to multi-step clarification; the account and invoice
facts supply variation without assigning personality or proficiency from a name.
