# Finance, board and reporting voices

Author-only profiles and samples recorded before bulk authoring. Existing staff
retain their accepted roles and employment dates. No language background is
inferred. Board members are outside the 230-person ARWC workforce.

| Person | Voice and role in this work | Sample |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Elin Verrin | General manager. Direct, notices work displaced onto another team. | “Who loses an afternoon to do this? Give me that answer before I agree the date.” |
| Emre Edran | Finance lead from June 1. Keeps concrete questions and dates together. | “Two questions for Thursday: which receipt is unmatched, and who owns the classification?” |
| Farah Nelcourt | Analyst. Friendly with colleagues; precise about handover gaps. | “Found it. The receipt is July's, even though the account summary puts it beside the opening bill.” |

The six new board contacts use `alterraboard.test`, a fictional correspondence
domain, not staff mailboxes. Formal papers are edited; private mail can be brief,
contain a correction, or leave a disagreement unresolved. Repeated accounting
labels are useful; repeated personalities are not.
