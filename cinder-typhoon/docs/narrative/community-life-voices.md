# ARWC community life voices

Author-only notes prepared before the new correspondence. The accepted
profiles in [people.md](people.md), workforce notes and departmental reviews
remain authoritative. No new employee or external speaker is introduced.

| Speaker | Audience and habit | Sample |

Mina is direct with experienced colleagues and willing to ask for preparation
time. Owen's enthusiasm can outrun his sentence; a correction should concern
what he meant, not turn him into a comic apprentice. Theo's private interest in
judging does not belong in the results notice. Clara can let a walking reply
become longer than its original question. Luc can accept a small occasion
without supplying a speech. Rosa starts with the reader's question. Nadia
and Priya can disagree or leave weekend plans undecided without a closing lesson.

Other supervisors retain their accepted departmental samples. Routine calendar
acknowledgements use a compact, repeatable form. Human invitations, decisions,
posts and private replies carry the actual subject of the exchange. Mistakes
are occasional and corrected in context; names imply no language proficiency.
