# ARWC laboratory and quality voices

Author-only writing guidance for issue 125. The roster already gives each person
a context and writing note; these samples record how those profiles appear in the
finished laboratory, quality and reporting correspondence. None of this guidance
is materialized into the workplace.

The result is deliberately uneven across media. Report notices repeat controlled
terms; service requests use equipment and dates; review threads preserve prior
runs; colleague notes can be fragmentary or gently funny. Names do not determine
fluency, and no laboratory colleague is written as a stereotype or merely as a
source of numbers for Nadia or operations.
