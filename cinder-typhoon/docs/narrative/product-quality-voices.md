# Product management and quality voices

Author-only writing guide for the eight recurring staff in the product-quality
corpus. Workforce records remain the source of identity and reporting facts;
these samples show how those profiles appear in current correspondence.

| Person | Working concern and voice | Exact sample |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Jory Wellard (`kwm039`) | Wants evidence and an explicit record before a status changes. Concrete, little padding. | `pq-0011`: “Before I record that, show me the compatibility evidence and the remaining limitation.” |
| Kaisa Fennard (`kwm040`) | Notices downstream work and asks colleagues to flag the support handover. Friendly but bounded. | `pq-0017`: “I have opened the product record from the engineering note.” |
| Kiran Orvel (`kwm041`) | Asks one question at a time and corrects shorthand when a window sounds booked. | `pq-0033`: “The proposed window is June compatibility review; it is not a commitment.” |
| Lara Velswick (`kwm042`) | Terse on routine evidence; expands when an exception could be mistaken for acceptance. | `pq-0004`: “One is deliberately about the absent optional field; the happy path alone is not enough.” |
| Leila Ferrell (`kwm043`) | Uses plain language and makes the next evidence action visible. | `pq-0020`: “I wrote five checks.” |
| Lena Parnell (`kwm044`) | Prefers a practical recorded result to a sweeping conclusion. | `pq-0036`: “The happy path alone is not enough.” |
| Leo Yervan (`kwm045`) | Short, direct quality notes; adds context only at the decision boundary. | `pq-0052`: “One is deliberately about the absent optional field.” |
| Lila Garsen (`kwm046`) | Checks the finish before calling work complete and retains open limitations. | `pq-0068`: “I wrote five checks.” |

The shared phrases above occur where the team is using the same acceptance
practice. Their surrounding messages retain different rhythm, follow-up habits,
and attention: downstream workload for Kaisa, correction for Kiran, concise
evidence boundaries for Lara and Leo, teaching clarity for Leila, practical
records for Lena, and completion detail for Lila.
