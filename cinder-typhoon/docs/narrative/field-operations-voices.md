# Field operations voices

Author-only additions to the accepted workforce writing notes. Mina and Owen
retain their profiles in [people.md](people.md); existing roster notes govern
the other operators. Checked before composing the new correspondence.

| Speaker | Medium and manner | Short sample |
| --- | --- | --- |
