# KeplerOps and Alterra: the enterprise world

Worldbuilding draft 2, 19 September 2026, with the workforce content slice added
on 20 September. These documents establish two fictional companies, the region
around them, and the people who work there. The authored records are in
`assets/narrative/`.

**KeplerOps Software** is a successful specialist deciding what kind of company
it wants to become. Early employees remember making a living by being
exceptionally useful to a few customers. Newer colleagues want careers that
do not depend on becoming the next indispensable person. Both groups have
reasons to like the place, and different ideas about what growing up means.

**Alterra Regional Water Company** is a regional employer whose history runs
through its staff, customers, and neighbourhoods. It has brought two water
services together, completed projects worth being proud of, and developed a
generation of employees who barely remember the separate organizations. Its
next task is renewing its workforce and assets while earning continued public
confidence in how it spends money.

People make friends, become more ambitious, discover that a colleague has
outgrown their advice, and put considerable effort into things they insist
are unimportant. The companies have ordinary weeks, seasonal work, successes,
and unfinished plans of their own.

## Canon and scope

The principal names remain **KeplerOps Software** and **Alterra Regional Water
Company (ARWC)**. Their domains are **keplerops.com** and **alterrawaterco.com**;
the author owns both and has authorized their use. **FieldKest** is KeplerOps'
product. **Cairn Reach Reservoir** is an ARWC asset. All company histories,
staff, supporting businesses, and local circumstances here are original fiction.

The ten existing actor IDs remain continuity keys. Leah, Luc, Mina, Owen, and
Rosa are narrative characters without assigned challenge identities. Their
jobs establish their place in the company, rather than application permissions.
The initial profiled cast comprises six KeplerOps employees, eight ARWC employees,
and Jules, an external contractor employed by Veybridge. The full roster adds
280 employees for a total of 64 at KeplerOps and 230 at ARWC. It does not add
challenge actors or privileges.

The subsequent [workplace asset collection](../../assets/narrative/README.md)
implements this draft as correspondence, documents, directories, and calendars.
Screened names are aligned in the active SDL, challenge cards, and technical
data contract. Older names in historical design material remain authoring
aliases; they do not represent in-world name changes.

Further content implementation is tracked in the
[enterprise content milestone](https://github.com/PaloAltoNetworks/shifter-scenarios/milestone/3).
Its issues deliver finished content slices, each with fit review, adversarial
review, assets, and native SDL authoring. The first issue extended the existing
character profiles; the workforce slice supplies the complete employee roster.
Generator infrastructure is outside that milestone's scope.

Worldbuilding owns personalities, histories, relationships, and ordinary work.
Exact evidence needed by an individual challenge remains in its technical
section. The [content direction](content-direction.md) records that boundary
once so it need not interrupt every character's biography.

## Editorial tone

The workplaces permit warmth, ambition, disappointment, ordinary mistakes,
irritation, and differences that people never completely settle. Humour grows
from familiarity and specific behaviour. Friendships can be uneven without
being exploitative; colleagues can disagree without becoming enemies.

Workplace stories contain no criminal conduct or harassment. Personal detail
stays respectful and suitable for a public commercial event. Characters have
lives that they choose to share selectively with colleagues. Those lives do
not all need to become stories, and every story does not need a neat lesson.
