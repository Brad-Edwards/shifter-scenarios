# Service account correspondence: writing notes

Author-only guidance prepared before the correspondence. Existing full profiles
remain authoritative for Rosa, Priya, Lila Perrow and Selma Ardenn.

Rosa answers the customer's actual question first. A clarification should be
short enough to answer on a phone. She signs external messages with her name
and ARWC; colleagues already know where she works. Sample: “The meter stays with
the building. Is the new address just for post, or are you leaving the property?”

Tamsin Peveran (`awm183`) is an established customer adviser, reporting to Soren.
She wants to finish changes in one exchange, but checks who should receive a
document before acting. Her questions are direct and her completion notes short.
Sample: “I can send a large-print copy. Would you like that for this statement
only, or for future post as well? There is no charge.”

Tariq Zorrel (`awm184`) works on the same customer-contact team. He likes a
concrete boundary: which building, which meter, which person may be contacted.
He writes a little more formally to organizations, loosely to close colleagues.
Sample: “That label is on the check meter. Can you send the number on the supply
meter beside it? The two readings belong in different places.”

Amara Kelswick (`awm197`) leads Accounts, reporting to Soren. She separates a
payment from its allocation and dislikes promising a refund before checking the
ledger. Brief internal notes can be fragments; customer explanations use words
before identifiers. Sample: “The receipt is there. It paid the July opening
invoice; it has not been lost or counted twice.”

Anika Selwyn (`awm198`) is an accounts officer reporting to Amara. She asks for
the reference needed to trace a payment, never a complete bank statement. Sample:
“Please send the receipt reference and the date. You can leave out your bank
details; we do not need them to check this.”

Customer writing varies by the reason for contact. A resident replying from a
phone may send one line. A building secretary distinguishes their personal home
from the building's account. A shop owner cares about post arriving before the
shop opens. Facilities contacts use building and meter labels. None is assigned
an English proficiency level from a name. Names, addresses and customer facts
are newly authored fiction. One-off customer contacts are not added to staff
directories or treated as recurring cast members.

ARWC Account Notices is an automatic sender. Its regular structure is deliberate:
account, opening invoice, paid amount, balance, due date and a single attachment.
It asks no question that would force a ritual acknowledgement. Those notices
are separate from the personal correspondence and counted once per delivery.
