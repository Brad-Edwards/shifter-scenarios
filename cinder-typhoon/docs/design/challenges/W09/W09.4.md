## Challenge description — player-facing

Combine the customer data you understand with the recovered read-integration contract. Reach the matching current process feed and recover a fresh observation.

## Story purpose

Completes the corporate-integration route to OT visibility.

## Prerequisites and starting position

**Required access or evidence:** ([W03.READ](../../capability-routes.json) AND [W09.DATA](../../capability-routes.json)).

**Starting material:** Corporate allocation records, a separately published meter export, readable units/period definitions, and integration discovery material.

**Campaign references:** No separately named capability output.

## Completion and downstream use

Resolve the active data binding and demonstrate a scoped read from the intended process feed. This supplies the integration variant of OT_READ; stale exports and unrelated feeds do not count.

## Difficulty and inspiration

A medium join of district/revision evidence and scoped integration access.

**Inspiration:** Original challenge for this campaign.
