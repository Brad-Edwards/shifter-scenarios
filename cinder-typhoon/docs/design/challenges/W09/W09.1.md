## Challenge description — player-facing

Recover the current allocation ledger and identify the commitments ARWC has made for the approaching dry period.

## Story purpose

Makes water availability a business constraint rather than an abstract number.

## Prerequisites and starting position

**Required access or evidence:** [CORPORATE](../../capability-routes.json).

**Starting material:** Corporate allocation records, a separately published meter export, readable units/period definitions, and integration discovery material.

**Campaign references:** No separately named capability output.

## Completion and downstream use

Recover the actual ledger with its district and reporting period. It is one input to W09.3, not a conclusion about usable reserve.

## Difficulty and inspiration

A readable corporate record with a clear planning purpose.

**Inspiration:** Original challenge for this campaign.
