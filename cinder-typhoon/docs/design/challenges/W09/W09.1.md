## Technical design


### Surface and normal behavior

The `planning.allocation-ledger` route in the planning_data service client document → native Metabase question ALLOC-DP3-CURRENT (GET `/api/card/<id>`). The participant uses the W06.3 Metabase session via `X-Metabase-Session` to read the allocation ledger from `corporate-db` under `planning_reader` SELECT grant. The initial state: allocation ledger ALLOC-2026-DP3-R9 commits 12.00 ML for Cairn Reach district, asset AST-CRR-017, planning window ALLOC-2026-DP3.

Native bindings:

- Route: `planning.allocation-ledger` in the planning_data client document → `GET https://reports.arwc.test/api/card/<id>`
- Metabase question: ALLOC-DP3-CURRENT on `public.allocation_ledger` in corporate-db
- PostgreSQL: `planning` DB, `planning_reader` role, SELECT on `allocation_ledger`
- Authentication: W06.3 Metabase session (X-Metabase-Session header)

### Vulnerability and intended solution

This card is an exact discovery, interpretation, or reconciliation task rather than an additional privilege flaw. Allocation ledger ALLOC-2026-DP3-R9 commits 12.00 ML for Cairn Reach asset AST-CRR-017 in planning window ALLOC-2026-DP3. The result must come from the declared source and bindings, not from a duplicate clue or pre-completed record.

The required result is: Recover the actual ledger with its district and reporting period. It is one input to W09.3, not a conclusion about usable reserve. Process quantities and state separation are governed by `arwc-process-model.md`, specifically `ALLOC-2026-DP3`, `REC-CRR-DP3-17`.

### Evidence and completion

The owning service writes an immutable audit for the accepted request and result. Caller-supplied output, a local copy, or knowledge of an identifier is not sufficient. Completion is evaluated from fresh service-owned evidence after the action. It cannot issue the identity, create the mutation, move a simulated actuator, or fabricate a downstream acceptance needed to prove itself.

Evidence sources are `features.a-corporate.a-data`. A wrong identifier, stale revision, replayed one-time value, different checkpoint, or cross-tenant artifact is rejected.

### Boundaries and persistence

The result remains local evidence unless a downstream workflow explicitly names it. Tenant-scoped mutations, sessions that have not expired, generated artifacts, and audits persist across ordinary retries and later sessions. Initial immutable records remain unchanged unless the named route explicitly permits a field-level revision. Infrastructure recovery and submission adjudication are external to the SDL.

The behavior is limited to the named fictional objects and bounded service operation. It provides no general shell, arbitrary network target, unrelated district access, real process interface, or authority not expressly declared by the card.

### Author checks

Repeat the accepted action according to its idempotence rule and confirm ordinary retries preserve the result. For a joined result, verify each destination observed the same correlation and that removal of any independently owned source prevents completion.
