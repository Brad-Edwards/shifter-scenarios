## Challenge description—player-facing

Establish what the approved planning read service actually publishes and what remains outside its boundary.

## Starting position

The customer-owned handover provides the genuine Veybridge supplier account
and its limited external document access. It grants no workforce, planning
administration or OT authority.

## Native surface and normal behavior

The engineering acceptance note refers to upstream PostgREST. The native OpenAPI document and the PostgreSQL dmz_reader grants control the exposed dmz_api view; no bespoke query service impersonates a database.

## Completion and product evidence

Use the actual OpenAPI relation/field metadata and a filtered live query to reconcile asset AST-CRR-017, planning window, source record, revision, reserve value and observation date. Establish denial for a base allocation relation and a write, with unchanged data. Compare those results to the acceptance note and trusted native product evidence.

Evidence collection is read-only. It cannot create a share, change a document,
issue a product session, or fabricate a product history entry. Native product
state and submitted observations must refer to the same range instance.

## Difficulty and research

Medium: combines independently obtained acceptance, live schema/query results and a demonstrated read boundary. Repeating the role name or endpoint from a note is insufficient.

Primary reference: [upstream product documentation](https://docs.postgrest.org/en/stable/references/api.html).

## Denied neighbors and reset

Check the ordinary user and one denied neighboring record through supported
interfaces. Retained-volume restarts preserve product state. Explicit native IT
reset restores the seeded accounts, documents, history and per-record grants;
it does not reset qualified OT.
