## Challenge description — player-facing

The developer closed the support portal before leaving. Recover the unfinished ARWC conversation retained by the browser.

## Story purpose

Introduces the recurring support engineer and the customer ticket.

## Prerequisites and starting position

**Required access or evidence:** the supplied KeplerOps developer foothold.

**Starting material:** The developer's working directory, browser profile, saved client settings, and local FieldKest tools.

**Campaign references:** No separately named capability output.

## Completion and downstream use

Recover the retained conversation and its actual ticket reference. It supplies optional story context, not the credentials or current customer binding required for entry.

## Difficulty and inspiration

A small browser artifact with a clear subject and limited evidence recovery.

**Inspiration:** Original challenge for this campaign.

## Technical design

### Surface and normal behavior
The supplied browser profile has normal session-restoration data for the FieldKest support gateway and its seeded ARWC conversation.

### Vulnerability and intended solution
Closing the tab did not clear recoverable browser state. The intended solution is forensic recovery of the existing conversation and correlation of its actual ticket ID.

### Evidence and completion
The support gateway logs the conversation restoration and exposes the immutable `SUP-2841` record as player evidence.

### Boundaries and reset
The restored view is read-only, limited to one conversation, and grants no support action or customer delivery; reset removes its session and event.

### Author checks
Verify no tab is initially open, the profile holds one fair restoration lead, the recovered ticket resolves once, direct unrelated ticket reads fail, and reset clears state.
