## Challenge description — player-facing

Recover the export job that support started for its unresolved customer case. Identify the actual dataset and job revision.

## Story purpose

Gives cloud collection a legitimate business workflow and a specific target.

## Prerequisites and starting position

**Required access or evidence:** [K13.4](../K13/K13.4.md) OR [K17.1](../K17/K17.1.md).

**Starting material:** Support job references, an ordinary customer-export interface, and the earned cloud or support identity.

**Campaign references:** No separately named capability output.

## Completion and downstream use

Recover the unpublished job record and dataset binding. The job is a lead, not the collection itself.

## Difficulty and inspiration

One accessible job-discovery task from either supported starting identity.

**Inspiration:** Original challenge for this campaign.
