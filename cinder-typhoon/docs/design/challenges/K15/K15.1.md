## Challenge description — player-facing

Recover the export job that support started for its unresolved customer case. Identify the actual dataset and job revision.

## Story purpose

Gives cloud collection a legitimate business workflow and a specific target.

## Prerequisites and starting position

**Required access or evidence:** [K13.4](../K13/K13.4.md) OR [K17.1](../K17/K17.1.md).

**Starting material:** Support job references, an ordinary customer-export interface, and the earned cloud or support identity.

**Campaign references:** No separately named capability output.

## Completion and downstream use

Recover the unpublished job record and dataset binding. The job is a lead, not the collection itself.

## Difficulty and inspiration

One accessible job-discovery task from either supported starting identity.

**Inspiration:** Original challenge for this campaign.

## Technical design


### Surface and normal behavior
The export job `EXP-2841` is a real K3s `batch/v1 Job` in the `fieldlink` namespace. Its container env vars contain the job summary: `EXPORT_ID=EXP-2841`, `CASE_ID=CAS-MAYA-2841`, `DATASET=support-case-history`, `REVISION=6`, `STATE=unpublished`. The `svc-fieldlink-export` ServiceAccount (from K14.2) has GET access to this specific Job via RBAC. `svc-fieldlink-workload` (K13.4) does not.

The `data.export` route on `cloud-api.keplerops.test` reads the K3s Job spec using the export SA token and formats the result.

### Vulnerability and intended solution
The export job is a real K3s Job with an `unpublished` state. The participant uses the K14.2 export SA token to read it via `GET /api/exports/EXP-2841`. The cloud-api calls the K3s Jobs API with the export SA token; K3s RBAC allows the read.

The intended solution:
1. Know the export ID `EXP-2841` from K13.3 (handover) or K14.1 (trust record service_ref).
2. Use the K14.2 export SA token to `GET /api/exports/EXP-2841` on `cloud-api.keplerops.test`.
3. The response includes `export_id=EXP-2841`, `state=unpublished`, `dataset=support-case-history`, `revision=6`.

### Evidence and completion
The K3s Job read returns a real Job spec. The export-jobs audit entry records `export_id=EXP-2841`, `dataset=support-case-history`, `revision=6`. The evidence is a fresh read from the real K3s API.

### Boundaries and persistence

Lead only. Participant-created service state persists for the life of the participant world and ordinary retries do not erase it. The action is limited to the exact records, identities, routes, and destination effects declared here; unrelated tenants and services remain denied. Infrastructure recovery and submission adjudication are outside this in-world contract.

### Author checks
Verify that `exp-2841` is a real K3s Job with the correct env vars, that svc-fieldlink-export can GET it (200), that svc-fieldlink-workload is denied (403 from K3s RBAC), and that the export-jobs audit entry is written.

## Hints

### Hint 1

The `data.export` route is listed in the cloud-api client reference. Know the export ID `EXP-2841` from K13.3 (handover export_id) or K14.1 (trust record service_ref). Use the K14.2 export SA token: `GET /api/exports/EXP-2841` with `Authorization: Bearer <export_sa_token>`.

### Hint 2

The response is the K3s Job summary: `export_id=EXP-2841`, `state=unpublished`, `dataset=support-case-history`, `revision=6`, `case_id=CAS-MAYA-2841`. The `state=unpublished` and `revision=6` are the K15.1 completion evidence. Keep the export SA token for K15.2.

### Hint 3

The svc-fieldlink-workload token (K13.4) is denied by K3s RBAC for the export Job. Only the svc-fieldlink-export token (K14.2) can read it. The job is a lead for K15.2-K15.4.
