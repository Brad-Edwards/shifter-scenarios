## Challenge description — player-facing

Use the available delegated job rights to redirect the export handover while keeping the customer's job valid.

## Story purpose

Changes a business workflow's data destination.

## Prerequisites and starting position

**Required access or evidence:** [K15.2](K15.2.md).

**Starting material:** Support job references, an ordinary customer-export interface, and the earned cloud or support identity.

**Campaign references:** No separately named capability output.

## Completion and downstream use

Demonstrate an accepted destination change on the correct job, with its source selection preserved. General job read access is insufficient.

## Difficulty and inspiration

Requires composing delegated rights with the export's destination and job binding.

**Inspiration:** Original challenge for this campaign.
