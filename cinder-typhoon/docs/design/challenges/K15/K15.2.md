## Challenge description—player-facing

Obtain the restricted export definition for the customer's job even though your identity does not own it.

## Story purpose

Establishes a distinct authorization breach in export administration.

## Prerequisites and starting position

**Required access or evidence:** [K15.1](K15.1.md).

**Starting material:** Support job references, an ordinary customer-export interface, and the earned cloud or support identity.

**Campaign references:** No separately named capability output.

## Completion and downstream use

Recover the protected job definition and its source boundaries. This does not yet change the destination or retrieve the collection.

## Difficulty and inspiration

A bounded object-ownership problem with explicit job identifiers.

**Inspiration:** Original challenge for this campaign.

## Technical design


### Surface and normal behavior
The export definition `exp-2841-definition` is a real K3s ConfigMap in `fieldlink` namespace, owned by `maya.ranscombe`. It contains: owner, tenant (TEN-ARWC-019), partitions, destinations, and partition_bindings.

The K15.2 ownership gap: the detail route checks that the caller has the export SA role (tenant visibility) but does NOT check that the caller owns the export job. K3s RBAC grants `svc-fieldlink-export` GET access to `exp-2841-definition` without an ownership check.

### Vulnerability and intended solution
The RBAC Role `export-definition-reader` allows `svc-fieldlink-export` to read `exp-2841-definition` even though the job belongs to `maya.ranscombe`. The cloud-api endpoint validates that the caller has the export SA token but omits the job ownership check.

The intended solution:
1. Use the K14.2 export SA token with `GET /api/exports/EXP-2841/definition` on `cloud-api.keplerops.test`.
2. K3s RBAC allows the read (200); the response shows `owner=maya.ranscombe`.
3. The definition includes destinations and partition_bindings needed for K15.3-K15.4.

### Evidence and completion
The export-definitions audit entry records `owner=maya.ranscombe` alongside `accessor=svc-fieldlink-export`. The definition content (owner, tenant, destinations, partition_bindings) is the K15.2 evidence.

### Boundaries and persistence

Required for destination change. Participant-created service state persists for the life of the participant world and ordinary retries do not erase it. The action is limited to the exact records, identities, routes, and destination effects declared here; unrelated tenants and services remain denied. Infrastructure recovery and submission adjudication are outside this in-world contract.

### Author checks
Verify that `exp-2841-definition` K3s ConfigMap has `owner=maya.ranscombe`, that svc-fieldlink-export reads it (200) via K3s RBAC, that the audit records `owner=maya.ranscombe` and `accessor=svc-fieldlink-export`, and that the definition includes destinations and partition_bindings.
