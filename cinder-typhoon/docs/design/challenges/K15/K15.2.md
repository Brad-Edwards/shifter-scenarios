## Challenge description — player-facing

Obtain the restricted export definition for the customer's job even though your identity does not own it.

## Story purpose

Establishes a distinct authorization breach in export administration.

## Prerequisites and starting position

**Required access or evidence:** [K15.1](K15.1.md).

**Starting material:** Support job references, an ordinary customer-export interface, and the earned cloud or support identity.

**Campaign references:** No separately named capability output.

## Completion and downstream use

Recover the protected job definition and its source boundaries. This does not yet change the destination or retrieve the collection.

## Difficulty and inspiration

A bounded object-ownership problem with explicit job identifiers.

**Inspiration:** Original challenge for this campaign.
