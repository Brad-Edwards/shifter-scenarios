## Challenge description — player-facing

The importer can retrieve material the developer cannot read directly. Recover usable authority over the release channel consumed by FieldKest customers.

## Story purpose

Creates the ordinary package-delivery route's publication capability.

## Completion and downstream use

## Difficulty and inspiration

A familiar application boundary with a clear registry target and bounded scope.

**Inspiration:** [Prison Pipeline (HTB Business CTF 2024; organizer Medium / writeup Easy)](../../reference-catalog.md#c01). Adapts the importer-to-registry portion of Prison Pipeline; production ARWC delivery remains K26.

## Technical design


### Surface and normal behavior
The FieldKest importer uses a registry configuration object that the developer cannot directly read, and a scoped Verdaccio publication identity for the customer connector channel.

### Vulnerability and intended solution
The importer exposes its resolved configuration in an error path after validating a normal package request. The intended solution is to recover and use the scoped credential through the registry's ordinary authenticated flow.

### Evidence and completion
Registry audit data must show the scoped identity performing its permitted release-channel action; this awards `A01`.

### Boundaries and persistence

Supplies A01 Participant-created service state persists for the life of the participant world and ordinary retries do not erase it. The action is limited to the exact records, identities, routes, and destination effects declared here; unrelated tenants and services remain denied. Infrastructure recovery and submission adjudication are outside this in-world contract.

### Author checks
Test direct developer denial, intended error evidence, scoped registry use, other-scope denial, no accepted result for copied configuration, and revocation.
