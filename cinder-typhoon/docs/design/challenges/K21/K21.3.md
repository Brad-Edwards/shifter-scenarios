## Challenge description — player-facing

Recover the support engineer's private escalation note about ARWC from the assistant's case context, although the assistant's ordinary support role should withhold it.

## Story purpose

Produces a bounded information result from an assistant trust failure.

## Prerequisites and starting position

**Required access or evidence:** [K21.1](K21.1.md).

**Starting material:** The repository's support-assistant references, retained assistant artifacts, and its ordinary support interface.

The case's retained assistant configuration and conversation activity identify the case and source references attached to a reply. The operator can inspect those records and compare them with the assistant's actual answer or refusal to investigate whether the note was available to that conversation. These are ordinary support records within the earned access, with the private note still withheld. The application does not diagnose the operator's attack or explain the model's reasoning.

**Campaign references:** No separately named capability output.

## Completion and downstream use

Recover the actual synthetic escalation note, including the unresolved commissioning concern. Public ticket text or an invented account of the dispute does not satisfy the objective. Acceptance concerns the recovered evidence and its case binding, with no required conversational wording.

## Difficulty and inspiration

A small, explicit context-disclosure objective using the exposed configuration.

**Inspiration:** [Chrono Mind (HTB Business CTF 2024; Easy)](../../reference-catalog.md#c02). Uses Chrono Mind as application-trust inspiration. Only the final challenge needs an in-story model response; no training or expensive computation is involved.

## Technical design


### Surface and normal behavior
`k-assistant` owns this part of the operation. Its starting state is exact: ctx-support-private-v4 route accepts case reference before applying support-role document filters; exact Qwen2.5-3B-Instruct artifact, deterministic decoding (temperature=0, fixed template/seed) receives private escalation source. The owning service uses the listener, route, identity, authorization, storage, and audit contract declared in the corresponding world module. Records that are not explicitly mutable remain immutable. The private service-loader contract `content.keplerops-k-assistant.k21-3-the-answer-it-should-withhold` freezes the request, denial, state-transition, evidence, and any exploit-critical implementation details.

### Vulnerability and intended solution
The intended path is limited to the authored software, data, configuration, identity, permission, or state described above. The participant must exercise that real condition through the declared service interface and produce this result: Response cites and discloses source ESC-SUP-2841-R3; retrieval/source audit proves grounding. Merely describing the condition, editing a local copy, or presenting a value obtained from another surface does not satisfy the contract.

### Evidence and completion
Completion is observed by the owning service from the fresh result and its immutable audit correlation. The evidence binds the participant, accepted identity or job, exact input and output identifiers, relevant revision or digest, and destination-side observation where a second service consumes the result. Objective evaluation does not create the result or grant authority.

### Boundaries and persistence

Assistant conclusion only Participant-created service state persists for the life of the participant world and ordinary retries do not erase it. The action is limited to the exact records, identities, routes, and destination effects declared here; unrelated tenants and services remain denied. Infrastructure recovery and submission adjudication are outside this in-world contract.

### Author checks
Verify the normal baseline, the exact intended path, and the fresh evidence described above. Reject a wrong identifier, revision, digest, tenant, identity, stale result, or cross-participant reference. Verify unrelated records and permissions remain unchanged and that the result remains recognizable and usable by every declared downstream consumer.
