## Technical design


### Surface and normal behavior
The FieldKest compatibility service exposes current package entitlements and retains a versioned legacy-format record for a named customer feature.

### Vulnerability and intended solution

The entitlement record `ENT-ARWC-DIAG-0698` lives in the `fieldkest/connector-archive` Gitea repository at `internal/fieldlink/legacy-diagnostics/CRR-OG2/`. Its commit history shows the progression from revision 3 to revision 4. The record contains the AES-256-GCM ciphertext and the HKDF context metadata.

The intended solution:
1. Navigate to `fieldkest/connector-archive` in Gitea and find `ENT-ARWC-DIAG-0698.json`.
2. Observe the revision history (revision 3 → revision 4 commits).
3. Obtain the HKDF derivation context from the K05.2 release record `REL-FLK-6.9.8-ARCHIVE.compatibility`.
4. POST to `packages.entitlement-check` with `checker=v1`, the entitlement record from Gitea, and the HKDF context from the release record.
5. The real checker (HKDF-SHA256 + AES-256-GCM inline in the registry service) decrypts and returns the entitlement fields: customer, feature, package revision, expiry policy, and plaintext SHA256.

The checker is the genuine inline versioned checker in the registry service. It performs real HKDF-SHA256 key derivation and AES-256-GCM decryption; there is no standalone separate component.

### Evidence and completion
Completion records the protected record revision, customer, feature, and legacy field grammar for K08.2.

### Boundaries and persistence

Input to K08.2 Participant-created service state persists for the life of the participant world and ordinary retries do not erase it. The action is limited to the exact records, identities, routes, and destination effects declared here; unrelated tenants and services remain denied. Infrastructure recovery and submission adjudication are outside this in-world contract.

### Author checks
Test current/legacy distinction, fair lookup lead, unique record recovery, no direct entitlement activation.
