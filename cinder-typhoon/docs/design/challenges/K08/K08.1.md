## Challenge description — player-facing

FieldKest still recognizes an old entitlement format. Recover its protected compatibility record and determine what the checker considers an entitlement.

## Story purpose

Introduces a legacy product boundary with a concrete protected record.

## Prerequisites and starting position

**Required access or evidence:** [G01 — K01.1](../K01/K01.1.md).

**Starting material:** The obsolete entitlement checker, representative license records, compatibility notes, and its bounded exercise interface.

**Campaign references:** No separately named capability output.

## Completion and downstream use

Recover the entitlement's actual structure and associated compatibility record. The current package-signing system is outside this task.

## Difficulty and inspiration

A compact format and behavior investigation with representative inputs.

**Inspiration:** [Tsayaki (HTB Cyber Apocalypse 2024; Hard)](../../reference-catalog.md#c06). Retains Tsayaki's legacy cryptographic entitlement problem; this concerns an obsolete component.

## Technical design


### Surface and normal behavior
The FieldKest compatibility service exposes current package entitlements and retains a versioned legacy-format record for a named customer feature.

### Vulnerability and intended solution
A compatibility lookup treats a legacy record as public metadata despite its protected feature binding. The intended solution recovers the record and models the checker's claimed entitlement fields.

### Evidence and completion
Completion records the protected record revision, customer, feature, and legacy field grammar for K08.2.

### Boundaries and persistence

Input to K08.2 Participant-created service state persists for the life of the participant world and ordinary retries do not erase it. The action is limited to the exact records, identities, routes, and destination effects declared here; unrelated tenants and services remain denied. Infrastructure recovery and submission adjudication are outside this in-world contract.

### Author checks
Test current/legacy distinction, fair lookup lead, unique record recovery, no direct entitlement activation.
