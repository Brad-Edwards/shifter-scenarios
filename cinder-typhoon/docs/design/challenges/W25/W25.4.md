## Challenge description — player-facing

Prove the bounded movement in native process history, then apply the authorized A20/B20 restoration and verify the live site has returned to baseline.

## Story purpose

Completes a small real operating task with an accountable handback, rather than leaving the range in a changed state.

## Prerequisites and starting position

**Required access or evidence:** ([W25.3](W25.3.md) AND [W17.OBS](../../capability-routes.json)).

**Starting material:** The accepted CRR-W25-MOVE-01 observation request and the native Cairn Reach history view.

**Campaign references:** `W25.MODE`.

## Completion and downstream use

Show good-quality position and flow history for the A20/B20 to A30/B10 movement, then use a second accepted HMI request to restore manual A20/B20. Confirm measured A20/B20, approximately 0.10 m³/s per outlet and 0.20 m³/s total, healthy I/O, clear travel faults, valid permissives, and no unsettled motion. This supplies W25.MODE and MODE.

## Difficulty and inspiration

Medium: the movement is fully prescribed, but the participant must join two accepted command sequences with time-series feedback and perform an exact operational handback. FUXA provides ordinary device-bound control, while the native historian preserves process history and gaps rather than treating an acknowledgement as physical proof.

**Research:** [FUXA documentation](https://frangoteam.github.io/FUXA/), [FUXA historian support](https://github.com/frangoteam/FUXA/blob/master/README.md), [OpenPLC Runtime Editor integration](https://github.com/Autonomy-Logic/openplc-runtime/blob/main/docs/EDITOR_INTEGRATION.md), [ISA-101 HMI principles](https://www.isa.org/standards-and-publications/isa-standards/isa-101-standards).

## Technical design


### Surface and normal behavior

The installed Cairn Reach OT products own this part of **A sequence the process can follow**. The exact initial state is: Authorized movement CRR-W25-MOVE-01 changes the live manual request from A 20/B 20 to A 30/B 10, records good native position and flow history, and uses a second accepted request to restore A 20/B 20 with both outlets settled, total flow near 0.20 m3/s, healthy I/O and no travel fault.

Native bindings:

- `a-engineering.a-hmi/FUXA:crr-controls`
- `a-engineering.a-historian/Grafana:arwc-history`
- `a-engineering.a-historian/PostgreSQL:public.process_history`
- `a-control.a-instruments/Modbus-TCP:position-and-flow-feedback`

The image-owned W25 operating envelope and bounded-movement instruction define the authorized work, but do not prove controller acceptance or movement. FUXA, OpenPLC, PostgreSQL history, and remote-I/O feedback remain authoritative; no private service-loader route duplicates them.

### Vulnerability and intended solution

This is a bounded operating observation and control task, not a privilege flaw. The participant must use the installed products to complete the authorized movement, prove the measured response in retained history, and restore the operating baseline. A copied value, synthetic response, or request acknowledgement without matching product evidence does not establish the result.

The required result is: Show good-quality position and flow history for the A20/B20 to A30/B10 movement, then use a second accepted HMI request to restore manual A20/B20. Confirm measured A20/B20, approximately 0.10 m³/s per outlet and 0.20 m³/s total, healthy I/O, clear travel faults, valid permissives, and no unsettled motion. This supplies W25.MODE and MODE.

### Evidence and completion

Completion compares the participant's observation with fresh product records captured by OpenPLC and the native historian. Product evidence is supplied by the range, never by the participant. W25.1 and W25.2 are read-only. W25.3 and W25.4 may use only the two authorized FUXA requests in CRR-OP-W25-R1, and completion cannot write a tag, issue a command, retry a rejection, or create product evidence.

Evidence sources are `features.a-engineering.a-hmi`, `features.a-engineering.a-historian`, `features.a-control.a-instruments`. A wrong asset, operating limit, command sequence, ownership, permissive, product time, process quality, measured response, or restored baseline is rejected.

### Boundaries and persistence

Supplies W25.MODE and MODE without live authority. The product keeps normal command and process history. Completion of the movement requires a second accepted HMI request, measured return to A 20 percent and B 20 percent, total flow near 0.20 m3/s, healthy I/O, and clear travel faults. Infrastructure recovery and submission adjudication are external to the SDL.

The behavior is limited to the installed Cairn Reach products, the named asset, and CRR-OP-W25-R1. It grants no dispatcher authority, engineering force, program upload, interlock bypass, or alternate control endpoint.

### Author checks

Verify the healthy baseline through the participant workstation. Confirm the two read-only cards against the installed project and live HMI, then execute the exact bounded movement through FUXA. Require product-owned accepted-sequence, ownership, permissive, position, flow, quality, and restored-baseline evidence. Reject wrong or stale intervals, missing sources, participant-supplied product evidence, rejected requests, unsettled feedback, active faults, or failure to restore baseline. Reset must return the same qualified baseline without changing product identities or trust material.

## Hints

### Hint 1

Use the Cairn Reach history dashboard to bracket the first accepted sequence and the settled A30/B10 feedback. Product history, not a screenshot alone, proves the movement.

### Hint 2

Restore through the same FUXA path: A20, B20, manual mode, 0.20 m³/s, then Apply once and retain the new accepted sequence.

### Hint 3

Finish only after both positions settle within 0.5 percentage point of 20, each measured flow is near 0.10 m³/s, total flow is near 0.20 m³/s, motion is stopped, I/O is healthy, and both travel faults are clear.
