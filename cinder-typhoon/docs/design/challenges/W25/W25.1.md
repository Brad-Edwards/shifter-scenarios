## Challenge description — player-facing

Read the Cairn Reach operating instruction and reconcile its bounded movement with the limits shown by the installed HMI and controller project.

## Story purpose

Establishes the real operating limits and the narrower authorized work before anyone moves equipment.

## Prerequisites and starting position

**Required access or evidence:** [OT_READ](../../capability-routes.json).

**Starting material:** `operating-envelope.csv`, `bounded-movement.md`, the FUXA Site controls view, and the supplied OpenPLC project.

**Campaign references:** `W25.ENVELOPE`.

## Completion and downstream use

Identify the installed manual-position and automatic-flow limits, the per-outlet physical capacity, and the exact baseline, observation target, mode, and restoration target authorized by CRR-OP-W25-R1. This supplies W25.ENVELOPE, not permission for another movement.

## Difficulty and inspiration

Easy: the instruction names the relevant product views and the participant performs a direct limit reconciliation. FUXA officially treats HMI controls as live device-bound controls, while OpenPLC Runtime executes the IEC project with deterministic scan cycles; the task therefore asks for ordinary operator/engineering interpretation, not reverse engineering.

**Research:** [FUXA documentation](https://frangoteam.github.io/FUXA/), [FUXA devices and tags](https://frangoteam.github.io/FUXA/HowTo-Devices-and-Tags/), [OpenPLC Runtime v4](https://github.com/Autonomy-Logic/openplc-runtime/blob/main/README.md).

## Technical design


### Surface and normal behavior

The installed Cairn Reach OT products own this part of **The operating envelope**. The exact initial state is: Operating instruction CRR-OP-W25-R1 binds the installed controller limits to one authorized manual movement: normal baseline A 20 percent and B 20 percent, bounded observation A 30 percent and B 10 percent, then restore A 20 percent and B 20 percent; total measured flow remains approximately 0.20 m3/s.

Native bindings:

- `a-engineering.a-engineering/filesystem:/home/fieldlink/CairnReach/Briefs/W25/operating-envelope.csv`
- `a-engineering.a-hmi/FUXA:crr-controls`
- `a-engineering.a-engineering/OpenPLC-Editor:SiteRequests-and-OutletControl`

The image-owned W25 operating envelope and bounded-movement instruction define the authorized work, but do not prove controller acceptance or movement. FUXA, OpenPLC, PostgreSQL history, and remote-I/O feedback remain authoritative; no private service-loader route duplicates them.

### Vulnerability and intended solution

This is a bounded operating observation and control task, not a privilege flaw. The participant must use the installed products to reconcile the documented job limits with the installed HMI and controller limits. A copied value, synthetic response, or request acknowledgement without matching product evidence does not establish the result.

The required result is: Identify the installed manual-position and automatic-flow limits, the per-outlet physical capacity, and the exact baseline, observation target, mode, and restoration target authorized by CRR-OP-W25-R1. This supplies W25.ENVELOPE, not permission for another movement.

### Evidence and completion

Completion compares the participant's observation with fresh product records captured by OpenPLC and the native historian. Product evidence is supplied by the range, never by the participant. W25.1 and W25.2 are read-only. W25.3 and W25.4 may use only the two authorized FUXA requests in CRR-OP-W25-R1, and completion cannot write a tag, issue a command, retry a rejection, or create product evidence.

Evidence sources are `features.a-engineering.a-hmi`, `features.a-engineering.a-engineering`. A wrong asset, operating limit, command sequence, ownership, permissive, product time, process quality, measured response, or restored baseline is rejected.

### Boundaries and persistence

Supplies W25.ENVELOPE and ENVELOPE. The product keeps normal command and process history. Completion of the movement requires a second accepted HMI request, measured return to A 20 percent and B 20 percent, total flow near 0.20 m3/s, healthy I/O, and clear travel faults. Infrastructure recovery and submission adjudication are external to the SDL.

The behavior is limited to the installed Cairn Reach products, the named asset, and CRR-OP-W25-R1. It grants no dispatcher authority, engineering force, program upload, interlock bypass, or alternate control endpoint.

### Author checks

Verify the healthy baseline through the participant workstation. Confirm the two read-only cards against the installed project and live HMI, then execute the exact bounded movement through FUXA. Require product-owned accepted-sequence, ownership, permissive, position, flow, quality, and restored-baseline evidence. Reject wrong or stale intervals, missing sources, participant-supplied product evidence, rejected requests, unsettled feedback, active faults, or failure to restore baseline. Reset must return the same qualified baseline without changing product identities or trust material.
