## Challenge description — player-facing

Apply the authorized A30/B10 manual request and distinguish the staged request, PLC decision, command owner, permissives, and measured outlet response.

## Story purpose

Makes command feedback accountable to independent physical observations.

## Prerequisites and starting position

**Required access or evidence:** ([W25.1](W25.1.md) AND [W25.2](W25.2.md)).

**Starting material:** CRR-OP-W25-R1 and the qualified A20/B20 manual baseline.

**Campaign references:** No separately named capability output.

## Completion and downstream use

Correlate one new HMI sequence with OpenPLC seen and accepted state, HMI ownership, the accepted A30/B10 settings, valid permissives, and subsequent position/flow feedback. An Apply acknowledgement or changed input field alone does not prove actuation.

## Difficulty and inspiration

Medium: the participant must correlate several product states over time rather than read one label. FUXA writes device-bound tag values and OpenPLC separately executes the control program; retained command and process history provide the independent timing and feedback needed to tell acceptance from actuation.

**Research:** [FUXA devices and tags](https://frangoteam.github.io/FUXA/HowTo-Devices-and-Tags/), [FUXA project and historian capabilities](https://github.com/frangoteam/FUXA/blob/master/README.md), [OpenPLC Runtime architecture](https://github.com/Autonomy-Logic/openplc-runtime/blob/main/README.md).

## Technical design


### Surface and normal behavior

The installed Cairn Reach OT products own this part of **Accepted is not actuated**. The exact initial state is: A FUXA Apply action stages one complete request and advances its sequence; OpenPLC separately records seen, accepted or rejected sequence, owner and requested settings before remote I/O position and flow feedback can prove physical actuation.

Native bindings:

- `a-engineering.a-hmi/FUXA:crr-controls`
- `a-engineering.a-hmi/OPC-UA:HmiSeen-HmiAccepted-Owner-PermissiveA-PermissiveB`
- `a-engineering.a-historian/PostgreSQL:public.command_history`
- `a-control.a-instruments/Modbus-TCP:position-and-flow-feedback`

The image-owned W25 operating envelope and bounded-movement instruction define the authorized work, but do not prove controller acceptance or movement. FUXA, OpenPLC, PostgreSQL history, and remote-I/O feedback remain authoritative; no private service-loader route duplicates them.

### Vulnerability and intended solution

This is a bounded operating observation and control task, not a privilege flaw. The participant must use the installed products to separate staged request values, PLC receipt and decision, command ownership, permissives, and physical feedback. A copied value, synthetic response, or request acknowledgement without matching product evidence does not establish the result.

The required result is: Correlate one new HMI sequence with OpenPLC seen and accepted state, HMI ownership, the accepted A30/B10 settings, valid permissives, and subsequent position/flow feedback. An Apply acknowledgement or changed input field alone does not prove actuation.

### Evidence and completion

Completion compares the participant's observation with fresh product records captured by OpenPLC and the native historian. Product evidence is supplied by the range, never by the participant. W25.1 and W25.2 are read-only. W25.3 and W25.4 may use only the two authorized FUXA requests in CRR-OP-W25-R1, and completion cannot write a tag, issue a command, retry a rejection, or create product evidence.

Evidence sources are `features.a-engineering.a-hmi`, `features.a-engineering.a-historian`, `features.a-control.a-instruments`. A wrong asset, operating limit, command sequence, ownership, permissive, product time, process quality, measured response, or restored baseline is rejected.

### Boundaries and persistence

The result remains local evidence unless a downstream workflow explicitly names it. The product keeps normal command and process history. Completion of the movement requires a second accepted HMI request, measured return to A 20 percent and B 20 percent, total flow near 0.20 m3/s, healthy I/O, and clear travel faults. Infrastructure recovery and submission adjudication are external to the SDL.

The behavior is limited to the installed Cairn Reach products, the named asset, and CRR-OP-W25-R1. It grants no dispatcher authority, engineering force, program upload, interlock bypass, or alternate control endpoint.

### Author checks

Verify the healthy baseline through the participant workstation. Confirm the two read-only cards against the installed project and live HMI, then execute the exact bounded movement through FUXA. Require product-owned accepted-sequence, ownership, permissive, position, flow, quality, and restored-baseline evidence. Reject wrong or stale intervals, missing sources, participant-supplied product evidence, rejected requests, unsettled feedback, active faults, or failure to restore baseline. Reset must return the same qualified baseline without changing product identities or trust material.

## Hints

### Hint 1

Enter A30, B10, manual mode, and 0.20 m³/s in FUXA, then Apply once. Keep the new sequence number.

### Hint 2

Receipt is HmiSeen. A controller decision is HmiAccepted or HmiRejected. Owner and accepted settings show what the PLC now owns; permissives say whether motion is allowed.

### Hint 3

Prove actuation only when independent measured positions and flows move from the A20/B20 baseline and settle near A30/B10 and 0.15/0.05 m³/s.
