## Challenge description — player-facing

Use FUXA and the live OpenPLC project to explain whether the Cairn Reach outlets can move now and which conditions establish that result.

## Story purpose

Makes a movement decision depend on live control state rather than a generic mode label.

## Prerequisites and starting position

**Required access or evidence:** [OT_READ](../../capability-routes.json).

**Starting material:** CRR-OP-W25-R1, the FUXA overview and outlet faceplates, and the supplied OpenPLC FBD.

**Campaign references:** No separately named capability output.

## Completion and downstream use

Identify the field-freshness, availability/selector, low-level, stop, fault, authorization, and per-outlet permissive conditions, then report their current live results and whether the authorized movement may proceed.

## Difficulty and inspiration

Easy: the HMI presents the current results and the supplied FBD shows their wiring. This matches normal HMI use: operators monitor process state and issue control instructions through the HMI, while engineering logic remains in the controller.

**Research:** [ISA description of HMI monitoring and control](https://www.isa.org/getmedia/b02b4a74-dcfd-4e01-b59a-c2cc51207650/ABoK-3rd-061318_Excerpt-final-1.pdf), [OpenPLC Runtime Editor integration](https://github.com/Autonomy-Logic/openplc-runtime/blob/main/docs/EDITOR_INTEGRATION.md), [FUXA documentation](https://frangoteam.github.io/FUXA/).

## Technical design


### Surface and normal behavior

The installed Cairn Reach OT products own this part of **Conditions before movement**. The exact initial state is: The live movement condition comes from OpenPLC field freshness, physical availability and remote selectors, low-level protection, travel faults, operator stop, controller authorization, and the two outlet permissives; FUXA shows their current results while the supplied FBD explains the chain.

Native bindings:

- `a-engineering.a-engineering/filesystem:/home/fieldlink/CairnReach/Briefs/W25/bounded-movement.md`
- `a-engineering.a-hmi/FUXA:crr-overview`
- `a-engineering.a-hmi/FUXA:crr-outlet-a`
- `a-engineering.a-hmi/FUXA:crr-outlet-b`
- `a-engineering.a-engineering/OpenPLC-Editor:live-permissive-chain`

The image-owned W25 operating envelope and bounded-movement instruction define the authorized work, but do not prove controller acceptance or movement. FUXA, OpenPLC, PostgreSQL history, and remote-I/O feedback remain authoritative; no private service-loader route duplicates them.

### Vulnerability and intended solution

This is a bounded operating observation and control task, not a privilege flaw. The participant must use the installed products to trace the installed permissive chain and identify the current live movement condition. A copied value, synthetic response, or request acknowledgement without matching product evidence does not establish the result.

The required result is: Identify the field-freshness, availability/selector, low-level, stop, fault, authorization, and per-outlet permissive conditions, then report their current live results and whether the authorized movement may proceed.

### Evidence and completion

Completion compares the participant's observation with fresh product records captured by OpenPLC and the native historian. Product evidence is supplied by the range, never by the participant. W25.1 and W25.2 are read-only. W25.3 and W25.4 may use only the two authorized FUXA requests in CRR-OP-W25-R1, and completion cannot write a tag, issue a command, retry a rejection, or create product evidence.

Evidence sources are `features.a-engineering.a-hmi`, `features.a-engineering.a-engineering`. A wrong asset, operating limit, command sequence, ownership, permissive, product time, process quality, measured response, or restored baseline is rejected.

### Boundaries and persistence

The result remains local evidence unless a downstream workflow explicitly names it. The product keeps normal command and process history. Completion of the movement requires a second accepted HMI request, measured return to A 20 percent and B 20 percent, total flow near 0.20 m3/s, healthy I/O, and clear travel faults. Infrastructure recovery and submission adjudication are external to the SDL.

The behavior is limited to the installed Cairn Reach products, the named asset, and CRR-OP-W25-R1. It grants no dispatcher authority, engineering force, program upload, interlock bypass, or alternate control endpoint.

### Author checks

Verify the healthy baseline through the participant workstation. Confirm the two read-only cards against the installed project and live HMI, then execute the exact bounded movement through FUXA. Require product-owned accepted-sequence, ownership, permissive, position, flow, quality, and restored-baseline evidence. Reject wrong or stale intervals, missing sources, participant-supplied product evidence, rejected requests, unsettled feedback, active faults, or failure to restore baseline. Reset must return the same qualified baseline without changing product identities or trust material.

## Hints

### Hint 1

FUXA shows field data, motion authorization, both outlet permissives, operator stop, and travel faults. Do not infer them from position alone.

### Hint 2

The main FBD wires field freshness, drive availability, and remote selectors into each outlet's base permit. The request and outlet blocks add the remaining conditions.

### Hint 3

Proceed only when I/O is healthy, both permissives are available, low level and operator stop are clear, both travel faults are clear, and the controller is armed for an HMI-owned manual request.
