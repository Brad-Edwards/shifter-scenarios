## Challenge description — player-facing

Change the federation policy for the support application's identity-provider registration so your controlled provider is accepted, while preserving the existing staff login relationship.

## Story purpose

Makes an identity trust relationship an attacker-controlled input.

## Prerequisites and starting position

**Required access or evidence:** [K18.2](K18.2.md).

**Starting material:** The support-note feature, ordinary identity-provider material, and a controlled note-review workflow.

**Campaign references:** No separately named capability output.

## Completion and downstream use

Demonstrate an accepted policy change and a successful existing-staff login. Merely reading the registration in K18.2 or replacing every user's identity configuration does not count.

## Difficulty and inspiration

Requires satisfying identity configuration constraints across the linked applications.

**Inspiration:** [SOS or SSO? (HTB Business CTF 2024; Hard)](../../reference-catalog.md#c18). Adapts SOS or SSO?'s support-content, identity, and backend-data composition.
