## Challenge description — player-facing

Change the federation policy for the support application's identity-provider registration so your controlled provider is accepted, while preserving the existing staff login relationship.

## Story purpose

Makes an identity trust relationship an attacker-controlled input.

## Prerequisites and starting position

**Required access or evidence:** [K18.2](K18.2.md).

**Starting material:** The support-note feature, ordinary identity-provider material, and a controlled note-review workflow.

**Campaign references:** No separately named capability output.

## Completion and downstream use

Demonstrate an accepted policy change and a successful existing-staff login. Merely reading the registration in K18.2 or replacing every user's identity configuration does not count.

## Difficulty and inspiration

Requires satisfying identity configuration constraints across the linked applications.

**Inspiration:** [SOS or SSO? (HTB Business CTF 2024; Hard)](../../reference-catalog.md#c18). Adapts SOS or SSO?'s support-content, identity, and backend-data composition.

## Technical design


### Surface and normal behavior
`k-staff` owns this part of the operation. Its starting state is exact: Preview metadata forwards an allowed method field; encoded traversal plus PATCH can add one fixture provider while preserving existing provider. The owning service uses the listener, route, identity, authorization, storage, and audit contract declared in the corresponding world module. Records that are not explicitly mutable remain immutable. The private service-loader contract `content.keplerops-k-staff.k18-3-the-provider-trusts-our-change` freezes the request, denial, state-transition, evidence, and any exploit-critical implementation details.

### Vulnerability and intended solution
The intended path is limited to the authored software, data, configuration, identity, permission, or state described above. The participant must exercise that real condition through the declared service interface and produce this result: Registration revision with fixture issuer and successful ordinary staff-login audit. Merely describing the condition, editing a local copy, or presenting a value obtained from another surface does not satisfy the contract.

### Evidence and completion
Completion is observed by the owning service from the fresh result and its immutable audit correlation. The evidence binds the participant, accepted identity or job, exact input and output identifiers, relevant revision or digest, and destination-side observation where a second service consumes the result. Objective evaluation does not create the result or grant authority.

### Boundaries and persistence
Controlled federation trust. Participant-created service state persists for the life of the participant world and ordinary retries do not erase it. The selected route cannot act on unrelated tenants, records, packages, identities, jobs, or services. Infrastructure recovery and submission adjudication are outside this in-world contract.

### Author checks
Verify the normal baseline, the exact intended path, and the fresh evidence described above. Reject a wrong identifier, revision, digest, tenant, identity, stale result, or cross-participant reference. Verify unrelated records and permissions remain unchanged and that the result remains recognizable and usable by every declared downstream consumer.
