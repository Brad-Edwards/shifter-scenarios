## Challenge description — player-facing

Complete the utility compromise while retaining the privilege needed to establish the scoped live control capability.

## Story purpose

Makes the hard exploit useful to the reservoir objective.

## Prerequisites and starting position

**Required access or evidence:** [W28.2](W28.2.md).

**Starting material:** The small engineering utility, its ordinary invocation examples, analysis material, and the live command capability it guards.

**Campaign references:** `W28.CONTROL`.

## Completion and downstream use

Demonstrate a control client with the intended live authority. This supplies W28.CONTROL and CONTROL, equivalent in scope to W26's route. Process knowledge remains required.

## Difficulty and inspiration

A privilege-sensitive completion of the native chain beyond controlling execution flow.

**Inspiration:** [Maze of Mist (HTB Cyber Apocalypse 2024; Hard)](../../reference-catalog.md#c16). Retains Maze of Mist's tiny native surface and privilege-sensitive exploitation objective.
