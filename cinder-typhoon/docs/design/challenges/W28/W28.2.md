## Challenge description — player-facing

Gain controlled execution flow in the engineering utility despite its restricted executable surface.

## Story purpose

Develops the substantive exploit capability for the control route.

## Prerequisites and starting position

**Required access or evidence:** [W28.1](W28.1.md).

**Starting material:** The small engineering utility, its ordinary invocation examples, analysis material, and the live command capability it guards.

**Campaign references:** No separately named capability output.

## Completion and downstream use

Demonstrate controlled flow under the utility's stated constraints. Crashing it or using a different unconstrained process does not count.

## Difficulty and inspiration

Constrained native exploitation directly inspired by Maze of Mist.

**Inspiration:** [Maze of Mist (HTB Cyber Apocalypse 2024; Hard)](../../reference-catalog.md#c16). Retains Maze of Mist's tiny native surface and privilege-sensitive exploitation objective.
