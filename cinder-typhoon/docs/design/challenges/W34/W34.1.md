## Challenge description — player-facing

Establish which planning consumer receives your controlled estimate and demonstrate a harmless change reaching it without altering raw measurements.

## Story purpose

Connects the optional elite capability to a real business decision.

## Prerequisites and starting position

**Required access or evidence:** ([PROCESS_INTERPRETATION](../../capability-routes.json) AND [W23.3](../W23/W23.3.md)) OR ([PROCESS_INTERPRETATION](../../capability-routes.json) AND [W27.3](../W27/W27.3.md)).

**Starting material:** The earned planning-view capability, current process interpretation, the planner's input/refresh records, and independent observations. This reporting-only stage needs no live control client, operating sequence, or release plan.

**Campaign references:** No separately named capability output.

## Completion and downstream use

Demonstrate the bound consumer effect and its actual input/refresh context. The earlier estimator or program compromise earns no additional credit here.
