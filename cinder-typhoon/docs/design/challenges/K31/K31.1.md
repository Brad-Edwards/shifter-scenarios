## Challenge description — player-facing

Recover the scheduler's maintenance policy and establish which identity its different operations actually evaluate.

## Story purpose

Separates job ownership from the identity of a running workload.

## Prerequisites and starting position

**Required access or evidence:** [K14.2](../K14/K14.2.md) OR [K09.4](../K09/K09.4.md).

**Starting material:** The maintenance scheduling contract, workload records, classification policy, and a protected field archive.

**Campaign references:** No separately named capability output.

## Completion and downstream use

Recover the protected policy and demonstrate the relevant identity distinction through a scoped operation. A policy read alone is insufficient.

## Difficulty and inspiration

A bounded comparison of documented identity contexts.

**Inspiration:** [ecs_efs_attack (CloudGoat; Hard challenge lab)](../../reference-catalog.md#c29). Adapts CloudGoat ecs_efs_attack's workload, identity, and resource-policy composition; its published tier is Hard.
