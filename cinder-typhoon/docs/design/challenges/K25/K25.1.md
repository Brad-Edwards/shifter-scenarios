## Challenge description — player-facing

Recover the ARWC support dossier and its retained attachment. Find out why the same maintenance ticket keeps returning to the developer.

## Story purpose

Introduces the customer and recurring support dispute through a complete document recovery.

## Prerequisites and starting position

**Required access or evidence:** [G01 — K01.1](../K01/K01.1.md).

**Starting material:** Repository-visible customer integration records, the current connector manifest, and references to the older ARWC support dossier.

**Campaign references:** No separately named capability output.

## Completion and downstream use

Recover the dossier and attachment as one disclosure. Their historical narrative is useful but not required to reconcile the current integration in K25.2.

## Difficulty and inspiration

A small customer-document recovery with a clear repository lead.

**Inspiration:** Original challenge for this campaign.
