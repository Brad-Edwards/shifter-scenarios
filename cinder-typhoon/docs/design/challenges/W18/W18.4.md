## Challenge description — player-facing

Map both Cairn Reach outlet gates from native register channels to PLC values, OPC UA identities, historian series, and a time-aligned physical observation.

## Story purpose

Completes a real engineering-to-field mapping for the two-outlet process.

## Prerequisites and starting position

**Required access or evidence:** ([W18.3](W18.3.md) AND [W17.OBS](../../capability-routes.json)).

**Starting material:** The verified CRR-IO-R364 register, W17.OBS, the historian tag catalog, and good time-aligned position and flow history.

**Campaign references:** `W18.MAP`.

## Completion and downstream use

Establish the complete A/B position-and-flow mapping for outlet group OG-CRR-02 and prove it with one good, time-aligned native observation. This supplies W18.MAP and MAPPING without issuing a command or changing process state. W19.4 remains an alternative path to the same operating context capability.

## Difficulty and inspiration

A small but sustained join across the register, catalog, prior live observation, and four time-aligned product series preserves the Medium allocation. The task requires physical consistency, not just matching names.

**Inspiration:** Original challenge for this campaign.

**Technical grounding:** [Modbus Application Protocol V1.1b3](https://www.modbus.org/modbus-specifications), [OPC UA Part 4 DataValue](https://reference.opcfoundation.org/specs/OPC-10000-4/7.11), and the [Telegraf OPC UA input](https://github.com/influxdata/telegraf/tree/master/plugins/inputs/opcua).

## Technical design


### Surface and normal behavior

The installed Cairn Reach OT products own this part of **Which outlet answers**. The exact initial state is: CRR-IO-R364 and the native catalog map both gates' position and flow channels, while time-aligned good product samples show each measured flow agrees with its corresponding measured position.

Native bindings:

- `a-engineering.a-engineering/filesystem:/home/fieldlink/CairnReach/Briefs/W18/tag-register.csv`
- `a-engineering.a-historian/PostgreSQL:public.historian_tag_catalog`
- `a-engineering.a-historian/PostgreSQL:public.process_history`
- `a-control.a-instruments/Modbus-TCP:outlet-A-and-B-input-registers`

The image-owned W18 native I/O register and history-provenance note are starting material only. OpenPLC, OPC UA, Modbus TCP, the historian catalog, and retained process history remain the authoritative sources; no private service-loader route duplicates them.

### Vulnerability and intended solution

This is a bounded operating observation and interpretation task, not a privilege flaw. The participant must use the installed products to reconcile the installed register with the running project and native catalog, distinguish Modbus PDU offsets from IEC addresses, interpret source, collection, and ingestion time with quality, and map both outlet channels from time-aligned native observations. A copied value, inferred default, or synthetic API response does not establish the result.

The required result is: Establish the complete A/B position-and-flow mapping for outlet group OG-CRR-02 and prove it with one good, time-aligned native observation. This supplies W18.MAP and MAPPING without issuing a command or changing process state. W19.4 remains an alternative path to the same operating context capability.

### Evidence and completion

Completion compares the participant's observation with fresh, read-only product records. Product evidence is supplied by the range, never by the participant, and the completion path cannot write a tag, issue a command, change controller state, or create its own product evidence.

Evidence sources are `features.a-engineering.a-engineering`, `features.a-engineering.a-historian`, `features.a-control.a-instruments`. A wrong register digest, project revision, point identity, scale, time, quality, or outlet mapping is rejected.

### Boundaries and persistence

Supplies W18.MAP and MAPPING. The task is read-only and creates no process mutation to retain or reset. Ordinary historian retention continues independently. Infrastructure recovery and submission adjudication are external to the SDL.

The behavior is limited to the installed Cairn Reach products, the named asset, and the supplied participant material. It provides no alternate challenge service, simulated completion endpoint, or authority not already present in the OT environment.

### Author checks

Verify the healthy baseline through the participant workstation. Complete the card using only the declared native products and image-owned starting material. Confirm that a matching post-action product snapshot accepts the observation, while a wrong asset, stale observation, mismatched native identity, participant-supplied product evidence, or missing required product source prevents completion. Confirm the flow does not upload, start, stop, force, or otherwise change controller state and reset leaves no challenge-created process state.
