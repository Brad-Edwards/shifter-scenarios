# KeplerOps challenge briefs

104 briefs across 31 operations. The player begins on the developer's
workstation. FieldKest's support, build, identity, cloud, package, and assistant
workflows provide both local objectives and routes toward the customer.

The recurring ARWC maintenance ticket connects those branches. Optional
advanced work has its own conclusions and later uses; it does not become a
hidden requirement for the ordinary customer transition.

Difficulty is conditional on the named prerequisites. All 104 cards have
complete five-part technical designs; all three hint sections remain empty.
