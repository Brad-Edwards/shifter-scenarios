## Challenge description — player-facing

Recover usable access to the support application and retrieve the session's protected handover.

## Story purpose

Gives the operator a second approachable supplier identity.

## Prerequisites and starting position

**Required access or evidence:** [G01 — K01.1](../K01/K01.1.md).

**Starting material:** The repository's support-client material, retained session artifacts, and ordinary support-account workflows.

**Campaign references:** `B01`.

## Completion and downstream use

Recover and demonstrate the limited support session. This supplies B01; no account-recovery or advanced identity task is required for that capability.

## Difficulty and inspiration

One retained application artifact and its scoped use.

**Inspiration:** Original challenge for this campaign.

## Technical design


### Surface and normal behavior
`fieldlink-support` (osTicket 1.18.4) owns this part of the operation. The k-support Python service is a facade and is NOT used for K17. The real surface is `support.keplerops.test` proxied to osTicket at `fieldlink-support:80`.

Its starting state: `maya.ranscombe` is a staff agent in osTicket with an Expanded Access role. SUP-2841 is a ticket in osTicket assigned to maya. A private internal note (the K17.1 handover) is seeded on SUP-2841, visible only to staff. The participant's starting material contains a retained osTicket session artifact for maya.ranscombe.

The handover note contains: case identifier, customer (ARWC), asset, connector revision, export reference, and transition context.

### Vulnerability and intended solution
The participant has a retained osTicket access artifact for maya.ranscombe (session cookie or staff credentials) in their starting material. Using this, they access osTicket as maya.ranscombe and read the private internal note on SUP-2841.

The intended solution:
1. Find maya.ranscombe's retained osTicket session or credentials in the starting material.
2. Authenticate to osTicket at `support.keplerops.test` as maya.ranscombe (staff panel login or retained OSTSESSID).
3. Navigate to SUP-2841's internal notes — the private handover note is visible to staff.
4. The handover contains: CAS-MAYA-2841, EXP-2841, SCHED-2841, ARWC export transition details.

### Evidence and completion
An authenticated staff session for maya.ranscombe in osTicket, and the private internal note content including the case ID, customer, export reference, and handover context. The osTicket session and note read are the K17.1 evidence.

### Boundaries and persistence

Supplies B01. Participant-created service state persists for the life of the participant world and ordinary retries do not erase it. The action is limited to the exact records, identities, routes, and destination effects declared here; unrelated tenants and services remain denied. Infrastructure recovery and submission adjudication are outside this in-world contract.

### Author checks
Verify that maya.ranscombe is a staff agent in osTicket, that SUP-2841 has a private internal note with the handover content, that the note is accessible only to staff (not the ticket submitter), and that the participant's starting material contains a valid retained access artifact for maya.ranscombe.
