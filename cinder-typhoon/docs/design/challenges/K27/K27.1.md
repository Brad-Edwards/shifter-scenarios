## Challenge description — player-facing

Bind a support diagnostic job to the active ARWC connector despite the support account's ordinary customer restrictions.

## Story purpose

Establishes the diagnostic route's customer-specific action.

## Prerequisites and starting position

**Required access or evidence:** ([B01 — K17.1](../K17/K17.1.md) AND [G02 — K25.2](../K25/K25.2.md)).

**Starting material:** The limited support session, current customer binding, diagnostic-job records, and the ordinary support delivery workflow.

**Campaign references:** `B02`.

## Completion and downstream use

Create the correctly bound unauthorized diagnostic job. This supplies B02; changing a display name or choosing another tenant does not count.

## Difficulty and inspiration

A bounded object-authorization and customer-binding problem.

**Inspiration:** Original challenge for this campaign.
