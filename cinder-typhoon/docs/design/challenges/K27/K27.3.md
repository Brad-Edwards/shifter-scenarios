## Challenge description — player-facing

Deliver the changed diagnostic through the support workflow and demonstrate its execution inside ARWC's corporate connector.

## Story purpose

Provides the same earned customer transition as the package route.

## Prerequisites and starting position

**Required access or evidence:** [K27.2](K27.2.md).

**Starting material:** The limited support session, current customer binding, diagnostic-job records, and the ordinary support delivery workflow.

**Campaign references:** `B04`.

## Completion and downstream use

Demonstrate actual customer-side execution with the intended tenant and job binding. This supplies B04 and CORPORATE, equivalent in scope to A04.

The conclusion pairs the submitted diagnostic job with the ARWC connector's actual changed output and customer identity. The familiar maintenance ticket appears in its customer context under ARWC's name. This route earns the same visible arrival as K26.2 through its own execution evidence; it does not require completing the package route.

## Difficulty and inspiration

A bounded delivery and execution-binding task; no advanced identity chain is required.

**Inspiration:** Original challenge for this campaign.
