## Challenge description — player-facing

Deliver the changed diagnostic through the support workflow and demonstrate its execution inside ARWC's corporate connector.

## Story purpose

Provides the same earned customer transition as the package route.

## Prerequisites and starting position

**Required access or evidence:** [K27.2](K27.2.md).

**Starting material:** The limited support session, current customer binding, diagnostic-job records, and the ordinary support delivery workflow.

**Campaign references:** `B04`.

## Completion and downstream use

Demonstrate actual customer-side execution with the intended tenant and job binding. This supplies B04 and CORPORATE, equivalent in scope to A04.

The conclusion pairs the submitted diagnostic job with the ARWC connector's actual changed output and customer identity. The familiar maintenance ticket appears in its customer context under ARWC's name. This route earns the same visible arrival as K26.2 through its own execution evidence; it does not require completing the package route.

## Difficulty and inspiration

A bounded delivery and execution-binding task; no advanced identity chain is required.

**Inspiration:** Original challenge for this campaign.

## Technical design


### Surface and normal behavior
`k-support` owns this part of the operation. Its starting state is exact: Connector diagnostic intake accepts signed job package and returns invocation/receipt. The owning service uses the listener, route, identity, authorization, storage, and audit contract declared in the corresponding world module. Records that are not explicitly mutable remain immutable. The private service-loader contract `content.keplerops-k-support.k27-3-the-other-side-of-support` freezes the request, denial, state-transition, evidence, and any exploit-critical implementation details.

### Vulnerability and intended solution
The intended path is limited to the authored software, data, configuration, identity, permission, or state described above. The participant must exercise that real condition through the declared service interface and produce this result: Actual bounded diagnostic executes for active tenant with changed output. Merely describing the condition, editing a local copy, or presenting a value obtained from another surface does not satisfy the contract.

### Evidence and completion
Completion is observed by the owning service from the fresh result and its immutable audit correlation. The evidence binds the participant, accepted identity or job, exact input and output identifiers, relevant revision or digest, and destination-side observation where a second service consumes the result. Objective evaluation does not create the result or grant authority.

### Boundaries and persistence
Supplies B04 and CORPORATE. Participant-created service state persists for the life of the participant world and ordinary retries do not erase it. The selected route cannot act on unrelated tenants, records, packages, identities, jobs, or services. Infrastructure recovery and submission adjudication are outside this in-world contract.

### Author checks
Verify the normal baseline, the exact intended path, and the fresh evidence described above. Reject a wrong identifier, revision, digest, tenant, identity, stale result, or cross-participant reference. Verify unrelated records and permissions remain unchanged and that the result remains recognizable and usable by every declared downstream consumer.
