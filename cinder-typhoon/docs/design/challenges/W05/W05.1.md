## Challenge description — player-facing

Recover Mira Vale's exposed starting arrangements and identify the temporary account and approved work assignment.

## Story purpose

Introduces the temporary employee through an ordinary misplaced onboarding document.

## Starting position

The earned customer context provides only supplier access. The two ordinary
onboarding artifacts are misplaced in the external Nextcloud document set.
Their account material is distinct from supplier, workforce administration,
business applications and plant control.

## Completion and downstream use

Recover Mira's account, employee number and approved assignment. These facts support the independent roster check and first-sign-in work that follow.

## Native surface and normal behavior

An ordinary Nextcloud starter document carries the real temporary Keycloak account and initial password. It contains no privileged session or application token. Its mistaken external document placement is the exposure.

## Completion and product evidence

Recover START-MIRA-2026, employee TMP-4821, the native username and the approved assignment from the actual file. Bind submitted observations to its native file identity and content hash. Do not disclose credentials in the score record.

## Difficulty and research

Easy: locate and read one ordinary onboarding document. The credential is actual limited account material, with first-login password change enforced by Keycloak.

Primary sources: [Keycloak 26.4.7 administration](https://www.keycloak.org/docs/26.4.7/server_admin/)
and [native Admin REST API](https://www.keycloak.org/docs-api/latest/rest-api/).
The upstream documented first-login actions and delegated view/manage roles
provide the mechanisms; no replacement identity service implements them.

## Hints

### Hint 1

The external handover contains ordinary starting arrangements alongside supplier documents. Look for the temporary employee named in the case.

### Hint 2

A starting letter identifies an actual work account and initial credential; it does not contain a ready-made privileged session.

### Hint 3

Read the employee starting document and retain its employee, account and assignment references with the native file identity.

## Persistence and retirement

Supported partial import skips existing users. Ordinary restarts do not seed
identities or reset passwords, sessions, groups or role changes. Pristine reset
rebuilds native product state and reimports the same coherent accounts/data.
Keep the old identity facade only while W06/W07 still consume its legacy
planner session, then remove those retired routes/assets in the same change.
