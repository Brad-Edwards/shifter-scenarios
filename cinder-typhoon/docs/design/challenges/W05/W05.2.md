## Challenge description — player-facing

Find Mira Vale in the activation roster and establish the native identity and assignment approved for that employee.

## Story purpose

Corroborates the exposed starter pack against an independently exported native identity record.

## Starting position

The earned customer context provides only supplier access. The two ordinary
onboarding artifacts are misplaced in the external Nextcloud document set.
Their account material is distinct from supplier, workforce administration,
business applications and plant control.

## Completion and downstream use

Recover Mira's native user identity, status, department and approved assignment. The matched identity anchors the first-sign-in and authority check.

## Native surface and normal behavior

The Nextcloud activation roster is exported from actual Keycloak user representations and UUIDs after supported native import. Existing workforce records supply ordinary surrounding staff rows. It is separate from the starter document.

## Completion and product evidence

Recover the enabled account's native user UUID, employee number, approved assignment and department from the real roster. Match them to independently collected Keycloak identity metadata; no activation-code endpoint is imitated.

## Difficulty and research

Easy: identify one employee row in a normal activation export and interpret its identity/status fields. No privilege-changing action is required.

Primary sources: [Keycloak 26.4.7 administration](https://www.keycloak.org/docs/26.4.7/server_admin/)
and [native Admin REST API](https://www.keycloak.org/docs-api/latest/rest-api/).
The upstream documented first-login actions and delegated view/manage roles
provide the mechanisms; no replacement identity service implements them.

## Hints

### Hint 1

An activation roster is retained separately from the starting letter. Each employee record includes its native identity references.

### Hint 2

Correlate the employee number, username and identity UUID rather than relying on a similar display name or a copied assignment label.

### Hint 3

Find the temporary employee in the activation export and compare its enabled state, assignment and native identity with the starting arrangements.

## Persistence and retirement

Supported partial import skips existing users. Ordinary restarts do not seed
identities or reset passwords, sessions, groups or role changes. Pristine reset
rebuilds native product state and reimports the same coherent accounts/data.
Keep the old identity facade only while W06/W07 still consume its legacy
planner session, then remove those retired routes/assets in the same change.
