# W05.3: Temporary, with the wrong rights

| Field | Value |

## Challenge description—player-facing

Complete the temporary employee's first sign-in and establish the directory authority that exceeds the approved account scope.

## Story purpose

Turns the two onboarding records into a real limited account with an excessive native directory-read role.

## Starting position

The earned customer context provides only supplier access. The two ordinary
onboarding artifacts are misplaced in the external Nextcloud document set.
Their account material is distinct from supplier, workforce administration,
business applications and plant control.

## Completion and downstream use

Complete Mira's first sign-in and prove both the unintended directory read and the remaining mutation boundary. The resulting ordinary workforce identity supports later business-system work only where explicitly granted.

## Native surface and normal behavior

Keycloak owns first-login password change, sessions, OAuth clients, role mappings and Admin Console/API permissions. Mira mistakenly has the built-in realm-management view-users/query-users read authority. She has no realm-admin/manage-users grant, no native business-application session and no OT authority.

## Completion and product evidence

Use the actual account, complete its required first-login action, correlate its subject with both onboarding records, then use the stock Admin Console/API to read a different staff identity and admin-only directory attributes. Prove user-management mutation and unrelated authority remain denied. Native actor/session/event and role records establish the result; the checker cannot create accounts, tokens, role mappings or accepted actions.

## Difficulty and research

Medium: joins independent identity artifacts, interprets the difference between account access and delegated administration, and crosses that actual client/authorization boundary. Merely quoting a role or using a pre-created administrator is insufficient.

Primary sources: [Keycloak 26.4.7 administration](https://www.keycloak.org/docs/26.4.7/server_admin/)
and [native Admin REST API](https://www.keycloak.org/docs-api/latest/rest-api/).
The upstream documented first-login actions and delegated view/manage roles
provide the mechanisms; no replacement identity service implements them.

## Hints

### Hint 1

The starting arrangements point to the actual identity system account. Complete its normal first-sign-in requirements before evaluating the access it provides.

### Hint 2

Account self-service and delegated administration are different client and permission boundaries. Compare the actual native role scope with the approved work assignment.

### Hint 3

Use the native identity console and documented administration interface to establish permitted directory reads and denied management actions under that same limited employee identity.

## Persistence and retirement

Supported partial import skips existing users. Ordinary restarts do not seed
identities or reset passwords, sessions, groups or role changes. Pristine reset
rebuilds native product state and reimports the same coherent accounts/data.
Keep the old identity facade only while W06/W07 still consume its legacy
planner session, then remove those retired routes/assets in the same change.
