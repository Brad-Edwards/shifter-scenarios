## Challenge description — player-facing

Establish the temporary employee's usable corporate identity with the restricted assignment the onboarding workflow should deny.

## Story purpose

Turns onboarding data into a meaningful identity compromise.

## Prerequisites and starting position

**Required access or evidence:** ([W05.1](W05.1.md) AND [W05.2](W05.2.md)).

**Starting material:** The temporary-staff onboarding portal, two separately exposed onboarding artifacts, and its ordinary assignment workflow.

**Campaign references:** No separately named capability output.

## Completion and downstream use

Demonstrate the limited staff identity against its protected assignment. Both records support the binding; copying their names is insufficient. This is one route into W06.

## Difficulty and inspiration

A bounded role-assignment and identity join.

**Inspiration:** Original challenge for this campaign.
