## Challenge description — player-facing

Execute the bounded opening and establish its actual effect on usable reserve. Show the replacement cost and restrictions that follow from the lost water.

## Story purpose

Completes the main story with gates moving and a credible financial consequence.

## Prerequisites and starting position

**Required access or evidence:** [W30.1](W30.1.md).

**Starting material:** The earned scoped control client, validated current process evidence, and the bounded release/consequence plan.

**Campaign references:** `W30.RESULT`.

## Completion and downstream use

Demonstrate the opening and resulting volume change through independent process observations. This supplies W30.RESULT. A command acknowledgement or altered report does not establish the result; the release remains within the normal channel envelope.

The conclusion follows the verified effect through three connected facts: the identified reservoir gates moved, usable reserve fell by the observed amount, and that loss produces the calculated replacement cost and water restrictions. ARWC's resulting continuity notice makes the economic consequence explicit. The gate state, before-and-after reserve, cost, and restrictions all belong to this operator's same completed release; success does not require waiting for an additional delay.

The earlier maintenance ticket and the outlet the operator identified anchor the ending in familiar evidence. Additional contextual records appear only when their source records are present. The fictional consequence remains financial loss and restrictions, with the dam intact and no catastrophic flooding. The authored notice expresses the existing consequence calculation; it does not require a new procurement, messaging, or control capability.

## Difficulty and inspiration

Execution and effect reconciliation under the established plan, with no additional elite gate.

**Inspiration:** Original challenge for this campaign.

### Evidence and completion

Evidence joins the independently owned service observations listed below; all are required for the same tenant instance, request correlation, identifiers, revision, and interval. Completion is evaluated from fresh service-owned evidence after the action. It cannot issue the identity, create the mutation, move a simulated actuator, or fabricate a downstream acceptance needed to prove itself.

Evidence sources are `features.a-control.a-reservoir`, `features.a-control.a-instruments`. A wrong identifier, stale revision, replayed one-time value, different checkpoint, or cross-tenant artifact is rejected.

### Boundaries and persistence

Supplies W30.RESULT and the main bounded consequence. Tenant-scoped mutations, sessions that have not expired, generated artifacts, and audits persist across ordinary retries and later sessions. Initial immutable records remain unchanged unless the named route explicitly permits a field-level revision. Infrastructure recovery and submission adjudication are external to the SDL.

The behavior is limited to the named fictional objects and bounded service operation. It provides no general shell, arbitrary network target, unrelated district access, real process interface, or authority not expressly declared by the card.

### Author checks

Build and test the normal baseline first. Exercise the exact authored path and verify the named result plus its immutable audit. Test wrong authority, wrong object or district, stale revision, replay, another tenant instance, and every declared constraint or unaffected record. Repeat the accepted action according to its idempotence rule and confirm ordinary retries preserve the result. For a joined result, verify each destination observed the same correlation and that removal of any independently owned source prevents completion.
