## Challenge description—player-facing

Execute the approved dispatcher request, prove the PLC and both outlets reached the requested flow, then take local control through FUXA and restore the normal A20/B20 baseline.

## Story purpose

Turns the new OT environment into a complete operator experience: remote request, controller decision, measured response, retained history, local priority, and safe handback.

## Prerequisites and starting position

**Required access or evidence:** [W30.1](W30.1.md).

**Starting material:** The exact request plan from W30.1, the installed MQTT and FUXA connections, and the healthy A20/B20 operating baseline.

**Campaign references:** `W30.RESULT`.

## Completion and downstream use

Demonstrate one completed automatic-flow request for `AST-CRR-017` through the authenticated MQTT dispatcher, native OpenPLC acceptance, and settled two-outlet field feedback at approximately 0.15 m³/s per outlet. Then use the local FUXA HMI to take priority and restore manual A20/B20, approximately 0.10 m³/s per outlet and 0.20 m³/s total, with healthy I/O, valid permissives, no motion, and no travel fault. This supplies W30.RESULT.

Broker receipt, durable dispatcher receipt, PLC acceptance, and settled equipment feedback are distinct. The dispatcher journal, native PLC decision history, and process historian must agree on the same request, sequence, and interval. Completion never issues a request, retries an uncertain result, or restores the plant on the participant's behalf.

## Difficulty and inspiration

Medium end-to-end operation and reconciliation across MQTT, the dispatcher journal, OpenPLC, FUXA, remote I/O, and PostgreSQL history. The supplied plan fixes the safe target so the work is integration and evidence, not setpoint invention.

**Inspiration:** Original challenge for this campaign.

## Technical design


### Surface and normal behavior

The installed Cairn Reach OT products own this part of **Operate and hand back**. The exact initial state is: One authenticated remote request must progress through durable receipt, native OpenPLC acceptance, and settled two-outlet feedback near 0.15 m3/s each; a later local FUXA request must take priority and restore manual A20/B20, total flow near 0.20 m3/s, healthy I/O, clear motion, and clear travel faults.

Native bindings:

- `a-engineering.a-hmi/Mosquitto:arwc/crr/commands/operator-and-outcomes/operator`
- `a-engineering.a-historian/PostgreSQL:public.dispatch_history-and-command_history`
- `a-control.a-reservoir/OpenPLC:RemoteAccepted-Owner-AutoMode`
- `a-control.a-instruments/Modbus-TCP:two-outlet-position-and-flow-feedback`
- `a-engineering.a-hmi/FUXA:crr-controls-local-restore`
- `a-engineering.a-historian/PostgreSQL:public.process_history`

The image-owned W30 plan fixes one safe remote target and one local restoration. Mosquitto, the durable dispatcher journal, OpenPLC, FUXA, PostgreSQL history, and remote-I/O feedback remain authoritative; no private challenge service duplicates or drives them.

### Vulnerability and intended solution

This is a bounded operating integration task, not a privilege flaw. The participant must use the installed products to enable the installed remote selection, issue one fresh non-retained QoS 1 request, distinguish broker and durable receipt from PLC acceptance and settled feedback, correlate the same request and sequence across native history, then use local FUXA priority to restore manual A20/B20. A locally invented response, synthetic API, request acknowledgement, or copied practice sequence does not establish the result.

The required result is: Demonstrate one completed automatic-flow request for `AST-CRR-017` through the authenticated MQTT dispatcher, native OpenPLC acceptance, and settled two-outlet field feedback at approximately 0.15 m³/s per outlet. Then use the local FUXA HMI to take priority and restore manual A20/B20, approximately 0.10 m³/s per outlet and 0.20 m³/s total, with healthy I/O, valid permissives, no motion, and no travel fault. This supplies W30.RESULT. Broker receipt, durable dispatcher receipt, PLC acceptance, and settled equipment feedback are distinct. The dispatcher journal, native PLC decision history, and process historian must agree on the same request, sequence, and interval. Completion never issues a request, retries an uncertain result, or restores the plant on the participant's behalf.

### Evidence and completion

Completion reads a range-supplied snapshot of the installed dispatcher contract, journal, native PLC decisions, and process history as applicable to the card. The participant cannot supply product evidence. The evaluator cannot issue a broker request, write a controller tag, move an outlet, retry an uncertain request, take local priority, or create product evidence.

Evidence sources are `features.a-engineering.a-hmi`, `features.a-engineering.a-historian`, `features.a-control.a-reservoir`, `features.a-control.a-instruments`. A wrong request identity, asset, principal, mode, expiry, transition order, PLC sequence, ownership, permissive, product time, process quality, measured feedback, or restored baseline is rejected.

### Boundaries and persistence

Supplies W30.RESULT as a verified bounded operation with the normal baseline restored. The dispatcher retains the request and transitions as its normal audit. OpenPLC and the historian retain native decisions and process measurements. Completion requires the later local HMI acceptance and measured return to manual A20/B20, approximately 0.10 m3/s per outlet and 0.20 m3/s total, healthy I/O, valid permissives, no motion, and clear travel faults. Infrastructure recovery and submission adjudication are external to the SDL.

The behavior is limited to AST-CRR-017 and CRR-OP-W30-R1. It grants no arbitrary MQTT topic, OPC UA write, engineering force, program upload, interlock bypass, automatic retry after uncertainty, or alternate control endpoint.

### Author checks

Begin at the qualified A20/B20 baseline. Execute the exact remote automatic-flow request through the participant workstation, then restore through local FUXA priority. Require ordered durable dispatcher transitions, the matching native PLC acceptance, remote ownership, good-quality two-outlet settled feedback, a later local HMI acceptance, and the fully restored baseline. Reject wrong or duplicate identities, conflicts, expiry, rejection, uncertainty, supersession before completion, missing sources, stale or bad-quality observations, participant-supplied product evidence, or failure to restore. Reset must return the same qualified baseline without replaying a dispatcher request or changing product identities and trust material.
