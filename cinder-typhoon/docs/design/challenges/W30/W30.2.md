## Challenge description — player-facing

Execute the bounded opening and establish its actual effect on usable reserve. Show the replacement cost and restrictions that follow from the lost water.

## Story purpose

Completes the main story with gates moving and a credible financial consequence.

## Prerequisites and starting position

**Required access or evidence:** [W30.1](W30.1.md).

**Starting material:** The earned scoped control client, validated current process evidence, and the bounded release/consequence plan.

**Campaign references:** `W30.RESULT`.

## Completion and downstream use

Demonstrate the opening and resulting volume change through independent process observations. This supplies W30.RESULT. A command acknowledgement or altered report does not count; the release remains within the normal channel envelope.

The conclusion follows the verified effect through three connected facts: the identified reservoir gates moved, usable reserve fell by the observed amount, and that loss produces the calculated replacement cost and water restrictions. ARWC's resulting continuity notice makes the economic consequence explicit. The gate state, before-and-after reserve, cost, and restrictions all belong to this player's same completed release; success does not require waiting for a cinematic sequence or completing another challenge.

The earlier maintenance ticket and the outlet the player identified anchor the ending in familiar evidence. Callbacks to optional investigations appear only when earned. The fictional consequence remains financial loss and restrictions, with the dam intact and no catastrophic flooding. The authored notice expresses the existing consequence calculation; it does not require a new procurement, messaging, or control capability.

## Difficulty and inspiration

Execution and effect reconciliation under the established plan, with no additional elite gate.

**Inspiration:** Original challenge for this campaign.
