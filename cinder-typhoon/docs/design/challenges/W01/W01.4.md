## Challenge description—player-facing

Correct the supplier handback for the pending Cairn Reach inspection and record that the work is in progress.

## Starting position

The customer-owned handover provides the genuine Veybridge supplier account
and its limited external document access. It grants no workforce, planning
administration or OT authority.

## Native surface and normal behavior

ERPNext stores the real maintenance Project and Task. Jules has an overbroad write DocShare for one supplier handback Task, without a staff or administrator role. Native Task versioning is enabled through supported metadata configuration.

## Completion and product evidence

Locate the correct Task by its project, case and inspection references. Set status to Working and retain the confirmed inspection reference in its description. Require the native Version entry attributed to Jules, the expected field changes and unchanged neighboring records. The observer never creates the mutation or its audit.

Evidence collection is read-only. It cannot create a share, change a document,
issue a product session, or fabricate a product history entry. Native product
state and submitted observations must refer to the same range instance.

## Difficulty and research

Medium: multiple record bindings must be reconciled before using a specific native per-record write grant. Merely possessing a supplier password or naming a Task does not satisfy completion.

Primary reference: [upstream product documentation](https://docs.frappe.io/erpnext/v14/user/manual/en/using-erpnext/document-versioning).

interfaces. Retained-volume restarts preserve product state. Explicit native IT
reset restores the seeded accounts, documents, history and per-record grants;
