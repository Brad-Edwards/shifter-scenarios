## Challenge description — player-facing

Make the corporate handover service replace the planner's inspection-summary attachment with Cinder's copy while preserving the original inspection and assignee.

## Story purpose

Gives arrival a small action payoff before the next pivot.

## Prerequisites and starting position

**Required access or evidence:** [CORPORATE](../../capability-routes.json).

**Starting material:** The earned FieldKest corporate context, local integration records, and ARWC's ordinary internal application references.

**Campaign references:** No separately named capability output.

## Completion and downstream use

Cause the server-side attachment replacement on the designated inspection handover, with the assignee and inspection binding unchanged. Connector execution alone or editing a local copy is insufficient.

## Difficulty and inspiration

A bounded corporate application action with an explicit record scope.

**Inspiration:** Original challenge for this campaign.
