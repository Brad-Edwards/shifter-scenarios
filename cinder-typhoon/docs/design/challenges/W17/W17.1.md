## Challenge description — player-facing

Record the reservoir's current measured state from the live operator view. Keep measured values separate from the controller's accepted settings and bind both to one UTC observation time.

## Story purpose

Provides an approachable first result after the OT visibility pivot.

## Prerequisites and starting position

**Required access or evidence:** [OT_READ](../../capability-routes.json).

**Starting material:** The Cairn Reach engineering desktop, the W17 live-observation sheet, and read-only access to FUXA and process history.

**Campaign references:** No separately named capability output.

## Completion and downstream use

Record the live level, usable volume, two outlet positions and flows, total flow, and accepted settings for the same product-owned observation time. A copied or fixed example is insufficient.

## Difficulty and inspiration

A clear live view and one bounded distinction between measured process values and accepted controller settings preserve the Easy allocation. Product login and navigation do not count as difficulty.

**Inspiration:** Original challenge for this campaign.

## Technical design


### Surface and normal behavior

The installed Cairn Reach OT products own this part of **The reservoir's present tense**. The exact initial state is: FUXA's Cairn Reach overview displays live level, usable volume, two measured outlet positions and flows, measured total flow, actual mode, command owner, field state, and motion authorization; accepted controller settings remain separately labelled.

Native bindings:

- `a-engineering.a-hmi/FUXA:crr-overview`
- `a-engineering.a-historian/PostgreSQL:public.process_history`

The image-owned W17 field sheet and engineering reference are starting material only. FUXA, OpenPLC, OPC UA, PostgreSQL history, and the remote-I/O measurements remain the authoritative sources; no private service-loader route duplicates them.

### Vulnerability and intended solution

This is a bounded operating observation and interpretation task, not a privilege flaw. The participant must use the installed products to distinguish measured state from accepted settings, read actual controller state, bind one physical instrument across its engineering identities, and correlate a short process interval. A copied value, inferred default, or synthetic API response does not establish the result.

The required result is: Record the live level, usable volume, two outlet positions and flows, total flow, and accepted settings for the same product-owned observation time. A copied or fixed example is insufficient.

### Evidence and completion

Completion compares the participant's observation with fresh, read-only product records. Product evidence is supplied by the range, never by the participant, and the completion path cannot write a tag, issue a command, change controller state, or create its own product evidence.

Evidence sources are `features.a-engineering.a-hmi`, `features.a-engineering.a-historian`. A wrong asset, point identity, unit, time interval, quality state, or inconsistent field relationship is rejected.

### Boundaries and persistence

The result remains local evidence unless a downstream workflow explicitly names it. The task is read-only and creates no process mutation to retain or reset. Ordinary historian retention continues independently. Infrastructure recovery and submission adjudication are external to the SDL.

The behavior is limited to the installed Cairn Reach products, the named asset, and the supplied participant material. It provides no alternate challenge service, simulated completion endpoint, or authority not already present in the OT environment.

### Author checks

Verify the healthy baseline through the participant workstation. Complete the card using only the declared native products and image-owned starting material. Confirm that a matching post-action product snapshot accepts the observation, while a wrong asset, stale observation, mismatched native identity, participant-supplied product evidence, or missing required product source prevents completion. Confirm the flow does not upload, start, stop, force, or otherwise change controller state and reset leaves no challenge-created process state.
