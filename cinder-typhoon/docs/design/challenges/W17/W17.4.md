## Challenge description—player-facing

Correlate one bounded live outlet B observation across FUXA, the OPC UA controller point, and PostgreSQL process history. Establish that position and measured flow belong to the same interval and point.

## Story purpose

Makes the first operator observation visibly agree across real OT products and field response.

## Prerequisites and starting position

**Required access or evidence:** ([W17.1](W17.1.md) AND [W17.2](W17.2.md) AND [W17.3](W17.3.md)).

**Starting material:** The W17 field sheet, the verified outlet B point from W17.3, and read-only access to FUXA, OPC UA, and process history. Use a 15-to-300-second interval; no process write is required.

**Campaign references:** `W17.OBS`.

## Completion and downstream use

Produce a time-bound trace whose FUXA variable, OPC UA NodeId, historian series, good sample count, flow summary, and field-position relationship all agree. This supplies W17.OBS without control authority.

## Difficulty and inspiration

A small evidence join across three native product views preserves the Medium allocation. The participant must choose one interval, align identities and source times, reject bad-quality samples, and check the physical position-to-flow relationship; ordinary tool navigation does not add difficulty.

**Inspiration:** Original challenge for this campaign.

## Technical design


### Surface and normal behavior

The installed Cairn Reach OT products own this card. The participant chooses a 15-to-300-second interval, queries `FlowBM3s` and `PositionBPct` from the historian, counts good samples, computes a flow summary, and verifies the position-to-flow relationship.

**Historian query** (`ot_history.process_samples`, which records all 50 OPC UA tags):

```sql
SELECT time,
  tags->>'variable' as variable,
  tags->>'series' as series,
  round((fields->>'value')::numeric, 4) as value,
  tags->>'unit' as unit
FROM ot_history.process_samples
WHERE tags->>'variable' IN ('FlowBM3s', 'PositionBPct')
  AND time BETWEEN '<interval_start>' AND '<interval_end>'
ORDER BY time, tags->>'variable';
```

Note: `public.process_history` only contains the aggregate tags (LevelM, FlowM3s, PositionPct) and does NOT include outlet B tags. Use `ot_history.process_samples` for FlowBM3s and PositionBPct.

**30-second example trace** (2026-11-16 17:16:32 to 17:17:01 UTC):
- FlowBM3s: 30 good samples, constant 0.1000 m³/s
- PositionBPct: 30 good samples, constant 20.00%
- Position-to-flow: 20% open → 0.100 m³/s = 20% of max capacity (0.5 m³/s) ✓

**Five identities (from W17.3):**
- FUXA variable: `FlowBM3s`
- OPC UA NodeId: `nsu=urn:arwc:crr:tags;s=CRR.FlowBM3s`
- Historian series: `flow_b_m3s`
- Unit: `m3/s`
- Historian table: `ot_history.process_samples`

Native bindings:
- `a-engineering.a-hmi/FUXA:FlowBM3s`
- `a-engineering.a-hmi/OPC-UA:nsu=urn:arwc:crr:tags;s=CRR.FlowBM3s`
- `a-engineering.a-historian/PostgreSQL:ot_history.process_samples`

### Vulnerability and intended solution

This is a bounded operating observation and cross-product correlation task. The participant must bind FlowBM3s across its FUXA display, OPC UA identity, and historian samples for a chosen 15-300 second interval, count good samples, summarize flow, and verify the position-flow relationship is physically consistent with the outlet capacity.

The required result is: Produce a time-bound trace whose FUXA variable, OPC UA NodeId, historian series, good sample count, flow summary, and field-position relationship all agree. This supplies W17.OBS without control authority.

### Evidence and completion

Completion compares the participant's observation with fresh, read-only product records. Product evidence is supplied by the range, never by the participant, and the completion path cannot write a tag, issue a command, change controller state, or create its own product evidence.

Evidence sources are `features.a-engineering.a-hmi`, `features.a-engineering.a-historian`, `features.a-control.a-instruments`. A wrong asset, point identity, unit, time interval, quality state, or inconsistent field relationship is rejected.

### Boundaries and persistence

Supplies W17.OBS without control authority. The task is read-only and creates no process mutation to retain or reset. Ordinary historian retention continues independently. Infrastructure recovery and submission adjudication are external to the SDL.

The behavior is limited to the installed Cairn Reach products, the named asset, and the supplied participant material. It provides no alternate challenge service, simulated completion endpoint, or authority not already present in the OT environment.

### Author checks

Verify the healthy baseline through the participant workstation. Complete the card using only the declared native products and image-owned starting material. Confirm that a matching post-action product snapshot accepts the observation, while a wrong asset, stale observation, mismatched native identity, participant-supplied product evidence, or missing required product source prevents completion. Confirm the flow does not upload, start, stop, force, or otherwise change controller state and reset leaves no challenge-created process state.
