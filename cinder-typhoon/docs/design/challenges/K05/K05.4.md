## Challenge description — player-facing

Demonstrate which dependency the check consumer really chooses from the available candidates. The answer must come from consumed behavior, not the release label.

## Story purpose

Distinguishes publisher intention from consumer behavior.

## Prerequisites and starting position

**Required access or evidence:** [K05.3](K05.3.md).

**Starting material:** The readable FieldKest package workspace, interface examples, release records, and an ordinary consumer-check facility.

**Campaign references:** No separately named capability output.

## Completion and downstream use

Make the intended check consumer identify its selected dependency through a fresh result. This is optional selection intelligence; K26 retains its own production delivery problem.

## Difficulty and inspiration

Requires a bounded experiment and interpretation of dependency selection.

**Inspiration:** Original challenge for this campaign.

## Technical design


### Surface and normal behavior
The isolated check consumer resolves the candidate dependencies using its real lock and range policy and emits a fresh resolution report.

### Vulnerability and intended solution
The release label is deliberately not authoritative. The intended solution is controlled behavioral validation of the reconciliation result through the consumer, not a guess from package names.

### Evidence and completion
The report binds resolved dependency, candidate set, consumer revision, and result digest; it is optional intelligence only.

### Boundaries and persistence

Optional selection intelligence Participant-created service state persists for the life of the participant world and ordinary retries do not erase it. The action is limited to the exact records, identities, routes, and destination effects declared here; unrelated tenants and services remain denied. Infrastructure recovery and submission adjudication are outside this in-world contract.

### Author checks
Verify labels can disagree with resolution, intended candidate is selected, incompatible candidates fail, a fresh receipt is required.
