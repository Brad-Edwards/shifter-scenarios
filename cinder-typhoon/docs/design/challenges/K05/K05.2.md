## Challenge description—player-facing

Recover the older connector release named in the support ticket. Establish how its native package deprecation relates to the retained customer maintenance record.

## Story purpose

Adds product history with its own information payoff.

## Prerequisites and starting position

**Required access or evidence:** [G01—K01.1](../K01/K01.1.md).

**Starting material:** The Verdaccio registry at `packages.keplerops.test` listing `@keplerops/fieldlink-connector` versions including 6.9.8 under `dist-tags.legacy`, and the `packages.release` route at `delivery.keplerops.test:8443/api/releases/REL-FLK-6.9.8-ARCHIVE`.

**Campaign references:** No separately named capability output.

## Completion and downstream use

Recover the actual retained package version, native deprecation notice and matching customer maintenance record. Carry its entitlement context into the existing compatibility investigation.

## Difficulty and inspiration

Native mechanisms: [npm package specifications](https://docs.npmjs.com/cli/v11/using-npm/package-spec/) and [Verdaccio package ACLs](https://www.verdaccio.org/docs/packages/). The existing Easy/Medium tier follows the bounded discovery or multi-record/behavior correlation required above.

## Technical design


### Surface and normal behavior
The support conversation refers to the retained 6.9.8 customer release. Verdaccio still stores its actual native npm version with a genuine deprecation notice; the separate customer rollout record carries its retained maintenance and entitlement context.

### Vulnerability and intended solution

The intended solution fetches the Verdaccio metadata for `@keplerops/fieldlink-connector` and finds 6.9.8 under `dist-tags.legacy`. The 6.9.8 version object carries a genuine `deprecated` field with the deprecation notice and a `dist.tarball` URL pointing to the downloadable archive. The participant downloads the tarball and fetches the release record `REL-FLK-6.9.8-ARCHIVE` from the `packages.release` route. That record has `status=yanked`, a `maintenance_note`, and a `compatibility` block for the retained entitlement investigation. Unknown versions return 404. A withdrawn customer rollout does not remove the package from the native registry.

### Evidence and completion
The native version/deprecation metadata, exact downloaded package and customer release record agree on package identity and version, and recover the maintenance context needed by the retained entitlement investigation.

### Boundaries and persistence

Optional history; active 7.4.2 stays visibly distinct Participant-created service state persists for the life of the participant world and ordinary retries do not erase it. The action is limited to the exact records, identities, routes, and destination effects declared here; unrelated tenants and services remain denied. Infrastructure recovery and submission adjudication are outside this in-world contract.

### Author checks
Verify that the real published 6.9.8 version remains readable, native deprecation is present, customer record identity matches, unknown versions fail normally and no fictitious registry view is used.
