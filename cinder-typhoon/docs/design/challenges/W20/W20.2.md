## Challenge description — player-facing

Recover the last recorded outlet inspection from the retained maintenance image and establish whether its entry is valid under the image's record rules.

## Story purpose

Gives storage analysis an actual record payoff.

## Prerequisites and starting position

**Required access or evidence:** [W20.1](W20.1.md).

**Starting material:** A retained maintenance image, simulated device-access material, and ordinary maintenance record examples.

**Campaign references:** No separately named capability output.

## Completion and downstream use

Recover the actual last-inspection entry, including its asset, inspection date, and completion state, with a valid interpretation of its record integrity. Finding isolated readable strings is insufficient.

## Difficulty and inspiration

Storage and record-semantics reconstruction inspired by Flash-ing Logs.

**Inspiration:** [Flash-ing Logs (HTB Cyber Apocalypse 2024; Hard)](../../reference-catalog.md#c12). Retains Flash-ing Logs' constrained record and storage problem using a simulated interface.
