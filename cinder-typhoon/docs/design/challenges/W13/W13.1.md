## Challenge description—player-facing

Recover the upcoming outlet-inspection appointment and its maintenance reference.

## Story purpose

Gives the planner's inspection a concrete schedule and purpose.

## Prerequisites and starting position

**Required access or evidence:** Corporate session (from W01).

**Starting material:** The contractor portal client document, discoverable from the client reference graph.

**Campaign references:** No separately named capability output.

## Completion and downstream use

Recover the actual appointment record. Its timing is scenario state, so reaching this work item does not require waiting for a real clock. The appointment_id, inspection, asset, and window are inputs to W13.2 and later cards.

## Difficulty and inspiration

A short appointment-disclosure task.

**Inspiration:** Original challenge for this campaign.

## Technical design

### Surface and normal behavior

The `contractor-portal.arwc.test` service serves the W13 arc. The `contractors.appointment-window` route in its client document → GET `contractor-portal.arwc.test/api/appointment-windows/current`.

**ERPNext source:** Appointment APT-CRR-2026-09-18 is seeded in ERPNext as an Event record. The contractor portal fetches the Event at startup using admin credentials from `/run/arwc-contractors-config/erp-reader.json` and stores it in service state. The route returns the state-held appointment.

The appointment fields:
- `record_id`: APT-CRR-2026-09-18
- `inspection`: INSP-CRR-2026-09-18
- `asset`: AST-CRR-017
- `window`: day=Thursday, starts=09:00, ends=11:00
- `owner_organization`: Northbank Civil Inspections
- `attendee`: northbank.inspector.117

**Gate:** The route requires the corporate session. No planner session or contractor session is required.

**Discovery path:** The contractor portal client document is reachable via the client reference graph (linked from the business workplace or the planning data client docs, following `related` links). The participant discovers `contractors.appointment-window` and calls it.

Native bindings:

- Route: `contractors.appointment-window` → GET `contractor-portal.arwc.test/api/appointment-windows/current`
- ERPNext Event: APT-CRR-2026-09-18 (seeded by seed-portal.py)
- Service: `a-contractors` container at `contractor-portal.arwc.test`

### Vulnerability and intended solution

This card is an exact discovery and read task. The participant traverses the client reference graph to find the contractor portal client document, discovers the `contractors.appointment-window` route, and calls it with their corporate session. The service writes an audit entry and returns the appointment.

The required result is: APT-CRR-2026-09-18, inspection=INSP-CRR-2026-09-18, asset=AST-CRR-017, Thursday 09:00–11:00.

### Evidence and completion

The archive service writes an immutable audit entry (`appointment-window-read`) when the route is called. Evidence sources are `features.a-maintenance.a-contractors`.

### Boundaries and persistence

`appointment_observed` state flag persists across restarts. The appointment record is sourced from ERPNext at service startup and falls back to the seeded constant if the fetch fails. Initial immutable records remain unchanged.

### Author checks

Verify that the route returns the correct appointment fields. Test wrong corporate session (401/403). Confirm the appointment record matches the ERPNext Event description. Verify the audit is written.

## Hints

### Hint 1

The contractor portal publishes its own client document at `contractor-portal.arwc.test/.well-known/arwc-client`. Traverse the published client reference graph—start from any service you have already reached and follow the `related` links until you find the contractor portal service.

### Hint 2

The contractor portal client document lists the `contractors.appointment-window` route. Use your corporate session to call it. No additional credential or session is required for this step.

### Hint 3

The route returns APT-CRR-2026-09-18 with inspection=INSP-CRR-2026-09-18, asset=AST-CRR-017, and a Thursday 09:00–11:00 window. Record all fields—the appointment_id, inspection, asset, and window are needed for W13.2 and later cards.
