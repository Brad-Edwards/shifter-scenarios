## Challenge description—player-facing

Replace the registered attendee on the outlet-inspection appointment with the contractor identity in Cinder's assignment, despite lacking the appointment owner's rights.

## Story purpose

Crosses an administrative boundary before credential issuance.

## Prerequisites and starting position

**Required access or evidence:** W13.1 (appointment observed) AND W13.2 (roster observed).

**Starting material:** The appointment from W13.1, the roster from W13.2, and the `contractors.appointment` route in the contractor portal client document.

**Campaign references:** No separately named capability output.

## Completion and downstream use

Demonstrate the attendee change on the correct appointment while preserving the inspection, asset, and scheduled window. The updated appointment is needed for W13.4 check-in.

## Difficulty and inspiration

A bounded ownership and identity-association problem.

**Inspiration:** Original challenge for this campaign.

## Technical design

### Surface and normal behavior

The `contractors.appointment` route → POST `contractor-portal.arwc.test/api/appointments/current`.

The request requires:
- `appointment_id`: APT-CRR-2026-09-18
- `expected_revision`: current appointment revision
- `attendee`: the contractor identity to set (must match roster identity)
- `caller_organization`: the caller's organization (must match roster contractor_organization)
- `inspection`, `asset`, `window`: must match appointment exactly (immutable fields)

**The BOLA:** The route checks that `attendee` belongs to `caller_organization` (from the roster), but does NOT check that the caller is the appointment owner (`Northbank Civil Inspections`). A Veybridge caller can therefore update the attendee from `northbank.inspector.117` to `veybridge.tech.204` without ownership.

**Immutable fields:** `inspection`, `asset`, and `window` cannot be changed. Any attempt to supply different values returns 409.

**Prerequisite gates:** Both `appointment_observed` and `roster_observed` flags must be True (W13.1 and W13.2 must be completed first).

**Evidence:** The service writes an audit entry (`appointment-created`) and returns the updated appointment record with `attendee=veybridge.tech.204` and `owner_organization=Northbank Civil Inspections` (unchanged).

Native bindings:

- Route: `contractors.appointment` → POST `contractor-portal.arwc.test/api/appointments/current`
- Appointment sourced from ERPNext (W13.1)
- Roster sourced from ERPNext (W13.2)

### Vulnerability and intended solution

The authored condition is **appointment-owner authorization omission**. The route validates:
- The attendee belongs to the caller's contractor organization ✓
- Inspection, asset, and window are immutable ✓
- appointment_id and expected_revision match ✓

But it does NOT validate:
- That the caller's organization is the appointment owner (`Northbank Civil Inspections`) ✗

The participant:
1. Uses appointment_id and revision from W13.1
2. Uses roster identity (`veybridge.tech.204`) and contractor_organization (`Veybridge Technical Services`) from W13.2
3. Submits the attendee-update with the immutable fields from the appointment
4. Receives the updated appointment with the new attendee despite not owning it

Denial proofs:
- Wrong attendee (not in roster) → 403
- Wrong caller_organization → 403
- Wrong inspection/asset/window → 409
- Missing appointment_observed or roster_observed → 403

### Evidence and completion

The archive service writes an immutable audit (`appointment-created`) when the update succeeds. The returned record has `attendee=veybridge.tech.204` and `owner_organization=Northbank Civil Inspections`. Evidence sources are `features.a-maintenance.a-contractors`.

### Boundaries and persistence

The updated attendee persists in the contractor state file across restarts. The revision increments by one after the update.

### Author checks

Verify attendee update succeeds with correct roster identity and caller organization. Test wrong attendee, wrong caller_organization, mismatched immutable fields, and missing prerequisites. Confirm owner_organization is unchanged. Confirm revision incremented.
