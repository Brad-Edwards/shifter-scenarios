## Challenge description — player-facing

Replace the registered attendee on the outlet-inspection appointment with the contractor identity in Cinder's assignment, despite lacking the appointment owner's rights.

## Story purpose

Crosses an administrative boundary before credential issuance.

## Prerequisites and starting position

**Required access or evidence:** ([W13.1](W13.1.md) AND [W13.2](W13.2.md)).

**Starting material:** Maintenance appointment records, a separately held access roster, and the contractor check-in workflow.

**Campaign references:** No separately named capability output.

## Completion and downstream use

Demonstrate the attendee change on the correct appointment while preserving the inspection, asset, and scheduled window. This supplies appointment authority, not yet a contractor session.

## Difficulty and inspiration

A bounded ownership and identity-association problem.

**Inspiration:** Original challenge for this campaign.
