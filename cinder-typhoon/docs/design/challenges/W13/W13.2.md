## Challenge description—player-facing

Recover the separate access roster for the inspection crew and identify the relevant contractor assignment.

## Story purpose

Adds independent identity evidence for the appointment.

## Prerequisites and starting position

**Required access or evidence:** Corporate session. W13.1 appointment is required context.

**Starting material:** The contractor portal client document, the appointment from W13.1, and the separate roster record in ERPNext.

**Campaign references:** No separately named capability output.

## Completion and downstream use

Recover ROSTER-CRR-SEP18-R2 and assignment CTR-VEY-204 (contractor identity veybridge.tech.204). The appointment document does not contain the full roster. The roster identity and assignment are needed for W13.3 and W13.4.

## Difficulty and inspiration

A distinct record recovery from corporate access.

**Inspiration:** Original challenge for this campaign.

## Technical design

### Surface and normal behavior

**ERPNext source:** Roster ROSTER-CRR-SEP18-R2 is seeded in ERPNext as a Task record. The contractor portal fetches the Task at startup using admin credentials and stores it in service state. The `contractors.expected-visitors` route returns the state-held roster.

The roster fields:
- `record_id`: ROSTER-CRR-SEP18-R2
- `revision`: 2
- `inspection`: INSP-CRR-2026-09-18
- `identity`: veybridge.tech.204
- `assignment`: CTR-VEY-204
- `contractor_organization`: Veybridge Technical Services

**Route:** `contractors.expected-visitors` → GET `contractor-portal.arwc.test/api/appointment-windows/current/visitors`.

**Gate:** Corporate session required. The roster is a separate record from the appointment—the participant must call the visitors route explicitly.

Native bindings:

- Route: `contractors.expected-visitors` → GET `contractor-portal.arwc.test/api/appointment-windows/current/visitors`
- ERPNext Task: ROSTER-CRR-SEP18-R2 (seeded by seed-portal.py)
- Service: `a-contractors` container

### Vulnerability and intended solution

This card is an exact discovery and read task. The participant calls the `contractors.expected-visitors` route with their corporate session and receives the roster record. The roster is stored separately from the appointment—it must be fetched explicitly.

The required result is: ROSTER-CRR-SEP18-R2, inspection=INSP-CRR-2026-09-18, identity=veybridge.tech.204, assignment=CTR-VEY-204.

### Evidence and completion

The archive service writes an immutable audit entry (`check-in-roster-read`). Evidence sources are `features.a-maintenance.a-contractors`.

### Boundaries and persistence

`roster_observed` state flag persists across restarts.

### Author checks

Verify the visitors route returns the correct roster fields. Test wrong corporate session. Confirm roster is sourced from ERPNext (not canned constant). Confirm audit is written.

## Hints

### Hint 1

The contractor portal client document also exposes `contractors.expected-visitors`. This is a separate route from `contractors.appointment-window`—the appointment record does not contain the full roster. Call it with your corporate session.

### Hint 2

The roster record is ROSTER-CRR-SEP18-R2, held separately from the appointment. It identifies the actual contractor who should appear at the gate, not just the appointment owner. Record the identity and assignment fields.

### Hint 3

The roster gives you `identity=veybridge.tech.204` and `assignment=CTR-VEY-204`. These are different from the appointment's attendee field. They are needed to construct the correct check-in in W13.3.
