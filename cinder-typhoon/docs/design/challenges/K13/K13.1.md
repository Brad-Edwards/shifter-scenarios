## Technical design


### Surface and normal behavior
SeaweedFS (`fieldlink-objects`) serves real S3 at `objects.keplerops.test` (via the `k-data` TLS proxy). The `fieldkest-workspaces` bucket has an **anonymous read ACL** in the SeaweedFS s3.json—the bucket is publicly listable and readable without credentials. The key `notebooks/deploy-2026-09.json` has two S3 object versions. The key `exports/EXP-2841/handover.json` contains the export reference.

The participant uses the real S3 API (ListObjectsV2, GetObject) against the anonymous-readable bucket.

### Vulnerability and intended solution
The `fieldkest-workspaces` bucket's anonymous identity in the SeaweedFS s3.json grants `Read` and `List` permissions to unauthenticated requests. This over-broad ACL is the vulnerability—no credentials are required to list or read the bucket.

The intended solution:
1. Discover `data.workspace-list` → `GET https://objects.keplerops.test/fieldkest-workspaces?list-type=2` from the developer client reference.
2. Issue the S3 ListObjectsV2 request without credentials (`--no-sign-request` or no auth header).
3. The XML response lists `notebooks/deploy-2026-09.json` and `exports/EXP-2841/handover.json`.
4. Retrieve the notebook: `GET https://objects.keplerops.test/fieldkest-workspaces/notebooks/deploy-2026-09.json`.
5. The notebook content (JSON) names the job reference `SCHED-2841` and export `EXP-2841`.

### Evidence and completion
The S3 ListObjectsV2 and GetObject responses are the evidence: the anonymous listing shows the exposed keys, and the notebook content reveals the job/export binding. The S3 response itself binds the object key, ETag, and content.

### Boundaries and persistence

Leads to K29/K30, grants no principal. Participant-created service state persists for the life of the participant world and ordinary retries do not erase it. The action is limited to the exact records, identities, routes, and destination effects declared here; unrelated tenants and services remain denied. Infrastructure recovery and submission adjudication are outside this in-world contract.

### Author checks
Verify that unauthenticated ListObjectsV2 returns the notebook and handover keys, that GetObject returns the notebook content without credentials, that authenticated access with the wrong credentials is denied, and that the notebook content binds job SCHED-2841 and export EXP-2841.
