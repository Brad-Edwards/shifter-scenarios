## Challenge description—player-facing

Find the separate support export mentioned in the workspace notes and recover its retained handover.

## Story purpose

Provides a second cloud data surface with a support-workflow purpose.

## Prerequisites and starting position

**Required access or evidence:** [G01—K01.1](../K01/K01.1.md).

**Starting material:** The cloud workspace notebook, object references, export notes, and ordinary developer-visible workload records.

**Campaign references:** No separately named capability output.

## Completion and downstream use

Recover the support handover from the separate export. It supplies a lead to export jobs, not the protected collection of K15.

## Difficulty and inspiration

A bounded discovery and retrieval task outside the notebook's version history.

**Inspiration:** Original challenge for this campaign.

## Technical design


### Surface and normal behavior
The K13.1 notebook content includes `export_ref: "EXP-2841"`. The corresponding handover object is in the same `fieldkest-workspaces` bucket at key `exports/EXP-2841/handover.json`. This key is listed in the anonymous S3 listing from K13.1 alongside the notebook.

The handover object is a separate support export artifact, not a version of the notebook. It records the export job reference, owner, and destination bucket.

### Vulnerability and intended solution
The same anonymous read ACL (K13.1) allows reading `exports/EXP-2841/handover.json` without credentials.

The intended solution:
1. The K13.1 notebook reveals `export_ref: "EXP-2841"`.
2. The K13.1 listing shows `exports/EXP-2841/handover.json` as a separate key.
3. Retrieve it: `aws s3api get-object --bucket fieldkest-workspaces --key exports/EXP-2841/handover.json --endpoint-url https://objects.keplerops.test --no-sign-request handover.json`
4. The handover content reveals `export_id=EXP-2841`, `job=SCHED-2841`, and `owner=maya.ranscombe`.

### Evidence and completion
The S3 GetObject response for `exports/EXP-2841/handover.json` and its content are the evidence. The `export_id`, `job`, and `owner` fields bind the export to the support workflow.

### Boundaries and persistence

Lead to K15. Participant-created service state persists for the life of the participant world and ordinary retries do not erase it. The action is limited to the exact records, identities, routes, and destination effects declared here; unrelated tenants and services remain denied. Infrastructure recovery and submission adjudication are outside this in-world contract.

### Author checks
Verify that `exports/EXP-2841/handover.json` is in the anonymous listing alongside the notebook key, that it is retrievable without credentials, and that its content binds export_id=EXP-2841, job=SCHED-2841, and owner=maya.ranscombe.
