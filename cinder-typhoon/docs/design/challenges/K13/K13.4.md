## Challenge description — player-facing

Establish the limited workload identity available from this workspace and recover the protected record within its actual scope.

## Story purpose

Makes cloud identity a demonstrated capability rather than a found label.

## Prerequisites and starting position

**Required access or evidence:** [G01 — K01.1](../K01/K01.1.md).

**Starting material:** The cloud workspace notebook, object references, export notes, and ordinary developer-visible workload records.

**Campaign references:** No separately named capability output.

## Completion and downstream use

Recover the protected workload record using the available identity. This supplies authenticated starting context for K14, K15, and K30. K31 requires the further scoped role from K14.2, or the separate K09.4 job-execution route.

## Difficulty and inspiration

Requires identifying the right workload context and distinguishing its granted access from the developer's.

**Inspiration:** Original challenge for this campaign.
