## Challenge description — player-facing

Reconstruct every commissioning step from product history and turn the measured results into an explicitly labelled one-hour operating projection.

## Story purpose

Requires an auditable separation between what the short trial measured and what the planned hour would do.

## Prerequisites and starting position

**Required access or evidence:** ([W33.3](W33.3.md) AND [W29.PLAN](../../capability-routes.json)).

**Starting material:** The W33.3 commissioning receipt, native dispatcher and historian records, the current usable-volume series, and current W29 tariff and allocation rows.

**Campaign references:** No separately named capability output.

## Completion and downstream use

Produce twelve history-derived settled flow means and the resulting projected one-hour volume, remaining usable volume, allocation shortfall, and tariff liability while preserving the measured-versus-projected distinction.

## Difficulty and inspiration

Remains Expert because the participant must join four product record families, recompute twelve interval results, preserve time and quality semantics, apply the correct corporate basis, and avoid overstating a short commissioning trial.

**Inspiration:** [Terraforming Mars (Hack-a-Sat 2023; relative 4/5)](../../reference-catalog.md#c30) supplies the trajectory-analysis difficulty. The evidence record and projection boundary follow the operating-record discipline in the [USBR SOP Guidelines](https://www.usbr.gov/assetmanagement/docs/SOPGuidelines-Jun2001.pdf).

## Technical design


### Surface and normal behavior

The installed Cairn Reach OT products own this part of **Prove the operating projection**. The exact initial state is: The final operating record reconstructs twelve settled flow means from product history, projects their one-hour reservoir volume, remaining usable volume, allocation shortfall, and tariff liability, and labels the short commissioning trial separately from the projection.

Native bindings:

- `a-engineering.a-historian/PostgreSQL:public.process_history-and-dispatch_history`
- `a-engineering.a-hmi/FUXA:crr-controls-local-handback`
- `a-corporate.a-data/PostgreSQL:public.settlement_tariff-and-allocation_ledger`
- `a-control.a-reservoir/OpenPLC:measured-commissioning-response`

The image-owned W33 operating window, schedule template, commissioning brief, and bounded client define the work but do not establish any measurement or acceptance. The existing Mosquitto dispatcher, OpenPLC controller, FUXA HMI, remote I/O, PostgreSQL historian, and corporate planning rows remain authoritative; no scheduler or rehearsal service duplicates them.

### Vulnerability and intended solution

This is a bounded operations-planning and commissioning task, not a privilege flaw. The participant must use the installed products to reconstruct the twelve measured step means from retained product history and calculate the one-hour volume, remaining usable volume, allocation shortfall, and tariff liability without presenting projection as measurement. A synthetic forecast, private rehearsal, locally invented response, or copied request receipt does not establish the result.

The required result is: Produce twelve history-derived settled flow means and the resulting projected one-hour volume, remaining usable volume, allocation shortfall, and tariff liability while preserving the measured-versus-projected distinction.

### Evidence and completion

Completion reads a range-supplied snapshot of the native dispatcher journal, PLC decisions, process history, and corporate planning records applicable to the card. The participant cannot supply product evidence. The evaluator cannot issue a broker request, write a controller tag, move an outlet, retry an uncertain request, take local priority, or create product evidence.

Evidence sources are `features.a-engineering.a-historian`, `features.a-engineering.a-hmi`, `features.a-corporate.a-data`, `features.a-control.a-reservoir`. A wrong asset, instruction, calibration request, source time, process quality, gain, schedule constraint, request identity, transition order, PLC sequence, measured result, handback, corporate record, unit, or measured-versus-projected interpretation is rejected.

### Boundaries and persistence

Supplies a measured commissioning record and explicit one-hour operating projection; it grants no reusable control authority. The dispatcher, OpenPLC command history, and historian retain the twelve commissioning steps as ordinary product records. Completion requires a later accepted local HMI request and measured return to manual A20/B20, approximately 0.10 m3/s per outlet and 0.20 m3/s total, healthy I/O, valid permissives, no motion, and clear travel faults. The one-hour effect remains a projection. Infrastructure recovery and submission adjudication are external to the SDL.

The behavior is limited to AST-CRR-017 and CRR-OP-W33-R1. It grants no arbitrary MQTT topic, OPC UA write, engineering force, program upload, interlock bypass, automatic retry after uncertainty, alternate control endpoint, or new scheduler authority.

### Author checks

Begin from the qualified manual A20/B20 baseline and an authentic completed W30 record. Verify calibration against native dispatcher and historian time, then accept multiple feasible schedules rather than one fixed answer. For commissioning, require twelve fresh sequential requests, the exact successful dispatcher path, matching native PLC acceptances, settled good-quality feedback, and a later local handback. Recompute every projection from product history and the current corporate rows. Reject infeasible schedules, missing or reordered stages, duplicate identities, rejection, uncertainty, bad quality, participant-supplied product evidence, invented measurements, stale planning records, confused measured/projected claims, or failure to restore. Reset must return the same qualified baseline without replaying a dispatcher request or changing product identities and trust material.
