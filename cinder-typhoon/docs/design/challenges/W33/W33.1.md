## Challenge description — player-facing

Calibrate outlet response delay and flow gain from a completed native operation and good-quality process history.

## Story purpose

Grounds operating planning in actual dispatcher, controller, and historian evidence.

## Prerequisites and starting position

**Required access or evidence:** [W30.RESULT](../../capability-routes.json).

**Starting material:** The W33 scheduling brief and one completed W30 record in the dispatcher, native command history, and process historian.

**Campaign references:** No separately named capability output.

## Completion and downstream use

Record the first good in-tolerance response after native PLC acceptance, at least five settled samples, the response delay, and measured-flow/requested-flow gain.

## Difficulty and inspiration

A bounded model/evidence fit across independent product records. It preserves Medium difficulty because the request identity and target are supplied while time basis, sample quality, settled interval, delay, and gain must still be reconciled correctly.

**Inspiration:** [Terraforming Mars (Hack-a-Sat 2023; relative 4/5)](../../reference-catalog.md#c30) supplies the later constrained trajectory planning. The calibration step follows the product-time and process-history discipline described by [NIST SP 800-82 Rev. 3](https://csrc.nist.gov/pubs/sp/800/82/r3/final).

## Technical design


### Surface and normal behavior

The installed Cairn Reach OT products own this part of **A forecast that matches the instrument**. The exact initial state is: Operating instruction CRR-OP-W33-R1 requires calibration from one completed W30 dispatcher request, its native PLC acceptance, and at least five good settled TotalFlowM3s samples; response delay begins at PLC acceptance and gain is measured flow divided by requested flow.

Native bindings:

- `a-engineering.a-engineering/filesystem:/home/fieldlink/CairnReach/Briefs/W33/scheduling-brief.md`
- `a-engineering.a-historian/PostgreSQL:public.dispatch_history-and-process_history`
- `a-engineering.a-hmi/FUXA:completed-W30-operating-record`

The image-owned W33 operating window, schedule template, commissioning brief, and bounded client define the work but do not establish any measurement or acceptance. The existing Mosquitto dispatcher, OpenPLC controller, FUXA HMI, remote I/O, PostgreSQL historian, and corporate planning rows remain authoritative; no scheduler or rehearsal service duplicates them.

### Vulnerability and intended solution

This is a bounded operations-planning and commissioning task, not a privilege flaw. The participant must use the installed products to calibrate response delay and flow gain from one completed W30 request, its native PLC acceptance, and at least five settled good-quality flow samples. A synthetic forecast, private rehearsal, locally invented response, or copied request receipt does not establish the result.

The required result is: Record the first good in-tolerance response after native PLC acceptance, at least five settled samples, the response delay, and measured-flow/requested-flow gain.

### Evidence and completion

Completion reads a range-supplied snapshot of the native dispatcher journal, PLC decisions, process history, and corporate planning records applicable to the card. The participant cannot supply product evidence. The evaluator cannot issue a broker request, write a controller tag, move an outlet, retry an uncertain request, take local priority, or create product evidence.

Evidence sources are `features.a-engineering.a-engineering`, `features.a-engineering.a-historian`, `features.a-engineering.a-hmi`. A wrong asset, instruction, calibration request, source time, process quality, gain, schedule constraint, request identity, transition order, PLC sequence, measured result, handback, corporate record, unit, or measured-versus-projected interpretation is rejected.

### Boundaries and persistence

The result remains local evidence unless a downstream workflow explicitly names it. The card is read-only and creates no dispatcher or controller state. It refers to the completed W30 operation and current planning rows without copying or revising their product records. Infrastructure recovery and submission adjudication are external to the SDL.

The behavior is limited to AST-CRR-017 and CRR-OP-W33-R1. It grants no arbitrary MQTT topic, OPC UA write, engineering force, program upload, interlock bypass, automatic retry after uncertainty, alternate control endpoint, or new scheduler authority.

### Author checks

Begin from the qualified manual A20/B20 baseline and an authentic completed W30 record. Verify calibration against native dispatcher and historian time, then accept multiple feasible schedules rather than one fixed answer. For commissioning, require twelve fresh sequential requests, the exact successful dispatcher path, matching native PLC acceptances, settled good-quality feedback, and a later local handback. Recompute every projection from product history and the current corporate rows. Reject infeasible schedules, missing or reordered stages, duplicate identities, rejection, uncertainty, bad quality, participant-supplied product evidence, invented measurements, stale planning records, confused measured/projected claims, or failure to restore. Reset must return the same qualified baseline without replaying a dispatcher request or changing product identities and trust material.
