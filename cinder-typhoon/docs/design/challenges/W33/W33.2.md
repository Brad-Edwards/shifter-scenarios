## Challenge description—player-facing

Construct a twelve-interval release and alternate-supply schedule using the measured gain, planned demand, and declared operating limits.

## Story purpose

Makes physical state, timing, and corporate planning context substantive parts of the challenge.

## Prerequisites and starting position

**Required access or evidence:** ([W33.1](W33.1.md) AND [ENVELOPE](../../capability-routes.json) AND [W29.PLAN](../../capability-routes.json)).

**Starting material:** The image-owned operating-window CSV, schedule template, W33.1 calibration record, installed controller limits, and current W29 tariff and allocation basis.

**Campaign references:** No separately named capability output.

## Completion and downstream use

Produce a feasible twelve-interval operating schedule meeting every published buffer, ramp, supply, and calibrated-volume constraint. A fixed maximum or copied reference trajectory is insufficient.

## Difficulty and inspiration

Constrained physical trajectory planning remains Hard because the participant must solve coupled interval, state, ramp, terminal, and volume constraints; the evaluator accepts any feasible schedule rather than one hidden answer.

**Inspiration:** [Terraforming Mars (Hack-a-Sat 2023; relative 4/5)](../../reference-catalog.md#c30) supplies the constrained trajectory difficulty. The water-operations framing follows the extended-period planning role of [EPA EPANET](https://www.epa.gov/water-research/epanet), without pretending this narrow plant model is a distribution simulator.

### Vulnerability and intended solution

This is a bounded operations-planning and commissioning task, not a privilege flaw. The participant must use the installed products to construct a twelve-interval schedule that satisfies the disclosed demand, calibrated gain, buffer, ramp, alternate-supply, and projected-volume constraints. A synthetic forecast, private rehearsal, locally invented response, or copied request receipt does not establish the result.

The required result is: Produce a feasible twelve-interval operating schedule meeting every published buffer, ramp, supply, and calibrated-volume constraint. A fixed maximum or copied reference trajectory is insufficient.

### Evidence and completion

Completion reads a range-supplied snapshot of the native dispatcher journal, PLC decisions, process history, and corporate planning records applicable to the card. The participant cannot supply product evidence. The evaluator cannot issue a broker request, write a controller tag, move an outlet, retry an uncertain request, take local priority, or create product evidence.

Evidence sources are `features.a-engineering.a-engineering`, `features.a-engineering.a-historian`, `features.a-corporate.a-data`. A wrong asset, instruction, calibration request, source time, process quality, gain, schedule constraint, request identity, transition order, PLC sequence, measured result, handback, corporate record, unit, or measured-versus-projected interpretation is rejected.

### Boundaries and persistence

The result remains local evidence unless a downstream workflow explicitly names it. The card is read-only and creates no dispatcher or controller state. It refers to the completed W30 operation and current planning rows without copying or revising their product records. Infrastructure recovery and submission adjudication are external to the SDL.

The behavior is limited to AST-CRR-017 and CRR-OP-W33-R1. It grants no arbitrary MQTT topic, OPC UA write, engineering force, program upload, interlock bypass, automatic retry after uncertainty, alternate control endpoint, or new scheduler authority.

### Author checks

Begin from the qualified manual A20/B20 baseline and an authentic completed W30 record. Verify calibration against native dispatcher and historian time, then accept multiple feasible schedules rather than one fixed answer. For commissioning, require twelve fresh sequential requests, the exact successful dispatcher path, matching native PLC acceptances, settled good-quality feedback, and a later local handback. Recompute every projection from product history and the current corporate rows. Reject infeasible schedules, missing or reordered stages, duplicate identities, rejection, uncertainty, bad quality, participant-supplied product evidence, invented measurements, stale planning records, confused measured/projected claims, or failure to restore. Reset must return the same qualified baseline without replaying a dispatcher request or changing product identities and trust material.
