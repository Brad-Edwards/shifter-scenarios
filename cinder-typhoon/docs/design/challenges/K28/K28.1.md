## Challenge description — player-facing

Determine when the archived connector behaves differently from its ordinary interface and recover the diagnostic evidence for that difference.

## Story purpose

Introduces a product-history mystery tied to the later engineering material.

## Prerequisites and starting position

**Required access or evidence:** [G01 — K01.1](../K01/K01.1.md).

**Starting material:** An archived FieldKest customer-connector binary, ordinary invocation examples, and retained diagnostic notes.

**Campaign references:** No separately named capability output.

## Completion and downstream use

Recover the actual behavior distinction and its relevant runtime condition. A binary filename or unexplained crash is insufficient.

## Difficulty and inspiration

A bounded behavior-comparison investigation with supplied normal examples.

**Inspiration:** [SatelliteHijack (HTB Business CTF 2024; Hard)](../../reference-catalog.md#c11). Adapts SatelliteHijack's discrepancy between superficial analysis and loaded behavior.
