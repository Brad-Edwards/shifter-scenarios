## Challenge description — player-facing

The new role is still denied the support export. Cross the export's own resource boundary and recover the specified support data.

## Story purpose

Shows that identity trust and resource authorization are separate problems.

## Prerequisites and starting position

**Required access or evidence:** [K14.2](K14.2.md).

**Starting material:** The earned workload context, its trust documentation, and the support-export service's ordinary policy view.

**Campaign references:** No separately named capability output.

## Completion and downstream use

Recover the support export through its distinct policy boundary. A successful role exchange alone is insufficient.

## Difficulty and inspiration

Requires resolving a second, resource-specific authorization decision.

**Inspiration:** Original challenge for this campaign.
