## Technical design


### Surface and normal behavior
The `fieldkest-exports` bucket in SeaweedFS contains the support export at `exports/support/EXP-2841`. The SeaweedFS s3.json has two distinct policies:
- `rowan.ito` (developer): has `Read:fieldlink-exports` and `Read:fieldlink-collections` but NOT `Read:fieldkest-exports`. Denied by SeaweedFS.
- `svc-fieldlink-export` (K14.2 role): has `Read:fieldkest-exports`. Allowed by SeaweedFS.

The boundary is enforced by real SeaweedFS s3.json at the bucket level. The design resource rule references the path with `%2f` encoding; the actual enforcement is a separate bucket policy boundary between the developer identity and the export role identity.

### Vulnerability and intended solution
The developer identity cannot access `fieldkest-exports`—it only has access to `fieldlink-exports` and `fieldlink-collections`. The K14.2 role-exchange provides `svc-fieldlink-export` credentials (via `GET /api/data/exports/{export_id}`) that ARE allowed by SeaweedFS.

The intended solution:
1. Know the support export location from K13.3 (`EXP-2841`) and K14.2 (`service_ref: fieldkest-exports`).
2. Try developer credentials → SeaweedFS returns 403 (AccessDenied).
3. Present K14.2 export SA token to `GET /api/data/exports/EXP-2841` → returns export S3 credentials.
4. Use export S3 credentials with `objects.keplerops.test` → SeaweedFS returns 200.
5. The support export content reveals `export_id=EXP-2841`, `tenant=TEN-ARWC-019`, `case_id=CAS-MAYA-2841`.

### Evidence and completion
SeaweedFS returns 403 with developer credentials and 200 with export credentials (provable by running both). The support export content includes `tenant=TEN-ARWC-019`. The export-credentials audit entry confirms the credential exchange.

### Boundaries and persistence

Distinct data-policy result. Participant-created service state persists for the life of the participant world and ordinary retries do not erase it. The action is limited to the exact records, identities, routes, and destination effects declared here; unrelated tenants and services remain denied. Infrastructure recovery and submission adjudication are outside this in-world contract.

### Author checks
Verify that rowan.ito is denied access to `fieldkest-exports` by real SeaweedFS (AccessDenied), that svc-fieldlink-export credentials allow reading `exports/support/EXP-2841`, that the cloud-api export-credentials endpoint validates the K14.2 SA token, and that the support export content includes TEN-ARWC-019.

## Hints

### Hint 1

The K14.1 trust record has `service_ref=fieldkest-exports`. The K14.2 role record has `scope=fieldkest-exports`. The developer credentials (rowan.ito) are denied by SeaweedFS for `fieldkest-exports`—the bucket is not in the developer's s3.json actions. The K14.2 exchange is required.

### Hint 2

Present the K14.2 export SA token (`Bearer <export_sa_token>`) to `GET /api/data/exports/EXP-2841`. The cloud-api validates the export SA token (K3s TokenReview confirms svc-fieldlink-export subject) and returns `access_key_id`, `secret_access_key`, and the S3 endpoint. Use these credentials to call `aws s3api get-object --bucket fieldkest-exports --key exports/support/EXP-2841 --endpoint-url https://objects.keplerops.test`.

### Hint 3

The support export content includes `export_id=EXP-2841`, `tenant=TEN-ARWC-019`, `case_id=CAS-MAYA-2841`. The TEN-ARWC-019 tenant confirms the ARWC context (without granting ARWC access). The export-credentials audit entry records the credential exchange. This is the K14.3 and K14/K16/K31 chain completion evidence.
