## Technical design


### Surface and normal behavior
The trust record `TRUST-FLX-04` is a real K3s ConfigMap in the `fieldlink` namespace. Its content is derived from real K3s SA token claims:
- `issuer`: the K3s OIDC issuer (`https://kubernetes.default.svc.cluster.local`)
- `subject`: `system:serviceaccount:fieldlink:svc-fieldlink-workload`
- `accepted_audiences`: the K3s token audiences
- `export_role`: `fieldkest-export-access` (the K14.2 scope boundary)
- `service_ref`: `fieldkest-exports`

The ServiceAccount `svc-fieldlink-workload` has GET access to `trust-flx-04` via a dedicated Role. The participant uses the K13.4 SA token to read it via `cloud.trust-record`.

### Vulnerability and intended solution
The workspace exchange response (`cloud.workspace-exchange`) returns `trust_record: "TRUST-FLX-04"`. The participant uses their K13.4 SA token to read the trust record via `GET /api/trust-records/TRUST-FLX-04`. The cloud-api forwards the request to K3s ConfigMap endpoint using the SA token. K3s RBAC allows the read.

The trust record is NOT a canned document—it contains the real K3s OIDC issuer and subject from the real SA token claims, verifiable against the JWT the participant holds.

The intended solution:
1. `GET /api/workspaces/current/token-exchange` (developer credentials) → reveals `trust_record=TRUST-FLX-04`.
2. `GET /api/trust-records/TRUST-FLX-04` (K13.4 SA Bearer token) → K3s returns the trust ConfigMap.
3. The response includes `issuer`, `subject`, `accepted_audiences`, `export_role`, `service_ref`.

### Evidence and completion
The trust-records audit entry records `trust_id=TRUST-FLX-04` and `subject=system:serviceaccount:fieldlink:svc-fieldlink-workload`. The response binds the K3s issuer, subject, and export_role.

### Boundaries and persistence

Separate evidence from K13.4/K09.4. Participant-created service state persists for the life of the participant world and ordinary retries do not erase it. The action is limited to the exact records, identities, routes, and destination effects declared here; unrelated tenants and services remain denied. Infrastructure recovery and submission adjudication are outside this in-world contract.

### Author checks
Verify that `trust-flx-04` is a real K3s ConfigMap, that the issuer in the ConfigMap matches the K3s OIDC issuer, that only `svc-fieldlink-workload` can read it, and that the trust record is separate evidence from the K13.4 BLD-1842 read.
