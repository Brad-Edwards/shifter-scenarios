## Challenge description — player-facing

Decode the supplied controller status capture and identify the condition preventing the practice outlet from moving.

## Story purpose

Connects a compact representation to a meaningful operating condition.

## Prerequisites and starting position

**Required access or evidence:** the supplied training workspace.

**Starting material:** Small training snapshots, readable field and status legends, a private practice tank, and a separate reporting replay case.

**Campaign references:** No separately named capability output.

## Completion and downstream use

Identify the actual blocking condition and its state in the capture. Selecting an arbitrary alarm from the legend does not count.

## Difficulty and inspiration

A small documented status representation.

**Inspiration:** Original challenge for this campaign.

## Technical design

### Surface and normal behavior

The practice controller publishes a compact status word and a versioned legend
alongside its HMI diagram. The captured record represents one outlet, one
sample time, and the condition that blocks a requested practice transition.
The immutable SC-018 record is served at `/captures/status.json`; its TR-1
status word is 5, meaning ready and service hold, with the outlet closed.
The matching bit legend is published at `/field-guide.md`. No other controller
revision is needed to interpret this small recorded case.

### Vulnerability and intended solution

This is a bounded protocol-and-documentation interpretation exercise. The
intended solution decodes the captured status with the matching controller
revision and correlates the asserted bit with the outlet state, rather than
selecting an alarm label by guesswork.

### Evidence and completion

The capture binds one status word to its sample ID and controller revision.
The matching legend identifies the asserted blocking condition. Reading this
record does not reveal, change, or authorize anything in the live practice
application; that application already exposes its own status and legend.

### Boundaries

The capture and its legend are immutable records, separate from the live
practice view. Reading this capture cannot alter the controller or issue a
command. The live view supplies its own current status and matching legend.

### Author checks

Check that the revision-matched legend is present, exactly one asserted
condition explains the recorded block, and the live application can be used
independently without reading or submitting this task.
