## Challenge description — player-facing

Move the specified bounded amount of water through the training outlet and verify the transfer from the tank's observations.

## Story purpose

Connects commands and operating conditions to actual effect.

## Prerequisites and starting position

**Required access or evidence:** the supplied training workspace.

**Starting material:** Small training snapshots, readable field and status legends, a private practice tank, and a separate reporting replay case.

**Campaign references:** No separately named capability output.

## Completion and downstream use

Demonstrate the requested transfer and final outlet state within the published bounds. An accepted request alone is insufficient; no campaign authority is granted.

## Difficulty and inspiration

A small documented state-sequence task with visible effects.

**Inspiration:** Original challenge for this campaign.

## Technical design

### Surface and normal behavior

The training HMI drives a deterministic isolated tank-and-outlet practice model.
It exposes a documented request interface, operating bounds, accepted/rejected
command record, and independently published tank observations. The interface
and numerical contract are published at `/field-guide.md`; the live view is
`/practice` and its current state is `/api/practice/state`. Initial tank PT-01
contains 1,200 L, is ready, has a service hold, and has its outlet closed.
The requested 200 L transfer leaves 1,000 L and a closed outlet after 10 seconds
at 20 L/s. The server retains its request register and independent observation
register. Read-only captures and the report replay belong to other tank IDs.

### Vulnerability and intended solution

This is a safe control-verification task, not an authorization bypass. The
intended solution is to inspect the live status and its available legend,
clear the condition through the normal practice workflow, request the specified bounded transfer, and validate the
result against observations.

### Evidence and completion

The request record, final outlet state, and independent before/after
measurements identify the same transfer. The observed volume difference must
match the requested amount within the documented tolerance. Request acceptance
alone does not establish physical movement in the practice model.

### Boundaries

The practice model belongs to the individual workspace and cannot reach any
campaign control state. No automatic rewind or refilling follows a transfer.
Initial state is established once when the environment is prepared; subsequent
state changes occur only through the documented operating controls.

### Author checks

Check blocked requests cause no movement, bounded transfers finish with the
outlet closed, invalid requests cannot change volume, independent measurements
show the exact delta, and a reconnect preserves the live tank and history.
