## Challenge description — player-facing

Recover the instrument's restricted sequence descriptor and reproduce the designated ordinary diagnostic observation.

## Story purpose

Gives the experimental branch a usable interface and baseline.

## Prerequisites and starting position

**Required access or evidence:** [CORPORATE](../../capability-routes.json).

**Starting material:** A retained diagnostic instrument artifact, its sequencer interface, ordinary observation examples, and protected calibration references.

**Campaign references:** No separately named capability output.

## Completion and downstream use

Recover the descriptor and a correctly bound baseline observation. Finding the binary or its version string is insufficient.

## Difficulty and inspiration

A bounded interface-reconstruction task with supplied examples.

**Inspiration:** [Spectrel Imaging (Hack-a-Sat 2023; relative 5/5)](../../reference-catalog.md#c14). Retains Spectrel Imaging's constrained experimental observation and protected-data recovery challenge.
