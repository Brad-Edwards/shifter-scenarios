## Challenge description — player-facing

Use the maintenance service to recover the protected record outside your ordinary assignment, bound to the asset you identified.

## Story purpose

Adds an actual data-access boundary after the reconciliation.

## Prerequisites and starting position

**Required access or evidence:** [W02.2](W02.2.md).

**Starting material:** The maintenance asset directory, contractor bundle references, and ordinary record-association examples.

**Campaign references:** No separately named capability output.

## Completion and downstream use

Recover the protected maintenance record through the association boundary. The identifier mapping alone does not count.

## Difficulty and inspiration

Requires applying the earned association to a separate object-authorization problem.

**Inspiration:** Original challenge for this campaign.
