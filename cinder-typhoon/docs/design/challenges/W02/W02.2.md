## Challenge description — player-facing

Reconcile the contractor, asset, and inspection identifiers so the maintenance record can be tied to the correct physical assignment.

## Story purpose

Turns inconsistent business data into useful operational evidence.

## Prerequisites and starting position

**Required access or evidence:** [W02.1](W02.1.md).

**Starting material:** The maintenance asset directory, contractor bundle references, and ordinary record-association examples.

**Campaign references:** No separately named capability output.

## Completion and downstream use

Produce the correct cross-record association with its source records. Matching display names alone is insufficient.

## Difficulty and inspiration

A small evidence join across distinct identifier systems.

**Inspiration:** Original challenge for this campaign.
