## Challenge description — player-facing

The opened project includes a commissioning review helper whose recorded archive access does not match its apparent behavior or the listed reviewers. Recover the concealed review identity and use its access in the retained review context to obtain the withheld commissioning comparison.

## Story purpose

Gives Cinder a usable route into the retained review archive and the comparison that the official handover omitted. The project decision is already earned in W22.3; this challenge exposes who could read beyond that handover.

## Prerequisites and starting position

**Required access or evidence:** [W22.3](W22.3.md).

**Starting material:** W22.3's opened package supplies the unmodified review helper, its captured commissioning session, ordinary review examples, the listed reviewer identities, and the matching retained archive context. The project decryption key and readable decision are already held. The helper's concealed identity and access are not supplied.

**Campaign references:** No separately named capability output.

## Completion and downstream use

Recover the usable concealed review identity from the helper's actual behavior and obtain the withheld commissioning comparison through its scoped archive access. Identity recovery and its first use form one achievement. The result must concern the same project and commissioning revision. The archive context is historical: this result grants no current corporate identity, maintenance approval, or CONTROL capability.

## Difficulty and inspiration

Expert is proposed for reconciling captured execution evidence with conflicting managed and native program representations, then reconstructing the concealed behavior into usable archive access. The earlier VM model and decryption result do not resolve this separate executable. Recognizing the representation mismatch alone is insufficient.
