## Technical design


### Surface and normal behavior

The engineering workbench owns this card. The participant calls `GET /api/sealed-projects/current/viewer` with their CORPORATE session to retrieve the delivery record. The service returns the delivery metadata and the three binary artifacts (viewer, program, sealed package).

**Service route**: `GET https://process-view.arwc.test/api/sealed-projects/current/viewer`
- Requires CORPORATE Bearer token
- Sets `viewer_observed=True` in state
- Returns: `record_id`, `viewer`, `viewer_language`, `platform`, `project_id`, `viewer_sha256`, `program_sha256`, `package_sha256`, `ordinary_cases`
- Artifacts (base64): `viewer_b64` (Nim viewer binary), `program_b64` (viewer program), `package_b64` (sealed project)

**Viewer binary** is also in Gitea: `clara.morivelle/retained-engineering-tools/engineering/sealed-viewer`. The participant can verify its SHA256 matches the service response. The delivery IDs and SHA256 are NOT in any shipped file — they come from the service call.

Native bindings:
- `a-engineering.a-engineering/process-view:process-view.arwc.test/api/sealed-projects/current/viewer`
- `a-engineering.a-engineering/gitea:clara.morivelle/retained-engineering-tools/engineering/sealed-viewer`

The private service-loader contract is `content.arwc-a-engineering.w22-1-the-viewer-in-the-delivery`.

### Vulnerability and intended solution

The participant calls the GET route with their earned CORPORATE session. The service returns the five binding fields (delivery ID, viewer ID, language, platform, project ID) and the SHA256 of the viewer binary. All values come from the service call; the participant cannot fabricate them from shipped files.

The required result is: Recover the correct viewer artifact and project binding. Locating an unrelated viewer binary is insufficient.

### Evidence and completion

The owning service writes an immutable audit for the accepted request and result. Caller-supplied output, a local copy, or knowledge of an identifier is not sufficient. Completion is evaluated from fresh service-owned evidence after the action. It cannot issue the identity, create the mutation, move a simulated actuator, or fabricate a downstream acceptance needed to prove itself.

Evidence sources are `features.a-engineering.a-engineering`. A wrong identifier, stale revision, replayed one-time value, different checkpoint, or cross-tenant artifact is rejected.

### Boundaries and persistence

The result remains local evidence unless a downstream workflow explicitly names it. Tenant-scoped mutations, sessions that have not expired, generated artifacts, and audits persist across ordinary retries and later sessions. Initial immutable records remain unchanged unless the named route explicitly permits a field-level revision. Infrastructure recovery and submission adjudication are external to the SDL.

The behavior is limited to the named fictional objects and bounded service operation. It provides no general shell, arbitrary network target, unrelated district access, real process interface, or authority not expressly declared by the card.
