## Challenge description — player-facing

Recover the protected maintenance-renderer profile and bind it to the approved inspection.

Identifies the actual consumer of the earned approval.

**Required access or evidence:** ([W13.SESSION](../../capability-routes.json) AND [W15.APPROVAL](../../capability-routes.json)).
