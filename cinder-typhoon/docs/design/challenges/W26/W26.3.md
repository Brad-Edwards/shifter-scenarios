## Challenge description — player-facing

Make the renderer recover its internal maintenance handover through an action executed under its service identity, while still producing the expected inspection preview.

## Story purpose

Adds an execution capability under real application constraints.

## Prerequisites and starting position

**Required access or evidence:** [W26.2](W26.2.md).

**Starting material:** The contractor session, earned maintenance approval, the maintenance rendering workflow, and its scoped action interface.

**Campaign references:** No separately named capability output.

## Completion and downstream use

Demonstrate renderer-context execution, recover the internal handover, and preserve the preview's required ordinary content. Application-level preview control from W26.2 alone does not count; live-action authority remains W26.4.

## Difficulty and inspiration

A constrained renderer execution transition beyond its protected application action.

**Inspiration:** [apexsurvive (HTB Cyber Apocalypse 2024; Insane)](../../reference-catalog.md#c08). Adapts the later renderer/action portion of apexsurvive; this is separate from K12's complete supplier browser chain.
