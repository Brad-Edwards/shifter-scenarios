## Challenge description — player-facing

Cross the remaining render-to-action boundary and establish a live control client bound to the earned contractor identity and maintenance approval.

## Story purpose

Completes the advanced maintenance route to control authority.

## Prerequisites and starting position

**Required access or evidence:** [W26.3](W26.3.md).

**Starting material:** The contractor session, earned maintenance approval, the maintenance rendering workflow, and its scoped action interface.

**Campaign references:** `W26.CONTROL`.

## Completion and downstream use

Demonstrate the scoped client's accepted authority under the correct approval binding. This supplies W26.CONTROL and CONTROL; it does not waive mode, mapping, or release limits.

## Difficulty and inspiration

Requires composing execution, identity, and the separate live-action authorization constraints.

**Inspiration:** [apexsurvive (HTB Cyber Apocalypse 2024; Insane)](../../reference-catalog.md#c08). Adapts the later renderer/action portion of apexsurvive; this is separate from K12's complete supplier browser chain.
