## Challenge description — player-facing

Recover the policy program embedded in the retained rules compiler and identify the input it actually evaluates.

## Story purpose

Makes the compiler's hidden policy an inspectable campaign artifact.

## Prerequisites and starting position

**Required access or evidence:** [G01 — K01.1](../K01/K01.1.md).

**Starting material:** The retained FieldKest rules-compiler artifact, example policy inputs, and its acceptance interface.

**Campaign references:** No separately named capability output.

## Completion and downstream use

Recover the actual policy entry and input binding. A filename or compiler banner is insufficient.

## Difficulty and inspiration

A focused artifact investigation with normal examples.

**Inspiration:** [Metagaming (HTB Cyber Apocalypse 2024; Hard)](../../reference-catalog.md#c07). Adapts Metagaming's compile-time program-analysis challenge to package policy.
