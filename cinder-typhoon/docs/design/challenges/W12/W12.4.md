## Challenge description — player-facing

Reconstruct the surviving collection relationship and obtain the current protected data associated with the archived maintenance identity.

## Story purpose

Makes old intelligence useful without substituting it for current plant observations.

## Prerequisites and starting position

**Required access or evidence:** [W12.3](W12.3.md).

**Starting material:** A quarantined historical collector, its retained collection records, an encrypted archive, and a referenced current data service.

**Campaign references:** No separately named capability output.

## Completion and downstream use

Recover fresh data through the still-restricted collection service with the correct current identity and revision. Replaying the historical archive is insufficient.

## Difficulty and inspiration

An authored cross-revision identity and collection-protocol composition beyond archive recovery.

**Inspiration:** [Confinement (HTB Cyber Apocalypse 2024; Hard)](../../reference-catalog.md#c17). Confinement supplies collector and archive reconstruction. The historical-to-current collection continuation is an authored extension.
