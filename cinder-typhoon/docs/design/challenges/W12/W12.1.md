## Challenge description — player-facing

Recover the historical collector associated with the archive lead and restore it as a usable analysis artifact.

## Story purpose

Continues the intelligence trail from contractor or lineage evidence.

## Prerequisites and starting position

**Required access or evidence:** [W08.4](../W08/W08.4.md) OR [W11.3](../W11/W11.3.md).

**Starting material:** A quarantined historical collector, its retained collection records, an encrypted archive, and a referenced current data service.

**Campaign references:** No separately named capability output.

## Completion and downstream use

Recover the actual collector and its archive binding. There is no objective to deploy it or damage data.

## Difficulty and inspiration

A bounded recovery task with a known collection reference.

**Inspiration:** [Confinement (HTB Cyber Apocalypse 2024; Hard)](../../reference-catalog.md#c17). Confinement supplies collector and archive reconstruction. The historical-to-current collection continuation is an authored extension.
