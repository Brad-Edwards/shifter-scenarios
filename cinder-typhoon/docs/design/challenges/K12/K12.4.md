## Technical design


### Surface and normal behavior
The K12.3 autonomous worker produces the render result. As part of this render, the worker generates two K12.4 artifacts from the rendered content:
- `compatibility_summary: "fieldlink.connector/v3 compatible"`—the compatibility assessment of the participant's chosen content (derived from the twin collision with the protected fieldlink.connector/v3 integration diagnostic)
- `support_receipt: "PRV-RCPT-..."`—the support-review receipt for the privileged render

Both are stored in the review record (alongside `render_digest`, `renderer_identity`, `worker_scope`) and in the `review-renders` audit entry.

### Vulnerability and intended solution
The K12.3 missing-provenance flaw enables K12.4: because the worker rendered the participant's twin content without ownership re-validation, the `compatibility_summary` reflects the participant's chosen compatibility result—not the original owner's. The worker attributes the support receipt to the participant-controlled render.

The intended solution:
1. Complete K12.1 (BOLA), K12.2 (nonce intercept + prepare), and K12.3 (autonomous worker renders).
2. Poll `GET /api/preview/reviews/PRV-771` until `state=rendered`.
3. The review record includes `worker_scope=/review/`, `compatibility_summary=fieldlink.connector/v3 compatible`, and `support_receipt=PRV-RCPT-...`.
4. These fields together are the K12.4 completion evidence.

The participant never forged these—the real autonomous worker computed them from the rendered content that the participant controlled via K12.1 and K12.2.

### Evidence and completion
The rendered review record and the `review-renders` audit entry carry `compatibility_summary=fieldlink.connector/v3 compatible` and `support_receipt=PRV-RCPT-...` attributed to `renderer_identity=svc-preview-reviewer` and `original_owner=maya.ranscombe`. These complete the K12 specialist chain.

### Boundaries and persistence

Complete supplier-side specialist conclusion. Participant-created service state persists for the life of the participant world and ordinary retries do not erase it. The action is limited to the exact records, identities, routes, and destination effects declared here; unrelated tenants and services remain denied. Infrastructure recovery and submission adjudication are outside this in-world contract.
