## Challenge description — player-facing

Complete the chain into the constrained renderer and make the support engineer's release preview reflect Cinder's chosen result.

## Story purpose

Delivers a memorable local conclusion to the long browser investigation.

## Prerequisites and starting position

**Required access or evidence:** [K12.3](K12.3.md).

**Starting material:** An ordinary release-preview account, preview application material, and a private support-review rehearsal.

**Campaign references:** No separately named capability output.

## Completion and downstream use

Demonstrate execution in the renderer's actual context and the changed consumed preview. Its scope remains the supplier's preview workflow.

The conclusion lets the player inspect the release document as the support engineer actually received it, alongside the ordinary version. The chosen change survives the privileged rendering path and appears inside the otherwise recognizable document. The comparison uses the proven renderer result; a local mock-up or decorative completion banner cannot substitute for it. This is a complete specialist payoff even if the player never reaches ARWC.

## Difficulty and inspiration

**Inspiration:** [apexsurvive (HTB Cyber Apocalypse 2024; Insane)](../../reference-catalog.md#c08). Retains apexsurvive's demanding browser-to-renderer composition in the supplier preview workflow.
