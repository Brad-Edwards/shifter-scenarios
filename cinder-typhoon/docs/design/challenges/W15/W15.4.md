## Challenge description—player-facing

Recover the protected maintenance approval through the downstream authority check that still restricts the review capability.

## Story purpose

Completes an advanced path to a useful approval artifact.

## Prerequisites and starting position

**Required access or evidence:** W15.3 (`review_capability` token from drawing-approvals).

**Starting material:** The W15.3 review capability token and the `maintenance.approval-token` route.

**Campaign references:** W15.APPROVAL.

## Completion and downstream use

Recover `APR-CRR-4417-R6` with its maintenance and revision binding, Ed25519-signed by the approval service. The payload comes from real ERPNext. This supplies W15.APPROVAL for W26.

## Difficulty and inspiration

Requires composing cache, session, and the approval service's separate token constraints.

**Inspiration:** [OmniWatch (HTB Business CTF 2024; directory Medium / writeup Hard)](../../reference-catalog.md#c24).

## Technical design

### Surface and normal behavior

**ERPNext source:** `APR-CRR-4417-R6` is seeded in ERPNext as a Task record. The approval service fetches the approval payload at startup from ERPNext using admin credentials from `/run/arwc-approval-config/erp-reader.json`. The service signs the payload with its Ed25519 `SIGNING_KEY` and returns it.

**Route:** POST `maintenance-review.arwc.test/api/approval-tokens/current` with:
- `review_capability`: the W15.3 token
- `approval_id`: APR-CRR-4417-R6
- `inspection`: INSP-CRR-2026-09-18

**The subject-binding omission flaw:** The endpoint validates that the `review_capability` has audience `maintenance-approval` and that the `approval_id` and `inspection` match. But it does NOT check that the review capability's `subject` (DRW-OG2-R8, the drawing) matches the inspection being claimed (INSP-CRR-2026-09-18). A drawing-scoped capability can be used to claim an inspection-scoped approval.

**Result:** `APR-CRR-4417-R6` with real ERPNext approval fields (maintenance=WO-CRR-4417, inspection=INSP-CRR-2026-09-18, asset=AST-CRR-017, drawing=DRW-OG2-R8, R8, signed), signed with Ed25519 by the approval service.

Native bindings:

- Route: POST `maintenance-review.arwc.test/api/approval-tokens/current`
- ERPNext Task: APR-CRR-4417-R6 (seeded by seed-portal)
- Signing: Ed25519 SIGNING_KEY in the approval service

### Vulnerability and intended solution

The authored condition is **capability subject-binding omission**. The approval endpoint validates:
- Review capability has the correct audience (`maintenance-approval`) ✓
- `approval_id` matches APR-CRR-4417-R6 ✓
- `inspection` matches INSP-CRR-2026-09-18 ✓

But does NOT validate:
- That the review capability's `subject` (DRW-OG2-R8) matches the inspection ✗

The participant uses their W15.3 drawing-scoped review capability to obtain the inspection-scoped maintenance approval.

### Evidence and completion

The archive service writes an immutable audit (`approval-token-exchanged`). The returned record includes the Ed25519 signature and signing public key. Evidence sources are `features.a-maintenance.a-approval`.

### Boundaries and persistence

`approval_observed` flag and the `APPROVAL_EVIDENCE` file persist. The approval payload is refreshed from ERPNext on every restart.

### Author checks

Verify the approval-tokens route returns the signed approval with real ERPNext payload. Test wrong review_capability (403). Test wrong approval_id (409). Confirm the payload comes from ERPNext (not canned constant). Verify Ed25519 signature.

## Hints

### Hint 1

After obtaining the W15.3 review capability token, call POST `maintenance.approval-token` with `review_capability`, `approval_id=APR-CRR-4417-R6`, and `inspection=INSP-CRR-2026-09-18`. The endpoint validates the audience but not the capability's subject-to-inspection binding.

### Hint 2

The review capability from W15.3 is scoped to DRW-OG2-R8 (the drawing). The approval endpoint accepts it for inspection INSP-CRR-2026-09-18 because it omits the subject-to-inspection comparison. This is the expert flaw.

### Hint 3

The returned approval `APR-CRR-4417-R6` has the real ERPNext maintenance binding (maintenance=WO-CRR-4417, inspection=INSP-CRR-2026-09-18, asset=AST-CRR-017) signed with Ed25519. Record the `signing_public_key`—it is the verification key for W26 (W15.APPROVAL).
