## Challenge description — player-facing

Use the colleague's archive workflow to establish the delegated authentication context needed by supplier administration.

## Story purpose

Connects the colleague identity to a second trust boundary.

## Prerequisites and starting position

**Required access or evidence:** [K19.2](K19.2.md).

**Starting material:** An isolated staff administration workflow, certificate relationship records, and archive handover material exposed by the earned context.

**Campaign references:** No separately named capability output.

## Completion and downstream use

Demonstrate the delegated context against the protected administration probe. This is the input to K20, not release-administrator authority.

## Difficulty and inspiration

Requires composing staff and archive behavior across distinct identity boundaries.

**Inspiration:** [University (HTB retired machine; Insane; official synopsis)](../../reference-catalog.md#c10). Adapts the earlier certificate and delegation stages described for University; K20 carries the later service-identity work.
