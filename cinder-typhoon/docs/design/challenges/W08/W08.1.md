## Challenge description — player-facing

Recover the contractor's hidden script from the retained work material and identify the collection it was intended to retrieve.

## Story purpose

Starts an intelligence-recovery branch from ordinary corporate work.

## Prerequisites and starting position

**Required access or evidence:** [W05.3](../W05/W05.3.md) OR [W03.READ](../../capability-routes.json).

**Starting material:** A retained contractor work bundle identified either by the staff assignment in W05.3 or the handover accompanying W03.READ. Both routes expose the same bundle for analysis; the read-integration credential is not being reused as a corporate account.

**Campaign references:** No separately named capability output.

## Completion and downstream use

Recover the actual script and its collection binding. Finding a suspicious filename alone is insufficient.

## Difficulty and inspiration

A bounded artifact recovery with a clear contractor context.

**Inspiration:** [Tales for the Brave (HTB Cyber Apocalypse 2025; index four stars / writeup Medium)](../../reference-catalog.md#c20). Adapts Tales for the Brave's script, messaging, and compiled-code analysis; its published ratings disagree as recorded in the catalog.
