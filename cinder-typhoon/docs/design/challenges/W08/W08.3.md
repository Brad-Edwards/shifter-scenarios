## Challenge description — player-facing

Recover the compiled collector carried by the reconstructed exchange and establish its usable analysis artifact.

## Story purpose

Adds a distinct compiled-code recovery task.

## Prerequisites and starting position

**Required access or evidence:** [W08.2](W08.2.md).

**Starting material:** A contractor's retained work artifacts, local messaging records, and references to an older collection tool.

**Campaign references:** No separately named capability output.

## Completion and downstream use

Recover the complete collector with the correct handover binding. A partial payload or an unrelated binary is insufficient.

## Difficulty and inspiration

A multi-format artifact reconstruction inspired by Tales for the Brave.

**Inspiration:** [Tales for the Brave (HTB Cyber Apocalypse 2025; index four stars / writeup Medium)](../../reference-catalog.md#c20). Adapts Tales for the Brave's script, messaging, and compiled-code analysis; its published ratings disagree as recorded in the catalog.
