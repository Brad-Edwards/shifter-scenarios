## Challenge description — player-facing

Cross the linked-document service's separate access boundary and recover the full restricted planning record behind that excerpt.

## Story purpose

Turns partial disclosure into a complete protected planning artifact.

## Prerequisites and starting position

**Required access or evidence:** [W10.2](W10.2.md).

**Starting material:** The district-report assistant's source records, ordinary search interface, and linked planning documents.

**Campaign references:** No separately named capability output.

## Completion and downstream use

Recover the correct district's full record and revision. Repeating the excerpt or generating a summary earns no new credit. No plant authority is granted.

## Difficulty and inspiration

Requires a distinct document-ownership decision after the retrieval disclosure.

**Inspiration:** [ATLAS application-trust inspiration](../../threat-inspiration.md#atlas). ATLAS informs the data-isolation theme; these objectives use deterministic retrieval, not costly model attacks.

## Technical design


### Surface and normal behavior

`a-business` and its `business-workplace` application own this part of **The document behind the answer**. Normal behavior applies the declared identity, tenant, object, revision, and process bindings and returns or changes only the caller-scoped object. The exact initial state is: Linked-document route authorizes possession of excerpt EXC-MER-DP3-4 but does not compare the document district; it returns full record PLN-MER-DP3-R6 and its revision.

Native bindings:

- `a-corporate.a-business/business-workplace/the-document-behind-the-answer`

The private service-loader contract is `content.arwc-a-business.w10-3-the-document-behind-the-answer`. It freezes accepted and denied requests, state transition, persistence, and evidence; it does not seed a successful result.

### Vulnerability and intended solution

The authored condition is **linked-object district authorization omission**. Linked-document route authorizes possession of excerpt EXC-MER-DP3-4 but does not compare the document district; it returns full record PLN-MER-DP3-R6 and its revision. The hand build must preserve every named version, encoding, bound, validation order, identity, and unaffected field; substituting a generic vulnerable service would change the task.

The required result is: Recover the correct district's full record and revision. Repeating the excerpt or generating a summary earns no new credit. No plant authority is granted.

### Evidence and completion

The owning service writes an immutable audit for the accepted request and result. Caller-supplied output, a local copy, or knowledge of an identifier is not sufficient. Completion is evaluated from fresh service-owned evidence after the action. It cannot issue the identity, create the mutation, move a simulated actuator, or fabricate a downstream acceptance needed to prove itself.

Evidence sources are `features.a-corporate.a-business`. A wrong identifier, stale revision, replayed one-time value, different checkpoint, or cross-tenant artifact is rejected.

### Boundaries and persistence

The result remains local evidence unless a downstream workflow explicitly names it. Tenant-scoped mutations, sessions that have not expired, generated artifacts, and audits persist across ordinary retries and later sessions. Initial immutable records remain unchanged unless the named route explicitly permits a field-level revision. Infrastructure recovery and submission adjudication are external to the SDL.

The behavior is limited to the named fictional objects and bounded service operation. It provides no general shell, arbitrary network target, unrelated district access, real process interface, or authority not expressly declared by the card.

### Author checks

Build and test the normal baseline first. Exercise the exact authored path and verify the named result plus its immutable audit. Test wrong authority, wrong object or district, stale revision, replay, another tenant instance, and every declared constraint or unaffected record. Repeat the accepted action according to its idempotence rule and confirm ordinary retries preserve the result. For a joined result, verify each destination observed the same correlation and that removal of any independently owned source prevents completion.
