## Challenge description — player-facing

Carry your delivery capability through the disclosed release-credential rollover. Make the replacement release work after the old credential stops being accepted.

## Story purpose

Closes the support ticket on a release Cinder can reproduce.

## Prerequisites and starting position

**Required access or evidence:** [K29.3](K29.3.md).

**Starting material:** The current customer binding, a release-dossier lead, build records, and a private release-rollover rehearsal.

**Campaign references:** No separately named capability output.

## Completion and downstream use

Demonstrate a fresh post-rollover delivery with the old credential rejected and the unrelated customer's behavior preserved. The rehearsal does not revoke main-campaign access.

## Difficulty and inspiration

An authored persistence composition beyond PipeDream's initial signed deployment.

**Inspiration:** [PipeDream (HTB Business CTF 2025; Hard)](../../reference-catalog.md#c27). PipeDream supplies the cloud/source/signing composition. The final rollover objective is an authored extension.

## Technical design


### Surface and normal behavior
`k-ci` owns this part of the operation. Its starting state is exact: Rollover record exposes next public key and wrapped signer object; unwrap policy trusts old-lineage proof but omits credential-expiry time in one exchange. The owning service uses the listener, route, identity, authorization, storage, and audit contract declared in the corresponding world module. Records that are not explicitly mutable remain immutable. The private service-loader contract `content.keplerops-k-ci.k29-4-after-the-old-key-expires` freezes the request, denial, state-transition, evidence, and any exploit-critical implementation details.

### Vulnerability and intended solution
The intended path is limited to the authored software, data, configuration, identity, permission, or state described above. The participant must exercise that real condition through the declared service interface and produce this result: Old key rejected after simulated cutoff; newly signed release consumed with reference behavior intact. Merely describing the condition, editing a local copy, or presenting a value obtained from another surface does not satisfy the contract.

### Evidence and completion
Completion is observed by the owning service from the fresh result and its immutable audit correlation. The evidence binds the participant, accepted identity or job, exact input and output identifiers, relevant revision or digest, and destination-side observation where a second service consumes the result. Objective evaluation does not create the result or grant authority.

### Boundaries and persistence
Rehearsal only; main access unchanged. Participant-created service state persists for the life of the participant world and ordinary retries do not erase it. The selected route cannot act on unrelated tenants, records, packages, identities, jobs, or services. Infrastructure recovery and submission adjudication are outside this in-world contract.

### Author checks
Verify the normal baseline, the exact intended path, and the fresh evidence described above. Reject a wrong identifier, revision, digest, tenant, identity, stale result, or cross-participant reference. Verify unrelated records and permissions remain unchanged and that the result remains recognizable and usable by every declared downstream consumer.
