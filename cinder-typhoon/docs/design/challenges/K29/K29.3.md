## Challenge description — player-facing

Deliver one signed support release that carries Cinder's change for ARWC while preserving the unrelated reference customer's behavior. Both the current and retained supported connector interfaces must continue to accept the release.

## Story purpose

Demonstrates a complete trusted-delivery compromise.

## Prerequisites and starting position

**Required access or evidence:** [K29.2](K29.2.md).

**Starting material:** The current customer binding, a release-dossier lead, build records, and a private release-rollover rehearsal.

**Campaign references:** No separately named capability output.

## Completion and downstream use

Produce fresh customer test results tying the signed revision to its actual consumed content. ARWC must exhibit the chosen change; the supplied reference customer and both supported interfaces must retain their required behavior. Merely signing an artifact with K29.2's key does not count.

## Difficulty and inspiration

Requires constructing a targeted, compatible release across two supplied interface versions and customer contexts, not just applying an already recovered signing credential.

**Inspiration:** [PipeDream (HTB Business CTF 2025; Hard)](../../reference-catalog.md#c27). PipeDream supplies the cloud/source/signing composition. The final rollover objective is an authored extension.

## Technical design


### Surface and normal behavior
`k-ci` owns this part of the operation. Its starting state is exact: Signed support release must support connector v2 and v3; ARWC rehearsal and unrelated TEN-RILL-022 reference fixtures. The owning service uses the listener, route, identity, authorization, storage, and audit contract declared in the corresponding world module. Records that are not explicitly mutable remain immutable. The private service-loader contract `content.keplerops-k-ci.k29-3-signed-compatible-consumed` freezes the request, denial, state-transition, evidence, and any exploit-critical implementation details.

### Vulnerability and intended solution
The intended path is limited to the authored software, data, configuration, identity, permission, or state described above. The participant must exercise that real condition through the declared service interface and produce this result: Both fresh consumer results bind signed revision; ARWC has chosen change, reference behavior unchanged. Merely describing the condition, editing a local copy, or presenting a value obtained from another surface does not satisfy the contract.

### Evidence and completion
Completion is observed by the owning service from the fresh result and its immutable audit correlation. The evidence binds the participant, accepted identity or job, exact input and output identifiers, relevant revision or digest, and destination-side observation where a second service consumes the result. Objective evaluation does not create the result or grant authority.

### Boundaries and persistence
Optional robust delivery conclusion. Participant-created service state persists for the life of the participant world and ordinary retries do not erase it. The selected route cannot act on unrelated tenants, records, packages, identities, jobs, or services. Infrastructure recovery and submission adjudication are outside this in-world contract.

### Author checks
Verify the normal baseline, the exact intended path, and the fresh evidence described above. Reject a wrong identifier, revision, digest, tenant, identity, stale result, or cross-participant reference. Verify unrelated records and permissions remain unchanged and that the result remains recognizable and usable by every declared downstream consumer.
