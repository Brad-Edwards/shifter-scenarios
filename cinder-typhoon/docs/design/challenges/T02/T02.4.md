## Challenge description — player-facing

Your recovered account can list its own assignments. Obtain the other operator's sealed rehearsal brief through the assignment service.

## Story purpose

Introduces authorization beyond successful authentication.

## Prerequisites and starting position

**Required access or evidence:** [T02.3](T02.3.md).

**Starting material:** The rehearsal identity desk, candidate account notes, a public directory, recovery workflow, and assignment service.

**Campaign references:** No separately named capability output.

## Completion and downstream use

Recover the specified other operator's private brief while retaining the recovered account's identity. Reading its own handover is not new credit.

## Difficulty and inspiration

A bounded ownership and assignment-binding problem.

**Inspiration:** Original challenge for this campaign.

## Technical design

### Surface and normal behavior

The recovered session's assignment list contains only Rhea's `ASG-204`. Its own
brief is revision 3 and names paired dispatch assignment `ASG-317`, providing a
fair record lead. `ASG-317` is revision 5 and belongs to `dax.hale`. `GET
/api/assignments/{assignment_id}/brief` requires the recovered session and logs
the session subject, requested ID, document revision, status, and UTC time.

### Vulnerability and intended solution

Native application RBAC maps the recovered Rhea principal to an intentionally
over-broad `read` grant on `assignment-briefs/*`. The list correctly filters by
owner, but the brief route checks only whether the subject owns any assignment;
it never compares the requested record's owner. Requesting discovered
`ASG-317` therefore returns Dax's brief. No session returns 401 and an unknown
assignment returns 404.

### Evidence and completion

The assignment service returns the specified other operator's brief and
records the authenticated subject, requested assignment, and document revision.
Reading the caller's own brief remains an ordinary separately identifiable read.

### Boundaries

Only two synthetic training assignments exist and document access is read-only.
The flaw cannot enumerate arbitrary accounts or cross into KeplerOps. Repeated
reads and unsuccessful requests preserve the recovered session and all records.

### Author checks

Check own-assignment access, denial without a recovered session, the intended
cross-assignment read, rejection of unknown assignments, and exact audit
fields. Repeated reads and unsuccessful requests must preserve the session.
