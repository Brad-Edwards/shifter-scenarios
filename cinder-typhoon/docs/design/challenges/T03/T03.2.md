## Challenge description — player-facing

Recover the release access exposed by the failed training build and retrieve the protected rehearsal-channel manifest.

## Story purpose

Establishes a limited release capability with a concrete result.

## Prerequisites and starting position

**Required access or evidence:** the supplied training workspace.

**Starting material:** The training repository, retained build diagnostics, a sample package, and the rehearsal publication/consumer interfaces.

**Campaign references:** No separately named capability output.

## Completion and downstream use

Recover and demonstrate the scoped publication access through the protected manifest. This supplies T03.4; copying a credential alone is insufficient.

## Difficulty and inspiration

A short build-artifact and scoped-access task.

**Inspiration:** Original challenge for this campaign.

## Technical design

### Surface and normal behavior

`developer.training:8082/jobs/BLD-204/log` publishes the retained failed release
log. It names registry `developer.training:8083`, package
`@cinder/delivery-formatter`, and the synthetic publisher bearer credential.
The registry protects `GET /api/channels/rehearsal/manifest`; its manifest is
revision 6 with baseline 1.4.0, consumer range `>=1.4.0 <2.0.0`, no prereleases,
and highest-compatible selection.

### Vulnerability and intended solution

The failed job logs the still-valid raw publisher value. Native application RBAC
grants its principal only `read` on `channels/rehearsal/manifest` and `publish`
on `packages/@cinder/delivery-formatter`. The intended solution retrieves the
protected manifest with that bearer value. Missing/wrong access returns 401 and
another package or administrative scope returns 403.

### Evidence and completion

Registry audit records the scoped identity reading the existing rehearsal
manifest and its revision. Reading it demonstrates existing access; it does
not award publication permission. The same credential already carries the
publication scope used by the later formatter task.

### Boundaries

The credential can read its one manifest and publish only the training formatter
scope; it cannot alter build configuration or other packages. Its permissions
exist before manifest retrieval and remain unchanged by that read.

### Author checks

Check readable failed-job records, correct credential scope, the protected
manifest response, denial for another package scope, and publication authority
that exists independently of manifest retrieval.
