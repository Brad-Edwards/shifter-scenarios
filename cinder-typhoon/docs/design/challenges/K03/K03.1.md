## Challenge description — player-facing

Someone from Cinder worked this lead before you. Recover the staging artifact buried in their retained document and identify the collection it belongs to.

## Story purpose

Connects the current operation to an earlier unfinished attempt.

## Prerequisites and starting position

**Required access or evidence:** the supplied KeplerOps developer foothold.

**Starting material:** A retained staging bundle from an earlier Cinder attempt, its carrier document, and a short collection note.

**Campaign references:** No separately named capability output.

## Completion and downstream use

Recover the actual staging artifact and its collection binding. It supplies the material for K03.2, not the final archive contents.

## Difficulty and inspiration

A bounded carrier/artifact investigation rather than a large forensic image.

**Inspiration:** [Game Invitation (HTB Cyber Apocalypse 2024; Hard)](../../reference-catalog.md#c04). The inspiration is document and script reconstruction; the objective remains collection for Cinder.

## Technical design


### Surface and normal behavior
A predecessor's retained document is a versioned local artifact that names a staging object and its collection identifier in the FieldKest archive.

### Vulnerability and intended solution
The document has been partially redacted in its current rendering while its ordinary source attachment preserves the staging artifact. The intended solution is recover-and-validate, not a blind archive search.

### Evidence and completion
The artifact digest and collection binding are recorded as operator evidence and become the sole input to K03.2.

### Boundaries and persistence

Starts optional predecessor thread Participant-created service state persists for the life of the participant world and ordinary retries do not erase it. The action is limited to the exact records, identities, routes, and destination effects declared here; unrelated tenants and services remain denied. Infrastructure recovery and submission adjudication are outside this in-world contract.

### Author checks
Confirm the visible document provides a lead, the source attachment yields one artifact, its collection is explicit, random artifact IDs fail.
