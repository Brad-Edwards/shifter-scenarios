## Challenge description — player-facing

Establish a separate retained delivery option through the customer's rollback workflow, even when the current release selection has moved on.

## Story purpose

Adds an optional supplier persistence conclusion after initial delivery.

## Prerequisites and starting position

**Required access or evidence:** [K26.2](K26.2.md).

**Starting material:** Earned publication authority, G02's customer binding, the consumed-package contract, and the customer's ordinary activation workflow.

**Campaign references:** No separately named capability output.

## Completion and downstream use

Demonstrate a fresh consumption through the distinct rollback/version-selection weakness in a private future-state rehearsal. Replaying the first execution is insufficient.

## Difficulty and inspiration

A separate version-selection boundary with a disclosed rollback contract.

**Inspiration:** [Prison Pipeline (HTB Business CTF 2024; organizer Medium / writeup Easy)](../../reference-catalog.md#c01). Adapts Prison Pipeline's package-consumption mechanism. The K20 authority alternative is optional; the A01 route is the ordinary medium route.
