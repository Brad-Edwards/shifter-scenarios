## Challenge description — player-facing

Make the active ARWC connector select and run your published package. Demonstrate the changed behavior from the customer's side.

## Story purpose

Pays off the organization transition with the same ticket under new letterhead.

## Prerequisites and starting position

**Required access or evidence:** [K26.1](K26.1.md).

**Starting material:** Earned publication authority, G02's customer binding, the consumed-package contract, and the customer's ordinary activation workflow.

**Campaign references:** `A04`.

## Completion and downstream use

Resolve actual consumer activation and demonstrate execution in FieldKest's limited ARWC corporate context. This supplies A04 and CORPORATE; an upload or registry receipt alone is insufficient.

The conclusion pairs the supplied release with the ARWC connector's actual changed output and customer identity. The maintenance ticket the player knew from KeplerOps now appears in its customer context under ARWC's name. The effect is their package running at the victim, with the existing execution evidence making that transition tangible. Customer identity and a familiar ticket alone are not proof of execution.

## Difficulty and inspiration

A documented activation and consumer-selection problem after a compatible package exists.

**Inspiration:** [Prison Pipeline (HTB Business CTF 2024; organizer Medium / writeup Easy)](../../reference-catalog.md#c01). Adapts Prison Pipeline's package-consumption mechanism. The K20 authority alternative is optional; the A01 route is the ordinary medium route.
