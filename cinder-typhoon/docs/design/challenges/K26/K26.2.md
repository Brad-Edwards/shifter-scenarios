## Challenge description — player-facing

Make the active ARWC connector select and run your published package. Demonstrate the changed behavior from the customer's side.

## Story purpose

Pays off the organization transition with the same ticket under new letterhead.

## Prerequisites and starting position

**Required access or evidence:** [K26.1](K26.1.md).

**Starting material:** Earned publication authority, G02's customer binding, the consumed-package contract, and the customer's ordinary activation workflow.

**Campaign references:** `A04`.

## Completion and downstream use

Resolve actual consumer activation and demonstrate execution in FieldKest's limited ARWC corporate context. This supplies A04 and CORPORATE; an upload or registry receipt alone is insufficient.

The conclusion pairs the supplied release with the ARWC connector's actual changed output and customer identity. The maintenance ticket the operator knew from KeplerOps now appears in its customer context under ARWC's name. The effect is their package running at the victim, with the existing execution evidence making that transition tangible. Customer identity and a familiar ticket alone are not proof of execution.

## Difficulty and inspiration

A documented activation and consumer-selection problem after a compatible package exists.

**Inspiration:** [Prison Pipeline (HTB Business CTF 2024; organizer Medium / writeup Easy)](../../reference-catalog.md#c01). Adapts Prison Pipeline's package-consumption mechanism. The K20 authority alternative is optional; the A01 route is the ordinary medium route.

## Technical design


### Surface and normal behavior
`a-connector` owns this part of the operation. Its starting state is exact: Customer poll/activation endpoint accepts only signed approved active-tenant channel; connector implements fieldkest.connector/v3. The owning service uses the listener, route, identity, authorization, storage, and audit contract declared in the corresponding world module. Records that are not explicitly mutable remain immutable. The private service-loader contract `content.keplerops-a-connector.k26-2-customer-accepted` freezes the request, denial, state-transition, evidence, and any exploit-critical implementation details.

### Vulnerability and intended solution
The intended path is limited to the authored software, data, configuration, identity, permission, or state described above. The participant must exercise that real condition through the declared service interface and produce this result: Consumer selects exact version, executes bounded diagnostic_summary, and returns customer-side output plus receipt. Merely describing the condition, editing a local copy, or presenting a value obtained from another surface does not satisfy the contract.

### Evidence and completion
Completion is observed by the owning service from the fresh result and its immutable audit correlation. The evidence binds the participant, accepted identity or job, exact input and output identifiers, relevant revision or digest, and destination-side observation where a second service consumes the result. Objective evaluation does not create the result or grant authority.

### Boundaries and persistence

Supplies A04 and CORPORATE; no other ARWC design implied Participant-created service state persists for the life of the participant world and ordinary retries do not erase it. The action is limited to the exact records, identities, routes, and destination effects declared here; unrelated tenants and services remain denied. Infrastructure recovery and submission adjudication are outside this in-world contract.

### Author checks
Verify the normal baseline, the exact intended path, and the fresh evidence described above. Reject a wrong identifier, revision, digest, tenant, identity, stale result, or cross-participant reference. Verify unrelated records and permissions remain unchanged and that the result remains recognizable and usable by every declared downstream consumer.
