## Challenge description — player-facing

Recover the restricted district quote that your corporate context is not authorized to view.

## Story purpose

Adds a concrete procurement-data boundary.

## Prerequisites and starting position

**Required access or evidence:** [W35.1](W35.1.md).

**Starting material:** The corporate procurement catalog, district quote references, and the replacement-supply ordering workflow.

**Campaign references:** No separately named capability output.

## Completion and downstream use

Recover the actual quote with its district and purchasing binding. Public list prices alone do not satisfy the objective.

## Difficulty and inspiration

A bounded quote-ownership and district-association problem.

**Inspiration:** Original challenge for this campaign.
