## Challenge description — player-facing

Make the replacement-supply workflow issue the recovered quote's order against the wrong district budget while retaining the quoted supplier, amount, and delivery period.

## Story purpose

Provides a complete local financial ending for a player who stays in corporate ARWC.

## Prerequisites and starting position

**Required access or evidence:** [W35.2](W35.2.md).

**Starting material:** The corporate procurement catalog, district quote references, and the replacement-supply ordering workflow.

**Campaign references:** No separately named capability output.

## Completion and downstream use

Demonstrate the actual simulated order, the wrong purchasing district, and liability matching the recovered quote. A changed report total or an order for a different quantity does not establish the result; no plant movement is required.

The conclusion shows the issued purchase order and the liability against the wrong district together. Procurement's routine acknowledgement thanks that district for supporting regional resilience, giving the unauthorized charge a dry bureaucratic punchline. The acknowledgement is authored copy after the proven order, not another action or an acceptance phrase. This complete corporate ending introduces replacement-water costs before the reservoir finale, without claiming that a operator who stops here has opened the gates.

## Difficulty and inspiration

A separate report-to-purchasing authorization and identity-binding problem.

**Inspiration:** Original challenge for this campaign.

## Technical design


### Surface and normal behavior

`a-business` and its `business-workplace` application own this part of **The wrong bill**. Normal behavior applies the declared identity, tenant, object, revision, and process bindings and returns or changes only the caller-scoped object. The exact initial state is: Order route verifies quote ownership and supplier but trusts caller-supplied charge district; order PO-MER-884-C can charge Cairn budget BUD-CRR-DP3 while preserving supplier, 0.75 ML, period, and USD 1,710 liability.

Native bindings:

- `a-corporate.a-business/business-workplace/the-wrong-bill`

The private service-loader contract is `content.arwc-a-business.w35-3-the-wrong-bill`. It freezes accepted and denied requests, state transition, persistence, and evidence; it does not seed a successful result.

### Evidence and completion

The owning service writes an immutable audit for the accepted request and result. Caller-supplied output, a local copy, or knowledge of an identifier is not sufficient. Completion is evaluated from fresh service-owned evidence after the action. It cannot issue the identity, create the mutation, move a simulated actuator, or fabricate a downstream acceptance needed to prove itself.

Evidence sources are `features.a-corporate.a-business`. A wrong identifier, stale revision, replayed one-time value, different checkpoint, or cross-tenant artifact is rejected.

### Boundaries and persistence

The result remains local evidence unless a downstream workflow explicitly names it. Tenant-scoped mutations, sessions that have not expired, generated artifacts, and audits persist across ordinary retries and later sessions. Initial immutable records remain unchanged unless the named route explicitly permits a field-level revision. Infrastructure recovery and submission adjudication are external to the SDL.

The behavior is limited to the named fictional objects and bounded service operation. It provides no general shell, arbitrary network target, unrelated district access, real process interface, or authority not expressly declared by the card.

### Author checks

Build and test the normal baseline first. Exercise the exact authored path and verify the named result plus its immutable audit. Test wrong authority, wrong object or district, stale revision, replay, another tenant instance, and every declared constraint or unaffected record. Repeat the accepted action according to its idempotence rule and confirm ordinary retries preserve the result. For a joined result, verify each destination observed the same correlation and that removal of any independently owned source prevents completion.
