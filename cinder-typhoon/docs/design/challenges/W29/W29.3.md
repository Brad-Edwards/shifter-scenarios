## Challenge description — player-facing

Compare corporate report `RPT-CRR-DP3-R7` with the later good-quality native usable-volume observation, calculate their signed difference, and explain what their different as-of times allow you to conclude.

## Story purpose

Makes the participant reconcile paper and process evidence without inventing a cause or treating a stale report as current physical truth.

## Prerequisites and starting position

**Required access or evidence:** ([W29.1](W29.1.md) AND [W29.2](W29.2.md)).

**Starting material:** The corporate reserve report, the W29 allocation export and method note, and the native historian interval used for W29.2.

**Campaign references:** `W29.PLAN`.

## Completion and downstream use

Calculate `signed paper variance = reported usable volume - later measured usable volume`, classify its direction from the actual values, and record both source times and units. Conclude that the dated paper report is not current process truth and that the differing as-of times do not by themselves establish a causal correction. This supplies W29.PLAN and CONSEQUENCE_PLAN as the verified current planning basis for later work, not as an executable control plan.

## Difficulty and inspiration

Medium: the participant must join independently owned records, retain the sign and provenance of the variance, and avoid overclaiming causality. Australian water-accounting guidance requires faithful representation and separates accrued commitments from physical flows; PostgreSQL's time types and consistent snapshots provide the ordinary tools for making the time basis explicit.

**Research:** [Australian Water Accounting Standard 1](https://www.bom.gov.au/water/standards/documents/ed_awas1_v1.0.pdf), [Bureau of Meteorology water-accounting concepts](https://www.bom.gov.au/water/standards/wasb/documents/Water-Accounting-Conceptual-Framework-Accessible.pdf), [PostgreSQL date and time functions](https://www.postgresql.org/docs/current/functions-datetime.html), [PostgreSQL transaction isolation](https://www.postgresql.org/docs/current/transaction-iso.html).

## Technical design

The installed Cairn Reach OT products own this part of **Paper truth**. The exact initial state is: Corporate report RPT-CRR-DP3-R7 records 13.40 ML as of 2026-11-09T00:00:00Z, while native history supplies a later measured usable volume; their signed difference is valid, but different as-of times mean the paper report cannot be presented as current process truth or assigned a causal correction without further evidence.

The image-owned W29 allocation export and reconciliation method explain the work, but do not replace the corporate PostgreSQL records or native process history. The current planning rows, historian timestamps, and process quality remain authoritative; no private service-loader route duplicates them.

### Vulnerability and intended solution

This is a bounded operating observation and interpretation task, not a privilege flaw. The participant must use the installed products to calculate the signed difference between the dated paper report and later native history, then state what the differing as-of times do and do not prove. A copied value, inferred default, or synthetic API response does not establish the result.

The required result is: Calculate `signed paper variance = reported usable volume - later measured usable volume`, classify its direction from the actual values, and record both source times and units. Conclude that the dated paper report is not current process truth and that the differing as-of times do not by themselves establish a causal correction. This supplies W29.PLAN and CONSEQUENCE_PLAN as the verified current planning basis for later work, not as an executable control plan.

### Evidence and completion

Completion compares the participant's observation with fresh, read-only product records. Product evidence is supplied by the range, never by the participant, and the completion path cannot write a tag, issue a command, change controller state, or create its own product evidence.

Evidence sources are `features.a-corporate.a-data`, `features.a-engineering.a-historian`. A wrong planning window, record revision, asset, source time, process quality, unit, signed calculation, or time-basis conclusion is rejected.

### Boundaries and persistence

Supplies W29.PLAN and CONSEQUENCE_PLAN as a verified current planning basis, not an executable control plan. The task is read-only and creates no process mutation to retain or reset. Ordinary historian retention continues independently. Infrastructure recovery and submission adjudication are external to the SDL.

The behavior is limited to the installed Cairn Reach products, the named asset, and the supplied participant material. It provides no alternate challenge service, simulated completion endpoint, or authority not already present in the OT environment.

## Hints

### Hint 1

Carry the paper report's `as_of` time beside the historian sample's `source_time` before comparing values.

### Hint 2

Use `reported - measured`; do not silently reverse the sign when naming the direction.

### Hint 3

A difference proves the records do not state the same current value. Different times alone do not prove which physical or administrative event caused it.
