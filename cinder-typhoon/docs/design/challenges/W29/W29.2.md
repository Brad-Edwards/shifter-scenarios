## Challenge description—player-facing

Select a fresh good-quality `UsableVolumeML` observation from native Cairn Reach history and reconcile it with the 12.00 ML allocation commitment to calculate the current uncommitted margin.

## Story purpose

Joins an accrued business obligation to measured plant state without flattening them into one synthetic record.

## Prerequisites and starting position

**Required access or evidence:** ([W29.1](W29.1.md) AND [W17.OBS](../../capability-routes.json)).

**Starting material:** The verified W29 planning records, the Cairn Reach history dashboard, and the supplied reconciliation method.

**Campaign references:** No separately named capability output.

## Completion and downstream use

Report the source time, process quality, unit, and value of a fresh `UsableVolumeML` sample, then calculate `uncommitted margin = measured usable volume - committed allocation`. State whether that current margin is positive, zero, or negative. A historical meter export or paper report is not a substitute for the current native observation.

## Difficulty and inspiration

Medium: the arithmetic is simple, but the participant must select a time-bounded, good-quality product sample and join it to the matching asset, unit, and planning window. EPA water-audit guidance distinguishes top-down records from bottom-up field measurements, while Grafana's PostgreSQL support makes the timestamped product series directly inspectable.

**Research:** [EPA Water Audits and Water Loss Control](https://www.epa.gov/sites/default/files/2015-04/documents/epa816f13002.pdf), [Grafana PostgreSQL data source](https://grafana.com/docs/grafana/latest/datasources/postgres/), [Grafana PostgreSQL time-series queries](https://grafana.com/docs/grafana/latest/datasources/postgres/query-editor/).

## Technical design


### Surface and normal behavior

The installed Cairn Reach OT products own this part of **What reserve remains uncommitted**. The exact initial state is: Current uncommitted margin is the latest good native UsableVolumeML historian observation for AST-CRR-017 minus the 12.00 ML accrued allocation commitment; observation time, process quality, unit, asset, and planning window must remain explicit.

Native bindings:

- `a-corporate.a-data/PostgreSQL:public.allocation_ledger`
- `a-engineering.a-historian/Grafana:arwc-history`
- `a-engineering.a-historian/PostgreSQL:public.process_history:usable_volume_ml`

The image-owned W29 allocation export and reconciliation method explain the work, but do not replace the corporate PostgreSQL records or native process history. The current planning rows, historian timestamps, and process quality remain authoritative; no private service-loader route duplicates them.

### Vulnerability and intended solution

This is a bounded operating observation and interpretation task, not a privilege flaw. The participant must use the installed products to join that commitment to a fresh good-quality UsableVolumeML observation and calculate the current uncommitted margin. A copied value, inferred default, or synthetic API response does not establish the result.

The required result is: Report the source time, process quality, unit, and value of a fresh `UsableVolumeML` sample, then calculate `uncommitted margin = measured usable volume - committed allocation`. State whether that current margin is positive, zero, or negative. A historical meter export or paper report is not a substitute for the current native observation.

### Evidence and completion

Completion compares the participant's observation with fresh, read-only product records. Product evidence is supplied by the range, never by the participant, and the completion path cannot write a tag, issue a command, change controller state, or create its own product evidence.

Evidence sources are `features.a-corporate.a-data`, `features.a-engineering.a-historian`. A wrong planning window, record revision, asset, source time, process quality, unit, signed calculation, or time-basis conclusion is rejected.

### Boundaries and persistence

The result remains local evidence unless a downstream workflow explicitly names it. The task is read-only and creates no process mutation to retain or reset. Ordinary historian retention continues independently. Infrastructure recovery and submission adjudication are external to the SDL.

The behavior is limited to the installed Cairn Reach products, the named asset, and the supplied participant material. It provides no alternate challenge service, simulated completion endpoint, or authority not already present in the OT environment.

## Hints

### Hint 1

Use the latest good `usable_volume_ml` sample near your observation time; a dashboard label without time and quality is incomplete.

### Hint 2

The allocation is an obligation in ML. It is not a measured tag and should not be searched for in OpenPLC.

### Hint 3

Keep the sign: measured reserve minus 12.00 ML determines whether the margin is positive or negative.
