## Challenge description—player-facing

Use the Cairn Reach allocation export and corporate planning records to identify the replacement-supply tariff, its effective interval, and the allocation commitment for the same asset and planning window.

## Story purpose

Establishes the dated business obligations that later cards will compare with measured process state.

## Prerequisites and starting position

**Required access or evidence:** [OT_READ](../../capability-routes.json).

**Starting material:** The W29 allocation export in the participant workstation and read access to the corporate planning records.

**Campaign references:** No separately named capability output.

## Completion and downstream use

Identify tariff `TAR-CRR-DP3-R4`, its USD/ML rate and effective interval, plus allocation `ALLOC-2026-DP3-R9` and its 12.00 ML commitment for `AST-CRR-017`. Do not substitute W35's procurement catalog or treat either business record as a live process measurement.

## Difficulty and inspiration

Easy: this is a narrow current-record recovery, but record identity, revision, unit, asset, planning window, and effective dates must agree. The Australian water-accounting standard treats commitments and physical flows as different accounting facts, and AWWA recommends sufficiently frequent meter readings to support accountable volume decisions.

**Research:** [Australian Water Accounting Standard 1](https://www.bom.gov.au/water/standards/documents/ed_awas1_v1.0.pdf), [Bureau of Meteorology allocation accounting guide](https://www.bom.gov.au/water/nwa/document/companion-guide.pdf), [AWWA metering and accountability policy](https://www.awwa.org/policy-statement/metering-and-accountability/).

### Vulnerability and intended solution

This is a bounded operating observation and interpretation task, not a privilege flaw. The participant must use the installed products to identify the effective tariff and accrued allocation commitment from the corporate planning records. A copied value, inferred default, or synthetic API response does not establish the result.

The required result is: Identify tariff `TAR-CRR-DP3-R4`, its USD/ML rate and effective interval, plus allocation `ALLOC-2026-DP3-R9` and its 12.00 ML commitment for `AST-CRR-017`. Do not substitute W35's procurement catalog or treat either business record as a live process measurement.

### Evidence and completion

Completion compares the participant's observation with fresh, read-only product records. Product evidence is supplied by the range, never by the participant, and the completion path cannot write a tag, issue a command, change controller state, or create its own product evidence.

Evidence sources are `features.a-corporate.a-data`, `features.a-engineering.a-engineering`. A wrong planning window, record revision, asset, source time, process quality, unit, signed calculation, or time-basis conclusion is rejected.

### Boundaries and persistence

The result remains local evidence unless a downstream workflow explicitly names it. The task is read-only and creates no process mutation to retain or reset. Ordinary historian retention continues independently. Infrastructure recovery and submission adjudication are external to the SDL.

The behavior is limited to the installed Cairn Reach products, the named asset, and the supplied participant material. It provides no alternate challenge service, simulated completion endpoint, or authority not already present in the OT environment.
