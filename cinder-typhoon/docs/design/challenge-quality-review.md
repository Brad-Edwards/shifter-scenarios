## Brief quality corrections applied

Several objectives named a “specified action” or “protected value” without
giving the reader a concrete goal. They now identify the affected artifact or
business action: the private commissioning concern, duplicate diagnostic
review, inspection attachment, contractor appointment, allocation adjustment
ledger, maintenance preview, historical approval record, and replacement order.
This makes both the challenge and its story consequence assessable before any
technical design is chosen.

The review also tightened distinctions between adjacent scores:

- K02's disclosed review note is evidence for the later job change, not implicit
  permission to edit or execute it. K18's application-registration read is
  distinct from changing the federation policy.

  outcomes. W06 distinguishes an action in the privileged browser from earning
  an independent session.
- W07.1 requires reconciling the service's claimed read-only role with observed
  operation permissions. Merely downloading a profile is insufficient.
- W14.3 requires adapting the retained client to the current interface within
  the earned contractor scope. Endpoint discovery and ordinary login do not
  receive another score.
- K29.3 now requires a targeted release across two supported interfaces while
  preserving the reference customer's behavior. Applying the signing material

  that combined work, not a rating inherited automatically from PipeDream.
- W23.3 and W27.3 explicitly supply the same bounded planning-estimate effect
  through different mechanisms, so W34's OR has a common capability contract.
- W31's diagnostic execution recovers historical approval evidence. It does
  not silently grant the current approval or CONTROL capability.

W09's completion wording also stopped disclosing the specific alias-counting
mistake that the participant is meant to discover. Goals and evidence remain
explicit without turning the brief into a solution.

## Dependency validation

[validate_challenges.py](validate_challenges.py) uses only the Python standard
library. The [complete ledger](challenge-dependencies.json) has 240 scored
nodes, 33 unscored capability nodes, and two supplied roots. The checker reads
the briefs as well: a stale ledger cannot pass simply because it is internally
consistent.

It validates node references, cycles through either kind of node, AND/OR
semantics, minimal prerequisite sets, allocations, metadata, dependency links,
named source references, index agreement, and the reserved blank sections.
Independent campaign assertions protect the intended access and evidence
contracts. These assertions are not generated from the editable dependencies.

The negative suite rejects 27 intentionally broken graphs. It includes missing
nodes, cycles, empty requirements, mandatory training, Hard ordinary entry,
both alternatives accidentally required, mapping substituted for current
evidence, live work without authority, discovery substituted for access, an
optional elite or AI gate added to the main route, and the ending rebound to
preparation rather than the actual result. The two new cases reject W22.4 or
W24.4 becoming available without the preceding challenge that recovers its
analysis artifacts. Parser cases exercise nested AND/OR
grouping. Mutation tests bypass document-agreement checking so they must fail
on graph meaning, not merely because a brief was left unchanged.

## Overall judgment at brief level

The strongest organizing idea is the recurring maintenance and commissioning
story. Supplier delivery, inconsistent business records, engineering evidence,
current process interpretation, and financial consequences refer to the same
underlying situation. Read access, understanding, and control are separate
achievements. That gives the main joins a narrative reason beyond collecting
keys. Corporate procurement and reporting outcomes also let players finish
meaningful episodes without completing the reservoir objective.

Breadth continues after each transition. The initial foothold exposes eight
campaign challenges across four operations; a shortest ordinary corporate
arrival exposes 43 next challenges across 28 operations, including unfinished
supplier work. That supports individual choice but makes the small lead set
in the [player-experience contract](player-experience.md) important. The graph
does not justify presenting dozens of equal-looking cards at once.

The review found a recurring risk in dividing a difficult source challenge into
milestones whose final step is only the consequence of an earlier solve. W22.3
and W24.3 now receive those complete recovery payoffs; their successors have
different artifacts to analyze and capabilities to earn. A source link still
supports lineage rather than automatically assigning a score or tier. The
portfolio has credible specialist depth in its stated problems, but these
documents alone do not establish a measured nation-state-operator ceiling.

The dependency contracts and the two editorial corrections are now complete
at brief level. No topology, solution sequence, technical design, or hint
content is introduced by these revisions.
