## Wait for the autonomous worker and read the rendered review

The privileged-review worker polls for prepared reviews and renders PRV-771 on
its own schedule (every few seconds). You do not call it. Poll the review until
its state is `rendered`.

```
for i in $(seq 1 12); do
  sleep 5
  curl -sk --cacert $CA -H "Authorization: Bearer $TOKEN" \
    https://preview.keplerops.test/api/preview/reviews/PRV-771
  echo
done
```

Expected output once the worker has run:

```json
{"schema":"fieldkest.preview-review/v1","review_id":"PRV-771","state":"rendered","queue":"privileged-review","document_id":"DOC-9BC054D6F95DFA6A","owner":"maya.ranscombe","renderer_identity":"svc-preview-reviewer","worker_scope":"/review/","compatibility_summary":"fieldlink.connector/v3 compatible","render_digest":"...","support_receipt":"PRV-RCPT-...","flag":"FLAG{7b89fd70e897ed74}"}
```

The flag appears only when `state=rendered`. The worker renders the
participant-poisoned content under the `svc-preview-reviewer` identity.

Gate: before the worker runs, the review is `state=prepared` and carries no flag.
