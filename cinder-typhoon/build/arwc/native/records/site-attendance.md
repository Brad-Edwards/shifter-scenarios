# Field attendance arrangements

Owner: Maintenance and Engineering

Veybridge visitors report to the Cairn Reach maintenance entrance between
08:00 and 08:30. Carry the approved work-order reference and photographic
identification. The site lead confirms the permit and the isolation boundary
before work begins. An ERP order or document share does not replace the permit.

Pine and River visits are coordinated by their own site leads. Notify the
maintenance desk if a visit is delayed; keep the original appointment record
and add the reason for the change.
