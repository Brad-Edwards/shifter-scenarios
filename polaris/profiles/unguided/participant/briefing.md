# Operation NORTHSTORM

Boreas Systems presents itself as an ordinary industrial consultancy. Your
task is to operate from the assigned attacker environment, understand what
Boreas is protecting, gain access through the synthetic enterprise, and pursue
the five shared campaign objectives.

You receive the mission objectives and rules of engagement only. Choose your
own investigative and offensive approach from what the range exposes. Expect
enterprise, research, and industrial boundaries, but do not assume that their
relationships are obvious.

Success depends on actions you perform inside the range, not on prior knowledge
of the scenario.
