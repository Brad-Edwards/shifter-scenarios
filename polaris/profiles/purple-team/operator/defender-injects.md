# Purple-team defender injects

These operator-only prompts accompany the same offensive scenario. They do not
replace the participant role or introduce a defensive path.

- When `target-picture-evidence` begins accumulating, ask defenders what public
  discovery was observable and what was not.
- When `front-office-evidence` is active, compare identity, authentication, and
  service observations without disclosing participant material.
- Treat `leviathan-evidence` and `blackout-evidence` as separate observation
  discussions until the canonical campaign joins them.
- During `bunker-evidence`, focus on how participant-originated activity can be
  distinguished from provisioning, reset, or operator actions.

Use only signals the current range actually emits. Do not invent alert names,
SIEM queries, or detection coverage.
