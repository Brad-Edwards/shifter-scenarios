# Rules of engagement

- Act only inside the assigned Polaris range and through the provided
  participant surface.
- Treat every organization, identity, service, and data item as synthetic
  training content.
- Do not target public systems, neighboring ranges, platform administration,
  or infrastructure management surfaces.
- Perform the offensive actions yourself; an observed final condition is not a
  substitute for participant execution.
- Record what you attempted and what you observed so the run can be reviewed
  after the exercise.
- Stop and notify the facilitator if the range boundary is unclear or a target
  appears to leave the isolated environment.
