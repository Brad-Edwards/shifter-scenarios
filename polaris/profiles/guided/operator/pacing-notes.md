# Guided facilitator pacing

Use the participant prompts as method coaching, not as a route reveal. Keep the
canonical dependency graph in `oracle/polaris-oracle.yaml` private.

- Begin with the target-picture outcome and ask participants to distinguish
  observations from assumptions.
- For the front-office phase, discuss trust relationships without naming the
  credential or service that advances the path.
- Let the lab and industrial branches develop independently after domain
  control. Do not disclose their join order.
- Use the self-checks to ask for participant-originated actions. Never confirm
  an objective from a screenshot of final state or an operator console.
- If a team stalls, choose a general method prompt first. Use existing CTFd
  hints only through the operator-owned challenge system.

The range remains `draft`; these notes do not claim the planned `aws_event`
binding has passed a live rehearsal.
