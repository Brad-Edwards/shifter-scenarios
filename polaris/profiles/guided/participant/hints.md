# General method prompts

Use these prompts when progress stalls:

- What information is already visible from the assigned attacker surface?
- Which observation is confirmed, and which part is still an assumption?
- What ordinary enterprise relationship could connect the facts you have?
- Have you examined both human-readable content and machine-visible metadata?
- Can the access you already hold reveal another in-world trust relationship?
- Are you testing an action from the participant surface, or only looking at a
  desired final condition?
- What can you safely revisit with a different tool or protocol?

These prompts deliberately avoid target names, action order, solution
material, and completion internals.
