# Guided operating plan

Use a repeatable offensive workflow throughout the exercise:

- inventory what the assigned attacker surface can observe before acting;
- form a small hypothesis from in-world information and test it directly;
- keep notes that separate confirmed observations from assumptions;
- preserve useful access while you explore a newly reached environment;
- revisit earlier observations when a branch stalls;
- verify each campaign objective through a participant action you can explain.

The five shared objectives are the destination. This plan teaches working
method, not the scenario route.
