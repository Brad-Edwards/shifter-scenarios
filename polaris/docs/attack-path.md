# Polaris attack-path boundary

This is an operator/oracle-only overview, not a participant walkthrough or a
source of answers. Polaris is an offensive campaign that starts from the
declared attacker surface, crosses the Boreas enterprise and research layers,
and reaches the NORTHSTORM operational objectives through the real components
declared by a future full runtime profile.

The authoritative executable and human path sources remain the existing build,
SDL, tests, and walkthrough material. They are not yet a canonical reference
triangle, and this document therefore makes no ordered hidden-path, flag,
credential, proof-predicate, or golden-proof claim.

The SDL's campaign outcomes are range-state assertions: target picture,
front-office compromise, Leviathan extraction, SCADA blackout with splice
access, and autonomous-platform seizure. They are deliberately independent of
the CTFd board's challenge records.
